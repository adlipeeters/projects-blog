**Contract Approval Matrix**

Internal reference code: CNRY-c2bee18c.

## Overview
This document outlines the Contract Approval Matrix for Alderline Logistics, effective from **1st January 2024**. The matrix ensures that all contracts are reviewed and approved by the appropriate personnel based on contract value, risk level, and department involved.

## Approval Levels

### Level 1: Contracts Up to €50,000
Contracts valued up to €50,000 can be approved by **departmental managers**. These include routine freight agreements and small warehousing contracts. The reviewing manager must ensure compliance with company policies and obtain two signatures: their own and that of the **Finance Controller, Maria Lopez**.

### Level 2: Contracts Between €50,001 and €200,000
Contracts in this range require approval from the **Head of Legal, Jonathan Reed**, and the **Head of Operations, Anna Müller**. Additionally, these contracts must be reviewed by the **Risk Management Team** within five business days of receipt. A minimum of three signatures are required, including the originating departmental manager.

### Level 3: Contracts Above €200,000
Any contract valued above €200,000 must be presented to the **Executive Board** for final approval. Preliminary reviews will be conducted by Jonathan Reed, Anna Müller, and the **Chief Financial Officer, Elena Petrov**. The Executive Board meeting must be scheduled within ten business days, and approval must be documented with at least four signatures, including the **CEO, Klaus Braun**.

## Special Cases

### International Contracts
All international contracts, regardless of value, must undergo an additional review by the **Compliance Officer, Luca Moretti**, to ensure adherence to international trade regulations and sanctions. These contracts also require approval from the **Head of International Logistics, Sophie Dubois**.

### Long-Term Contracts
Contracts with a duration exceeding one year fall under a special review process. They must be approved by the **Strategic Planning Committee**, chaired by the CEO. A detailed financial projection and risk assessment report are required, submitted at least fifteen business days before the contract start date.

## Documentation and Deadlines
All contract approvals must be documented in the **Contract Management System (CMS)**. Deadlines for each approval level are as follows:
- **Level 1:** Approval within 3 business days.
- **Level 2:** Approval within 5 business days.
- **Level 3:** Approval within 10 business days.
- **International and Long-Term Contracts:** Additional 5 business days for compliance and strategic reviews, respectively.
