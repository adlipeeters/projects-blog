## Legal Compliance Review
Anita Rossi presented the quarterly legal compliance report. Key points discussed included:

1. **EU Freight Regulation Update:** New regulations effective from 2025-04-01 require all freight vehicles to be equipped with GPS tracking devices. Implementation costs are estimated at €2.5 million. The logistics team has proposed a phased rollout starting Q2 2025.

2. **Warehousing Safety Standards:** Recent audits identified non-compliance in three warehouses located in Düsseldorf, Antwerp, and Lyon. Remediation plans must be submitted by 2025-04-15, with a budget allocation of €450,000 approved for immediate corrective actions.

3. **Contract Renewals:** Review of upcoming contract renewals with key clients: 
    - **Dahlgren & Co. (Automotive Parts Supplier):** Contract expires 2025-06-30. Recommended renewal terms include a 15% increase in freight rates and a 10% volume commitment over two years.
    - **Vogel Fashion Retail:** Contract expires 2025-09-30. Proposed extension for three years with variable rate clauses tied to fuel price indices.

## Insurance Review
Henrik Sørensen reported on the annual insurance review. The current insurer, Nordica Insurance Group, has offered a 7% discount on cargo insurance premiums if the company maintains a claim-free record for another year. Additionally, a new cyber insurance policy has been proposed to cover potential data breaches, with an annual cost of €120,000 and coverage limit of €10 million.
