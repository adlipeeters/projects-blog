### Inspection Overview

Internal reference code: CNRY-1f4a45a3.

On March 15, 2024, Alderline Logistics underwent a routine regulatory inspection conducted by the European Road Transport Authority (ERTA). The inspection focused on our compliance with EU Regulation 561/2006, which governs driving time, rest periods, and recording of driving activity.

### Key Findings

The inspection revealed several areas requiring immediate attention:

1. **Driving Time Violations**: Between January 1, 2024, and March 15, 2024, 12 instances were recorded where drivers exceeded the allowable weekly driving time of 60 hours. The most significant violation occurred on February 12, 2024, when Driver Marcus Huber logged 67 hours of driving within a single week.

2. **Rest Period Non-Compliance**: Six drivers were found to have taken rest periods shorter than the mandated minimum of 45 minutes after every 4.5 hours of driving. Notably, Driver Anna Schmidt’s logbook showed only 30-minute rest periods on three separate occasions in February.

3. **Tachograph Irregularities**: Three vehicles (license plates DE-AB123, DE-BC456, and DE-CD789) had tachograph data discrepancies. These included missing timestamps and incomplete daily records.

### Corrective Actions

To address these issues, the following corrective actions have been implemented:

1. **Training Program**: A mandatory compliance training session will be held on April 5, 2024, for all drivers and fleet managers. The training will cover EU Regulation 561/2006 requirements and proper tachograph usage.

2. **Revised Logging Procedures**: Effective immediately, all drivers must use the updated digital logging system, which automatically flags potential violations. A trial period will run from April 1, 2024, to April 30, 2024, to ensure smooth implementation.

3. **Audit and Monitoring**: A bi-weekly audit of driving logs and tachograph data will be conducted by the Compliance Team led by Laura Müller. The first audit will take place on April 15, 2024.

### Timeline for Full Compliance

We aim to achieve full compliance within the next three months. By June 15, 2024, all violations should be resolved, and all drivers should be fully compliant with EU Regulation 561/2006. Regular progress reports will be submitted to ERTA on May 1, 2024, and June 1, 2024.
