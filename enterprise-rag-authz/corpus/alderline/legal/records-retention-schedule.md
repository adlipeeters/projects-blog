## Introduction

This document outlines the retention periods for various categories of records held by Alderline Logistics. Adhering to these schedules ensures compliance with European and national regulations, optimizes storage costs, and facilitates efficient record management.

## Retention Periods

### Financial Records

- **Invoices and Receipts:** Retain for **7 years** from the date of issuance, in accordance with EU VAT directives and German commercial law (§§ 257, 262 HGB).
- **Tax Records:** Maintain for **10 years** to satisfy statutory audit requirements under the German Tax Code (AO).
- **Bank Statements:** Keep for **5 years** post-finalization of related financial periods.

### Employment Records

- **Employee Contracts:** Store for **5 years** after the employee's departure, per the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG).
- **Payroll Records:** Retain for **4 years** following the end of the employment relationship.
- **Training and Development Records:** Keep for **2 years** after an employee completes a program, to support potential future training audits.

### Operational Records

- **Delivery Logs:** Retain for **2 years** from the date of delivery to support customer service inquiries and potential dispute resolutions.
- **Vehicle Maintenance Records:** Maintain for **5 years** or the lifespan of the vehicle, whichever is longer, to comply with EU vehicle safety regulations.
- **Warehouse Inventory Reports:** Store for **3 years** to aid in annual stock audits and loss prevention analyses.

### Legal and Compliance Records

- **Litigation Documents:** Retain **until the conclusion of all related legal proceedings**, plus an additional **2 years** for potential appeals.
- **Compliance Audit Reports:** Keep for **3 years** following the audit date to demonstrate adherence to EU and national compliance standards.
- **Insurance Policies:** Maintain for the duration of the policy plus **2 additional years**.

## Disposal Procedures

All records must be disposed of securely, following the company’s Data Destruction Policy. Digital records should be permanently deleted using certified data erasure software. Physical documents must be shredded using cross-cut shredders and disposed of through licensed waste disposal services.
