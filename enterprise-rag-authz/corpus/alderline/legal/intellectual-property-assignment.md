## Introduction

This document outlines the Intellectual Property (IP) Assignment policy for Alderline Logistics, effective from 1st October 2024. It is designed to ensure that all intellectual property created by employees during their tenure at Alderline Logistics is assigned to the company, thereby protecting our collective assets and innovations.

## Scope and Applicability

This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and contract employees of Alderline Logistics across all departments and locations, including but not limited to our headquarters in Amsterdam, our warehouses in Warsaw and Lyon, and our regional offices in Berlin and Madrid. It covers any IP created, whether alone or jointly, during the course of employment, or that utilizes company resources.

## Types of Intellectual Property Covered

The following types of IP are covered under this assignment:

1. **Patents and Inventions:** Any novel inventions, processes, or improvements developed during employment. For example, the "SmartLoad" system developed by the Technology Team in Q2 2024.
2. **Copyrights:** All literary, musical, and artistic works, including software code, marketing materials, and internal training manuals. This includes the "Alderline Logistics Operational Handbook," updated in June 2024.
3. **Trademarks and Service Marks:** Any distinctive signs, designs, or names used in the course of business. Examples include our company logo and the "Alderline Express" service mark.
4. **Trade Secrets:** Confidential business information, such as customer databases, proprietary algorithms, and internal processes. An example is our route optimization algorithm, "RouteMaster 2.0," implemented in January 2024.

## Employee Responsibilities and Acknowledgements

All employees are required to sign an IP Assignment Agreement upon their start date. This agreement must be signed electronically via our HR portal by **15th October 2024**. Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action. Additionally, employees must promptly disclose any potential IP to their immediate supervisor and the Legal Department. Disclosure must be made in writing and submitted to Laura M. Jensen, Legal Counsel, via email (laura.jensen@alderlinelogistics.com) within **10 working days** of conception.

## Conclusion

Adherence to this IP Assignment policy is crucial for maintaining the competitive edge and protecting the collective intellectual assets of Alderline Logistics. By assigning all relevant IP to the company, we ensure that our innovations and proprietary information remain secure and can be leveraged to drive growth and efficiency across all our operations.
