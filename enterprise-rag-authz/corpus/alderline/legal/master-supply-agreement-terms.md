**Master Supply Agreement Terms**

Internal reference code: CNRY-92288c96.

## Agreement Overview
This Master Supply Agreement (MSA) is effective from **January 1, 2025**, and outlines the terms governing the provision of road freight and warehousing services by **Alderline Logistics** to **Clients**. The agreement ensures consistent, high-quality service delivery while protecting both parties' interests.

## Payment Terms
Invoices will be issued bi-weekly and are due within **30 days** of receipt. Late payments incur a penalty of **2% per month** on the outstanding balance. Payment methods accepted include bank transfer and credit card. A **2% discount** is available for payments made within **10 days** of the invoice date.

## Service Levels and Penalties
Alderline Logistics guarantees a **95% on-time delivery rate** for all road freight services. Failure to meet this target will result in a credit note of **5% of the monthly invoice value** for each percentage point below the agreed service level. Warehousing services include **24/7 security monitoring** and **weekly inventory audits**, with penalties applied for any breaches of these standards.

## Termination Clauses
Either party may terminate the MSA with **90 days** written notice. Immediate termination is permitted if either party breaches material terms and fails to cure the breach within **30 days** of receiving written notice. Upon termination, Alderline Logistics will return all client property and provide a **final settlement statement** within **30 days**.
