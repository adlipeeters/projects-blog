## Project Overview

This Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) covers the implementation of the new **Transport Management System (TMS) v3.2** at Alderline Logistics, scheduled for rollout on **15 October 2024**. The system will handle approximately **45,000 daily transactions** across our European road freight and warehousing operations in **12 countries**.

## Data Processing Activities

The TMS will process the following categories of personal data:

1. **Employee Information**: Names, contact details, employment histories, and salary data of **280 staff members**.
2. **Customer Data**: Names, addresses, delivery instructions, and invoice details of **8,500 business clients** and **120,000 private customers**.
3. **Driver Data**: Driving licenses, vehicle assignments, and GPS tracking data for **650 drivers**.
4. **Supplier Information**: Contact details and contract terms for **230 suppliers**.

All data will be stored in an on-premise server located at our **Brussels headquarters** and will be accessible to **authorized personnel only** through role-based access controls.

## Risk Assessment

### Data Security Risks

- **Unauthorized Access**: Risk level **medium**. Mitigation: Multi-factor authentication and encrypted data storage.
- **Data Breach**: Risk level **high**. Mitigation: Regular penetration testing, incident response plan, and GDPR-compliant breach notification procedure within **72 hours** of discovery.

### Data Retention Risks

- **Excessive Data Retention**: Risk level **low**. Mitigation: Implementation of a **data retention policy** that automatically deletes inactive customer records after **5 years** and employee records upon termination plus **2 years**.

### Data Transfer Risks

- **Cross-Border Data Transfers**: Risk level **medium**. Mitigation: Use of **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** for transfers to non-EU countries and regular audits of third-party processors.

## Implementation Timeline and Responsibilities

- **15 October 2024**: System go-live under the supervision of **Markus Becker, IT Director**.

All departments are required to comply with the new data handling procedures outlined in the **Employee Data Handling Guide**, distributed on **1 October 2024**.
