### Case Overview

Internal reference code: CNRY-842e8e45.

**Case ID:** ET-2024-AL-017
**Claimant:** Maria Silva
**Respondent:** Alderline Logistics Ltd.
**Date of Hearing:** 15th October 2024
**Claim Type:** Unfair Dismissal and Constructive Dismissal

### Background

Maria Silva, a warehouse supervisor employed since 1st January 2020, was dismissed on 20th June 2024. Maria, who is based out of our Lisbon, Portugal warehouse, had a clean disciplinary record until March 2024. On 12th March 2024 Maria was late for work on three consecutive days, her explanation being that the public transport system in Lisbon was on strike, an event well reported in national media outlets at the time and beyond the control of Alderline Logistics. Maria was issued a written warning on 15th March 2024.

### Tribunal Findings

The tribunal, held at the Lisbon Employment Tribunal Centre, concluded on 22nd October 2024. The findings were as follows:

1. **Unfair Dismissal:** The tribunal ruled in favour of the claimant, determining that Alderline Logistics failed to follow the appropriate disciplinary procedure as outlined in the Employee Handbook. Specifically, the company did not conduct a final warning stage before proceeding to dismissal.
   
2. **Constructive Dismissal:** The tribunal also found in favour of the claimant regarding constructive dismissal. It was concluded that the issue of the written warning was unreasonable given the circumstances, and that this contributed to an irretrievable breakdown of the employment relationship.

### Financial and Operational Impact

**Compensation Award:** €45,000

**Costs:** The legal costs incurred by Alderline Logistics amount to approximately €20,000. Additionally, the claimant's legal costs, which are payable by the company under the tribunal's ruling, are estimated at €15,000.

**Reputational Impact:** Internal communications have been issued to all staff reminding them of the disciplinary procedure, and an external PR firm has been engaged to manage any potential media interest. The case has also prompted a review of our HR policies to ensure compliance with employment law in all European countries in which we operate.

**Future Actions:** A mandatory training session for all HR personnel is scheduled for 10th November 2024, focusing on fair disciplinary practices and constructive dismissal laws across the European Union. A review of all current disciplinary cases is also underway to ensure no further breaches occur.
