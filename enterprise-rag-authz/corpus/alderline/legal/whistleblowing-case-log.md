**Whistleblowing Case Log**

Internal reference code: CNRY-fd5527e3.

**Department: Legal**

## Case Overview

**Case ID:** WB-2023-017
**Date Received:** 15 October 2023
**Reporter:** Maria Schmidt, Warehouse Supervisor, Berlin Facility
**Date of Alleged Incident:** 02 October 2023

## Allegations

Maria Schmidt reported witnessing systematic misdeclaration of cargo weights on bills of lading for shipments handled between 01 July 2023 and 30 September 2023. The misdeclaration resulted in underpayment of road tolls and fuel surcharges, leading to an estimated financial loss of €120,000 to Alderline Logistics and potential legal penalties from regulatory bodies.

## Investigation Details

An internal investigation was initiated on 17 October 2023, led by the Legal Department in conjunction with the Internal Audit Team. The investigation focused on the following:

- **Document Review:** Examination of shipping records, bills of lading, and financial statements from the specified period.
- **Interviews:** Conducted with Maria Schmidt, warehouse staff, logistics coordinators, and the relevant accountancy team.
- **Data Analysis:** Cross-referencing declared weights with actual weigh-in data from the company’s scales.

The investigation confirmed that 14 shipments had incorrect weight declarations, with an average under-declaration of 12%. The total value of the affected shipments was €950,000.

## Actions Taken

- **Disciplinary Measures:** The logistics coordinator, Jan Bekker, was suspended pending further action. HR initiated proceedings for possible termination.
  
- **Financial Reconciliation:** The Finance Department calculated the exact loss and began the process of amending the relevant bills of lading and settling the correct toll and fuel charges, estimated at €132,000 including penalties.

- **Policy Review:** The Legal Team revised the company’s weight declaration and auditing procedures to prevent future occurrences. Training sessions for all warehouse and logistics staff are scheduled for November 2023.

- **Whistleblower Protection:** Maria Schmidt was assured of full protection under the company’s Whistleblowing Policy. No retaliation was observed or reported.
