### **Current Liability Caps Overview**

Internal reference code: CNRY-903475f5.

As of Q2 2024, Alderline Logistics has implemented specific liability caps to manage potential risks and financial exposures related to customer contracts. These caps are designed to protect both the company and our clients by setting clear boundaries on financial responsibility in the event of service disruptions, damages, or losses.

The current maximum liability cap for standard road freight contracts is **€100,000 per incident**. For premium contracts, which include additional services such as expedited delivery or specialized handling, the cap is set at **€250,000 per incident**. These figures represent a 30% increase from the previous year to accommodate rising operational costs and market adjustments.

### **Exceptions and Special Cases**

There are specific scenarios where higher liability caps may apply:

1. **High-Value Goods**: For shipments valued above €500,000, the liability cap can be negotiated up to **€1,000,000 per incident**, subject to additional premium payments and comprehensive risk assessment.
2. **Warehousing Services**: In our warehousing facilities, the standard liability cap is **€50,000 per incident**. However, for clients utilizing our secure storage units with enhanced security protocols (e.g., climate-controlled or high-security zones), the cap increases to **€150,000 per incident**.

### **Implementation Timeline and Responsibility**

All new contracts signed on or after **September 1, 2024**, must adhere to these updated liability caps. Existing contracts will gradually transition to the new caps over the next 12 months, with renegotiations initiated by the **Contracts and Compliance Team**.

Responsibility for ensuring compliance falls on the **Legal and Contracts Department**, led by **Eva Müller, Senior Legal Counsel**. Eva and her team will work closely with account managers to update contract templates and provide necessary training.

### **Financial Impact and Risk Management**

Implementing these liability caps is expected to reduce potential financial exposure by approximately **15% annually**. This adjustment aligns with industry standards and our risk management strategy, ensuring that Alderline Logistics remains financially stable while offering fair terms to our customers.

A detailed financial impact analysis, prepared by **Finance Analyst Marco Bianchi**, projects a reduction in potential claim payouts from an average of €3 million annually to approximately €2.55 million. This change will also allow us to allocate more resources towards enhancing service quality and expanding our client base.
