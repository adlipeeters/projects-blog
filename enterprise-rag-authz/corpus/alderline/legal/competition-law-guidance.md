As our operations expand across Europe, it's crucial to adhere to competition law regulations to avoid legal pitfalls and maintain our reputation. This document provides specific guidance to ensure our practices remain compliant.

## Key Regulations and Thresholds

European competition law, primarily governed by the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (TFEU) Articles 101 and 102, prohibits anti-competitive agreements and abuse of dominant positions. For Alderline Logistics, the most relevant thresholds include:

- **Market Share Thresholds**: Agreements between competitors are generally safe if each party has a market share below 10% in the relevant market. For our road freight division, this means ensuring no single agreement pushes any partner above this limit in any European country where we operate.
  
- **Significant Market Power**: If a company holds a market share of 40% or more in a particular market, it is considered to have significant market power. Currently, our market share in Benelux road freight stands at 12%, but we must monitor any growth closely.

## Common Pitfalls and Best Practices

### Pricing and Discounts

- **Price Fixing**: Explicitly agreeing with competitors on pricing is illegal. However, we can engage in independent pricing strategies based on market analysis. For example, if we adjust our rates for the German corridor due to fuel cost increases, this must be our independent decision, not coordinated with competitors.

- **Discriminatory Discounts**: Offering disproportionately lower rates to certain customers can be seen as predatory pricing. Ensure that any discounts offered to large clients, like our deal with **EuroTech Solutions** (15% discount effective from March 1, 2024), are justified by cost savings or volume thresholds and are applied consistently.

### Joint Ventures and Collaborations

- **Shared Warehousing Facilities**: Collaborating with other logistics firms to share warehousing costs in **Rotterdam** is permissible as long as the agreement does not result in controlling access to the facility or fixing prices for stored goods. Our current agreement with **Van der Meer Logistics** allows shared use but maintains independent pricing.
