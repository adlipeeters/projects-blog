### Financial Review

Internal reference code: CNRY-62fc2398.

We have completed an initial review of Trans-European Freight Solutions (TEFS) financial statements for the year ending December 31, 2023. TEFS reported a total revenue of €78.4 million, with a net profit of €4.2 million. The company’s debt-to-equity ratio stands at 0.65, which is within acceptable limits. However, we identified discrepancies in their accounts receivable, where €1.1 million remains uncollected beyond 180 days. Their annual operating expenses total €12.8 million, with logistics and warehousing costs accounting for 65% of this figure.

### Contractual Obligations

TEFS has several long-term contracts with key clients that expire between 2025 and 2028. The most significant contract is with MetroChain Retail, worth €18.6 million annually, expiring in March 2026. There are also three contracts with regional manufacturing firms, collectively worth €9.3 million per year, which expire in 2027. It is crucial to assess the renewal terms and potential penalties for early termination. Additionally, TEFS has lease agreements for their three main warehouses in Rotterdam, Munich, and Warsaw, with remaining lease terms of 5, 3, and 4 years respectively.

### Legal Compliance

Our legal team has reviewed TEFS’s compliance records and found that they are generally in good standing. However, there are two ongoing investigations by the Dutch and German transport authorities regarding alleged breaches of labor laws concerning driver working hours and rest periods. If these allegations are proven, TEFS could face fines of up to €500,000 in each country. Moreover, there is a pending litigation with a former employee, Jan Bekker, over alleged misuse of company assets, estimated at €750,000.

### Intellectual Property and IT Systems

TEFS’s intellectual property portfolio consists mainly of trademarks and software licenses. They hold 12 trademarks related to their brand and service names. Their IT infrastructure relies heavily on a legacy warehouse management system (WMS) that was implemented in 2018. The system requires an upgrade, estimated to cost €2.5 million, to ensure compatibility with modern logistics software. There are no significant patent holdings or proprietary technology that would provide a competitive advantage post-merger.
