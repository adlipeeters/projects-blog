## Overview
This document outlines the exceptional scenarios in which minor deviations from our standard Anti-Bribery Policy may be considered permissible under Alderline Logistics. These exceptions are strictly controlled, time-bound, and require explicit approval from senior management.

## Exception Criteria

1. **Emergency Border Crossings**: When facing unprecedented border delays that threaten the delivery schedule of time-sensitive goods (e.g., perishable medical supplies), a one-time payment of up to **€150** may be authorized to expedite processing. Such approvals must be documented and countersigned by the **Head of Legal, Marcus Weber**, and the **Regional Operations Director**.

2. **Force Majeure Situations**: In cases of natural disasters blocking roads (e.g., severe flooding in the Alps affecting Route A-81), a maximum gratuity of **€300** may be offered to local authorities for emergency guidance. This requires prior notification to the **Compliance Office** within **24 hours** of the incident.

3. **Limited Cultural Practices**: In specific regions identified in our **Risk Assessment Matrix** (currently: Turkey, Morocco, and Bulgaria), a nominal gift valued at **under €50** may be exchanged during localized business initiation meetings. Such gifts must be non-cash, pre-approved gift articles from our vendor list, and reported within **48 hours**.

## Approval Workflow
All exception requests must follow this process:

1. Submit a **Request Form AL-EBF** to the Compliance Office.
2. Await **48-hour review** by the Ethics Committee.
3. Receive written confirmation from **Marcus Weber**.
4. Execute the action within **5 business days** of approval.

Failure to adhere to this protocol will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## Monitoring and Audits
A quarterly audit conducted by our **Internal Audit team** will review all exception cases from the previous quarter. Any irregularities will be reported to the **Board of Directors** for further action.
