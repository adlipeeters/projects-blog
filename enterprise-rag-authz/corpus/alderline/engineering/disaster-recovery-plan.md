### **Data Backup Procedures**

Internal reference code: CNRY-8f451f01.

Every Wednesday at 2:00 AM CET, all critical data from our warehouse management system (WMS), transportation management system (TMS), and customer relationship management (CRM) databases are automatically backed up to an off-site secure server located in Rotterdam. These backups are encrypted using AES-256 and are retained for a minimum of 6 months. Monthly, on the second Tuesday, a verification process is conducted to ensure data integrity and recovery feasibility.

### **Hardware Failure Response**

In the event of a hardware failure at any of our three main hubs (North Hub in Hamburg, Central Hub in Paris, and South Hub in Milan), the following steps will be taken:

1. Immediate switch to redundant systems within 15 minutes.
2. Notification of the engineering team via our emergency communication platform.
3. If the failure persists beyond 2 hours, activate the disaster recovery server located in Zurich.

Our contingency plan ensures that no shipment delays exceed 4 hours, and full operational recovery is expected within 24 hours.

### **Natural Disaster Protocol**

Given our geographical spread, we have specific protocols for natural disasters:

- **Floods**: All electronic equipment is elevated to at least 1.5 meters above ground level. Water sensors are installed to trigger automatic shutdowns and alerts.
  
- **Storms**: Each hub has reinforced storage areas designed to withstand winds up to 150 km/h. Critical data centers are equipped with industrial-grade generators capable of 48 hours of continuous operation.

### **Communication and Reporting**

During any disaster scenario, the designated Disaster Recovery Coordinator, currently Marcus Weber, will maintain hourly updates to senior management and all staff via the emergency communication channel. Post-incident, a detailed report must be submitted within 72 hours, outlining the incident, response measures taken, and recommendations for future improvements. The last drill simulating a widespread power outage was conducted on March 15, 2023, and the next is scheduled for November 10, 2023.
