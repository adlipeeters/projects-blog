**Production Access Runbook**

Internal reference code: CNRY-4d09d31a.

**Department: Engineering**

## Purpose
This runbook outlines the standardized procedure for granting production system access to authorized engineering personnel at Alderline Logistics. Effective from **January 15, 2024**, this protocol ensures secure and efficient access management for our critical road freight and warehousing systems, including the **TLM-2024 Transit Logistics Management** platform and **WMS-5.1 Warehouse Management System**.

## Access Request Process

1. **Submission**: Engineering staff must submit an **Access Request Form (ARF)** via the **IntraLink** portal. The ARF must include:
   - Full name and employee ID
   - Specific system(s) requiring access (e.g., TLM-2024, WMS-5.1)
   - Reason for access with business justification
   - Expected duration of access (e.g., **30 days**, **90 days**, or **Permanent**)
   - Supervisor approval signature

2. **Review**: The **Access Review Committee (ARC)**, comprising **Janek Kowalski (Lead Systems Engineer)**, **Lina Müller (Security Officer)**, and **Marc Bauer (Engineering Manager)**, will review the ARF within **2 business days**.

3. **Approval & Notification**: Upon approval, the **Infrastructure Team** will process the request. The requester will receive a notification via **IntraLink** and email, including temporary credentials if required.

## Access Duration & Renewal

- **Temporary Access**: Grants for durations of **30 days** or **90 days** must be renewed before expiration. Renewal requests should be submitted **at least 5 business days** prior to expiry.
  
- **Permanent Access**: Requires annual re-validation. The **Security Office** will send a reminder **30 days** before the validation date.

## Security Protocols

All personnel with production access must adhere to the following:

- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** is mandatory for all production system logins.
- **Session Logging**: All activities will be logged and monitored. Any suspicious activity will be reported to **Lina Müller**.
- **Data Handling**: Sensitive data must be encrypted and stored according to **Alderline’s Data Protection Policy v3.2**.

Access privileges will be revoked immediately upon role change, resignation, or violation of security protocols.
