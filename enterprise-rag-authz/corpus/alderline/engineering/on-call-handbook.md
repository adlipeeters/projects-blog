## Emergency Contact Protocol
When responding to an emergency, follow these steps:

1. **Assess the Situation:** Determine the severity and impact of the issue. For critical systems (e.g., warehouse automation down > 4 hours), escalate immediately.
2. **Notify the Team:** Call the on-call engineer and leave a detailed voicemail if unreachable within 5 minutes. Send a follow-up email with a summary of the issue, including any error codes or logs.
3. **Document Everything:** Log the incident in the Engineering Incident Management System (EIMS) with tags such as "Urgent," "Automation," or "Fleet Maintenance."
4. **Implement Temporary Fixes:** If applicable, apply documented workarounds to minimise disruption. For example, if the automated palletiser at Warehouse B is down, switch to manual stacking procedure SMP-B-101.
