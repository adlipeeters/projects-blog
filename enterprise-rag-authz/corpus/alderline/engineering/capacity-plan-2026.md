**Capacity Plan 2026**

Internal reference code: CNRY-cd9298aa.

## Current Capacity Overview
As of Q1 2026, Alderline Logistics operates a fleet of 450 road freight vehicles and manages 12 warehousing facilities across Europe. Our current annual cargo handling capacity stands at approximately 2.8 million metric tons, with an average truck utilization rate of 82%. The fleet comprises 350 standard semi-trailer trucks, 80 refrigerated units, and 20 heavy-haul vehicles. Our warehouses collectively offer 550,000 square meters of storage space, with an average occupancy rate of 75%.

## Expansion Targets for 2026
For 2026, we aim to increase our road freight capacity by 15% and warehouse capacity by 20%. This expansion will be achieved through the following measures:

1. **Fleet Expansion**: We plan to acquire 75 new vehicles, including 50 standard semi-trailer trucks, 20 refrigerated units, and 5 heavy-haul vehicles. These acquisitions are scheduled to be completed by Q3 2026, with a total investment of €120 million.

2. **Warehouse Expansion**: Three new warehousing facilities will be opened in key logistics hubs: two in Germany (Frankfurt and Munich) and one in Poland (Warsaw). Each new facility will have a capacity of 85,000 square meters, bringing the total warehouse capacity to 725,000 square meters. The construction budget for these facilities is set at €90 million, with completion expected by Q4 2026.

3. **Technology Upgrades**: To support the increased capacity, we will invest €25 million in upgrading our fleet management and warehouse management systems. This includes the implementation of IoT-based tracking systems and automated storage solutions.

## Financial Projections
The total investment for the 2026 capacity expansion is projected at €235 million. We anticipate that these investments will be recouped within three years through increased revenue. The projected additional revenue from the expanded capacity is estimated at €180 million annually. We will finance this expansion through a combination of bank loans (60%), retained earnings (30%), and a capital increase (10%).

## Timeline and Milestones
- **Q1 2026**: Finalize acquisition plans and secure financing.
- **Q2 2026**: Begin procurement of new vehicles and construction of warehouses.
- **Q3 2026**: Deliver and integrate new vehicles into the fleet.
- **Q4 2026**: Complete construction of new warehouses and implement technology upgrades.

This strategic expansion is expected to position Alderline Logistics as a leading player in European road freight and warehousing by the end of 2026.
