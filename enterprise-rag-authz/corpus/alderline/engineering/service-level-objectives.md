## **Engineering Service Level Objectives (SLOs) for 2024**

Internal reference code: CNRY-27f32d82.

### **Vehicle Maintenance Uptime

Our primary SLO for vehicle maintenance uptime is to ensure that **95% of the fleet is operational at any given time**. This target was established following extensive analysis from Q4 2023, where we observed an average fleet availability of 92%. To meet this goal, the Maintenance Team, led by **Erik Larsen**, will implement a bi-weekly preventive maintenance schedule starting **January 15, 2024**. Additionally, we aim to reduce average repair time from **3.5 hours** to **2.5 hours** by investing in new diagnostic equipment and training technicians in advanced troubleshooting techniques.

### **Warehouse Equipment Reliability

For warehouse operations, our SLO is to maintain **98% reliability of material handling equipment** (forklifts, pallet jacks, and order pickers). Last year, we experienced several downtime incidents due to battery failures and hydraulic leaks. To address this, we have budgeted **€250,000** for the replacement of older units and the introduction of a condition-based maintenance program. This program, developed in collaboration with **Siemens Industrial Solutions**, will utilize IoT sensors to predict and prevent failures before they impact operations.

### **Infrastructure Project Completion

We have committed to completing the **automated sorting hub upgrade** in **Leipzig** by **June 30, 2024**. This project is critical to improving our sortation efficiency from the current **85% accuracy** to **95%**. The Engineering Team, under the supervision of **Dr. Ana Petrović**, will work in two shifts to ensure that all phases—electrical, mechanical, and software integration—are finalized within the deadline. A contingency budget of **€100,000** has been allocated for any unforeseen challenges.

### **Safety Incident Reduction

Lastly, our safety SLO is to reduce **reportable incidents** by **30%** compared to 2023’s total of **45 incidents**. We have introduced mandatory quarterly safety drills for all engineering staff and are rolling out a new incident reporting app developed by **SafetyTech Innovations** on **March 1, 2024**. This app will streamline reporting and analysis, allowing for quicker corrective actions and improved overall workplace safety.

These SLOs are designed to enhance our operational efficiency, reduce downtime, and ensure we remain a reliable partner for our clients in 2024 and beyond.
