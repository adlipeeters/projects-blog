### Project Overview

Internal reference code: CNRY-3704fe6b.

We are initiating the migration of our legacy fleet management system, currently running on an aging Oracle 11g database, to a modern cloud-based platform, Microsoft Azure. The project, codenamed **Project Phoenix**, will span 9 months, starting on **March 1, 2024**, and concluding by **November 30, 2024**. The primary goals are to enhance system scalability, improve real-time data analytics capabilities, and reduce operational costs by approximately **15%**.

### Migration Phases

The migration will be executed in three distinct phases to minimize disruption:

1. **Phase 1: Assessment and Planning (March 1 - April 30, 2024)**
    - Conduct a comprehensive audit of current system functionalities and data structures.
    - Engage with key stakeholders from operations, IT, and finance departments.
    - Develop a detailed migration roadmap and risk mitigation strategies.

2. **Phase 2: Development and Testing (May 1 - August 31, 2024)**
    - Build the new system architecture on Microsoft Azure, including microservices and containerization using Docker and Kubernetes.
    - Migrate data in batches, ensuring data integrity through rigorous validation processes.
    - Perform extensive testing, including unit tests, integration tests, and user acceptance testing (UAT).

3. **Phase 3: Deployment and Training (September 1 - November 30, 2024)**
    - Gradually roll out the new system to production environments, starting with a pilot group of 20 vehicles.
    - Provide comprehensive training sessions for all users, with at least **3 training sessions per week**.
    - Establish a dedicated support team to address any issues during the initial rollout period.

### Resource Allocation

The project will be led by **Samantha Green**, Senior IT Project Manager, with a core team of 8 engineers and 4 data analysts. A budget of **€1.2 million** has been allocated, broken down as follows:

- **Software and Licensing**: €400,000
- **Hardware and Infrastructure**: €300,000
- **Consulting and External Services**: €200,000
- **Training and Documentation**: €150,000
- **Contingency**: €150,000

We aim to complete this migration successfully, ensuring a seamless transition and positioning Alderline Logistics at the forefront of technological innovation in the road freight industry.
