**Intercompany Charges Policy**

Internal reference code: CNRY-b2fdc5db.

As of **1st June 2024**, Alderline Logistics implements a standardized policy for intercompany charges between our road freight and warehousing divisions. This policy ensures transparency, consistency, and compliance with both European and international accounting standards.

## Service Charges for Warehousing Division

The Warehousing Division will invoice the Road Freight Division for storage and handling services. The standard charge is **€35 per pallet per day**, with a minimum charge of **€150 per day**. For bulk storage exceeding 1,000 pallets, a discounted rate of **€30 per pallet per day** will apply. All invoices will be issued monthly, covering the previous month’s services. For example, the invoice for July services will be issued on **15th August 2024**.

## Service Charges for Road Freight Division

The Road Freight Division will invoice the Warehousing Division for transportation services. The charge is based on a fixed rate of **€2.50 per kilometre**. For trips exceeding **500 kilometres**, a reduced rate of **€2.20 per kilometre** will apply. Additionally, there is a handling fee of **€50 per load**. Invoices will be issued bi-weekly, with the next invoice scheduled for **15th July 2024**, covering services from **1st to 14th July 2024**.

## Payment Terms and Reconciliation

All invoices must be paid within **30 days** of the invoice date. Late payments will incur a penalty charge of **5% per month** on the outstanding amount. To ensure accurate reconciliation, all charges must be supported by detailed service reports and GPS logs. Any discrepancies should be reported to the Finance Department within **10 business days** of receiving the invoice.

## Reporting and Compliance

Each division must submit a detailed report to the Finance Department by the **10th of each month**, summarizing all intercompany transactions for the previous month. These reports will be reviewed quarterly by the Internal Audit team to ensure compliance with this policy. Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action.

This policy aims to streamline our intercompany transactions, ensure fair pricing, and maintain our commitment to financial integrity.
