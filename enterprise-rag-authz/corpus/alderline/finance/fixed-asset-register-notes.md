This document provides guidelines for maintaining our Fixed Asset Register, crucial for accurate financial reporting and audit compliance. It covers essential procedures, responsible parties, and key deadlines.

### Register Structure and Updates

The Fixed Asset Register is managed in an Excel-based system named **AlderAssetPro**. It includes the following columns:

- **Asset ID**: Unique identifier (e.g., FA-2023-TRK-001)
- **Asset Type**: Categories include Trucks, Warehouse Equipment, Office Furniture, and IT Equipment
- **Purchase Date**: Recorded in MM/DD/YYYY format
- **Purchase Cost**: Recorded in EUR (e.g., €285,000)
- **Depreciation Method**: Straight-line or Reducing Balance
- **Useful Life**: In years (e.g., 5 years for trucks)
- **Accumulated Depreciation**: Updated monthly
- **Net Book Value**: Calculated as Purchase Cost minus Accumulated Depreciation
- **Location**: Specific site (e.g., Alderline Logistics, Munich Hub)

All updates must be submitted no later than the **5th** of each month for the previous month’s transactions. For example, January’s updates are due on February 5th.

### Key Deadlines and Procedures

- **Monthly Reconciliation**: Completed by the **10th** of each month. Discrepancies must be resolved within **7 days**.
- **Annual Audit Preparation**: Register must be fully up-to-date by **March 1st** annually for internal audit preparation.
- **Asset Disposal**: Disposal forms must be submitted within **2 weeks** of asset removal. Disposed assets must be removed from the register immediately.
