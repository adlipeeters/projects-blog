### **Revenue Recognition Overview**

Internal reference code: CNRY-32f97033.

At Alderline Logistics, our revenue recognition policy adheres to the IFRS 15 standard, ensuring that revenue is recognized when control of goods or services is transferred to the customer. This policy applies to all segments, including our road freight and warehousing services.

### **Criteria for Revenue Recognition**

Revenue from our core services is recognized as follows:

1. **Road Freight Services**: Revenue is recognized upon delivery of goods to the final customer, as this is the point at which control is transferred. For example, a shipment from our warehouse in Antwerp to a client in Cologne will have its revenue recognized on the date the goods are delivered to the client’s facility.

2. **Warehousing Services**: For storage fees, revenue is recognized on a straight-line basis over the contract period. If a client, such as TechNova Ltd., pays €120,000 for an annual storage contract starting on January 1, 2025, we recognize €10,000 monthly.

3. **Value-Added Services**: Revenue from services like custom packaging or labeling is recognized when the service is completed and the client accepts the goods. For instance, if we complete a labeling job for PharmaCare Inc. on March 15, 2025, and they accept the goods on March 20, revenue is recognized on March 20.

### **Contract Modifications and Adjustments**

When there are modifications to existing contracts, we assess whether the modification is a separate performance obligation. If it is, we allocate the transaction price to the new obligation based on its relative stand-alone selling price. For example, if GreenGrocer Ltd. adds a cross-docking service to their existing contract for an additional €15,000 annually, we recognize this revenue as the service is performed, starting from the date the modification is effective.

### **Disclosures and Reporting**

We provide detailed disclosures in our financial statements regarding the nature of our revenue streams, the performance obligations within contracts, and the timing of revenue recognition. This includes a breakdown of revenue by service type and geography. As of Q2 2025, road freight services accounted for 65% of total revenue (€13.5M), warehousing services for 30% (€6.2M), and value-added services for 5% (€1.03M).

Adherence to this policy ensures compliance with accounting standards and provides transparency to our stakeholders.
