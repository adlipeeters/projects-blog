### Current Debt Overview
As of Q2 2024, Alderline Logistics carries a total debt of €18.5 million, comprised of a €12 million term loan with BBVA, due in 2028, and a €6.5 million revolving credit facility with Deutsche Bank, due in 2026. The current interest rates are 5.2% for the term loan and 4.8% for the revolving facility.

Internal reference code: CNRY-dea814b4.

### Refinancing Strategy
Our plan is to refinance €15 million of this debt in two tranches. The first tranche of €10 million will be refinanced through a new 5-year term loan with ING Bank at a fixed interest rate of 4.5%, starting October 1, 2024. The second tranche of €5 million will be a 3-year revolving credit facility with KBC Bank at a variable rate pegged to EURIBOR + 1.5%, expected to close by December 1, 2024.

### Expected Benefits
This refinancing will result in an immediate reduction of our annual interest expense by approximately €650,000. The new ING term loan will replace the BBVA loan, which has a higher interest rate and shorter maturity. Additionally, the KBC revolving facility offers more favorable terms than our current Deutsche Bank facility.

### Implementation Timeline
- **August 15, 2024**: Finalize loan agreements with ING and KBC.
- **September 1, 2024**: Submit necessary documentation to banks.
- **October 1, 2024**: Disburse €10 million from ING and begin repayment of the BBVA loan.
- **December 1, 2024**: Disburse €5 million from KBC and begin repayment of the Deutsche Bank facility.

We anticipate that this refinancing will improve our cash flow position and reduce our overall financial risk, allowing us to focus more effectively on growth initiatives in the European logistics market.
