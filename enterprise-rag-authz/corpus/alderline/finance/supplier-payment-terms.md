### Current Payment Terms Overview

Internal reference code: CNRY-4f498fdb.

As of Q2 2024, Alderline Logistics has standardized our supplier payment terms to improve cash flow management and strengthen relationships with our key vendors. This document outlines the updated terms, applicable from **June 1, 2024**.

#### Standard Payment Terms

For the majority of our suppliers, the standard payment term is **net 30 days** from the date of the invoice receipt. This applies to routine goods and services, including fuel, tires, and routine maintenance.

#### Early Payment Discount

To encourage timely settlement and reduce processing fees, we are introducing an **early payment discount** of **2%** for payments made within **10 days** of the invoice date. This discount is applicable automatically if the payment is processed before the 10-day mark. For example, an invoice of **€10,000** would qualify for a discount of **€200** if paid within the stipulated period.

#### Extended Payment Terms for Key Suppliers

In recognition of our long-standing partnerships, we have agreed to extended payment terms with the following key suppliers:

1. **Mexboro Industrial Supplies**: Net 60 days
2. **EuroTech Solutions**: Net 45 days
3. **GreenFuel Ltd.**: Net 30 days with a 1.5% discount for payments within 15 days

These extended terms are effective from **July 1, 2024**, and will be reviewed annually.

#### Late Payment Penalties

To ensure timely payments and maintain healthy supplier relationships, late payments will incur a penalty of **1.5% per month** on the outstanding amount, compounded monthly. This penalty will be applied after the grace period, which is the standard or agreed-upon term for each supplier.

#### Payment Processing Schedule

All payments will be processed on the **15th and the last day of each month**. In the event that these dates fall on a weekend or public holiday, payments will be processed on the next business day.

We appreciate your cooperation in adhering to these updated payment terms, which are designed to benefit both Alderline Logistics and our valued suppliers.
