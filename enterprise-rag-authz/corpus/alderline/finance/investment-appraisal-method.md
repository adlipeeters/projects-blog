**Investment Appraisal Method**

Internal reference code: CNRY-f105a6a2.

## Overview

This document outlines the standardised investment appraisal method used across Alderline Logistics. Effective from 1st January 2024, this method will be applied to all capital expenditure projects exceeding €50,000. The goal is to ensure consistent, transparent, and financially sound decision-making.

## Methodology

### 1. Net Present Value (NPV)

All projects will undergo an NPV analysis. The discount rate applied will be the company’s weighted average cost of capital (WACC), currently set at 8%. Cash flows will be projected over a five-year period, with adjustments for inflation and risk factors specific to each project.

### 2. Payback Period

In addition to NPV, the payback period will be calculated. This metric will help assess the time required to recover the initial investment. Projects with a payback period exceeding three years will require additional justification.

### 3. Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

The IRR will also be computed to compare the efficiency of different projects. Any project with an IRR below the company’s hurdle rate of 10% will not be approved.

## Implementation

### Responsibility

The Finance Department, led by CFO Anna Müller, will be responsible for conducting these analyses. All project proposals must be submitted to the Finance Team by the end of Q1 each year.

### Timeline

Analysis will be completed within four weeks of proposal submission. The Board of Directors will review all appraisals at the quarterly meeting, with final decisions expected by the end of Q2.

## Financial Thresholds

To ensure alignment with our strategic goals, the following financial thresholds have been set:

- **Minor Projects**: €50,000 - €200,000 – Require NPV > €0 and Payback < 2 years.
- **Major Projects**: €200,000 - €500,000 – Require NPV > €20,000 and Payback < 3 years and IRR > 12%.
- **Strategic Projects**: > €500,000 – Require NPV > €50,000 and Payback < 4 years and IRR > 15%.

These guidelines will help us optimise our capital allocation and drive sustainable growth.
