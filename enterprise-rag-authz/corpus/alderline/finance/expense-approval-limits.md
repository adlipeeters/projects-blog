**Expense Approval Limits**

Internal reference code: CNRY-7f19691e.

As of **June 1, 2024**, Alderline Logistics has updated its expense approval procedures to ensure efficiency, transparency, and proper financial management. These guidelines apply to all employees and departmental heads involved in submitting or approving expenses.

## General Guidelines
All expenses must be supported by original receipts or invoices unless otherwise stated. Expenses should be categorized correctly according to the company’s chart of accounts. Failure to provide adequate documentation may result in the expense being rejected.

## Approval Limits

### **Junior Staff & Non-Management Employees**
Employees in roles such as **Logistics Coordinators**, **Warehouse Staff**, and **Driver Assistants** are authorized to approve expenses up to **€200** without prior authorization. Any expense exceeding **€200** must be approved by their direct supervisor.

### **Department Heads & Mid-Level Managers**
Department Heads, including **Head of Logistics, Marcus Reinhardt**, and **Warehouse Manager, Elena Kovacs**, may approve expenses up to **€500**. For expenses between **€500.01** and **€1,500**, approval from the respective **Department Head** is required. Expenses exceeding **€1,500** will need approval from the **Finance Director, Sophie Lang**. 

### **Finance Team**
Members of the Finance Team, including **Lead Accountant, Liam O’Connell**, can approve any amount but are expected to follow internal audit procedures for transparency. For expenses over **€2,000**, a secondary approval from the **CFO, Henrik Sørensen**, is mandatory.

## Special Cases

### **Travel and Accommodation**
Travel and accommodation expenses up to **€300** per trip can be approved by a department head. For business trips exceeding **€300**, an advanced request form must be submitted and approved by the Finance Director.

### **Equipment and Supplies**
Purchases of office supplies, IT equipment, and vehicle maintenance up to **€400** can be approved by the relevant department head. Any procurement exceeding this limit must be processed through the **Procurement Office** and approved by the CFO.

These updated limits aim to streamline our expense management process and ensure that all expenditures align with company policies and budgetary constraints. Please adhere to these guidelines to avoid delays in reimbursement and maintain financial integrity across the organization.
