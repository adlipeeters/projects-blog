## Q3 2024 Financial Overview

Internal reference code: CNRY-fee75257.

We are pleased to present the Q3 2024 financial results for Alderline Logistics. This report covers the period from July 1, 2024, to September 30, 2024.

### Revenue and Profitability

Our total revenue for Q3 2024 reached **€18.2 million**, marking a **12% increase** compared to Q3 2023. This growth is primarily attributed to the increased volume in our road freight division, which contributed **€13.5 million** of the total revenue, a **15% increase** year-over-year.

Operating expenses amounted to **€15.8 million**, resulting in an operating profit of **€2.4 million**. Our net profit stood at **€1.9 million** after accounting for taxes and interest expenses.

### Cost Management

We have successfully managed to keep our cost of goods sold (COGS) at **€9.6 million**, a **8% decrease** from the previous year. This was achieved through better negotiation with suppliers and improved inventory management in our warehousing facilities.

Employee salaries and benefits totaled **€4.2 million**, representing a **5% increase** due to inflationary pressures and the recent salary adjustments for our warehouse staff in the Dortmund facility.

### Cash Flow and Investments

Our operating cash flow for Q3 2024 was **€2.8 million**. We used **€1.2 million** of this cash for capital expenditures, primarily for the maintenance and upgrade of our fleet in preparation for the upcoming winter season.

We also allocated **€500,000** for the expansion of our warehouse in Warsaw, which is expected to be completed by Q2 2025. Despite these investments, our cash and cash equivalents at the end of Q3 stood at **€4.1 million**, indicating a healthy liquidity position.

### Outlook

We anticipate continued growth in Q4 2024, with projected revenue of **€20 million** and an operating profit of **€2.7 million**. We remain focused on optimizing our operations and expanding our market share in the European road freight and warehousing sectors.
