## **Credit Limit Guidelines**

To maintain financial stability and minimise risk, Alderline Logistics has established the following credit limit framework based on customer categories:

- **Tier 1 Customers (Annual Revenue > €10 million):** Maximum credit limit of €250,000.
- **Tier 2 Customers (Annual Revenue €2 million - €10 million):** Maximum credit limit of €100,000.
- **Tier 3 Customers (Annual Revenue < €2 million):** Maximum credit limit of €50,000.

All credit limits will be reviewed quarterly and adjusted based on the customer's payment history and financial health.

## **Payment Terms and Due Dates**

Standard payment terms for all invoices are **Net 30**, meaning full payment is expected within 30 days of the invoice date. For Tier 1 customers with a proven track record of timely payments, we may offer **Net 45** terms upon approval from the finance department.

Invoices will be sent electronically via our billing system on the date of service completion. A grace period of **3 days** will be allowed before late fees are applied. Late payments will incur a penalty fee of **5% per month** of the outstanding amount, compounded monthly.

## **Credit Monitoring and Collection Process**

A structured approach will be employed to monitor and collect outstanding payments:

1. **Days 0-30:** Invoices issued and sent to customers.
2. **Days 31-45:** First reminder email sent. Account manager to follow up with a phone call.
3. **Days 46-60:** Second reminder email sent. Finance department to escalate the issue to the customer’s accounts payable department.
4. **Days 61-90:** Formal letter before action sent. Legal department to be informed for potential next steps.
5. **Days 91+:** Legal action to be considered, including referral to a collections agency or small claims court.

## **Reporting and Review**

The Credit Control team will submit a weekly report to the finance department, detailing outstanding balances, ageing of receivables, and actions taken on overdue accounts. A monthly review meeting will be held with the management team to assess the effectiveness of the credit control policy and make any necessary adjustments.
