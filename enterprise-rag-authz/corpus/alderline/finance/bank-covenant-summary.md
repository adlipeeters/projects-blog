### Current Covenant Status
As of Q2 2023, Alderline Logistics is compliant with all our primary banking covenants. Our key covenants and current positions are outlined below:

Internal reference code: CNRY-55ac9ae6.

**Debt-to-Equity Ratio**: Our covenant limits this ratio to 2.5:1. As of the end of Q2, our total debt stands at €45 million, and shareholders’ equity is €18 million, resulting in a Debt-to-Equity ratio of 2.5:1, exactly meeting our covenant limit.

**Interest Coverage Ratio**: The covenant requires an Interest Coverage Ratio of at least 3.0. With an EBIT of €7 million and interest expenses of €2.3 million, our current ratio is 3.04, which is above the required threshold.

**Liquidity Ratio**: We must maintain a Liquidity Ratio (current assets/current liabilities) of at least 1.2. Our current assets are €12 million, and current liabilities are €9 million, giving us a ratio of 1.33, comfortably above the covenant requirement.

### Covenant Breaches and Actions
We have had one historical breach in Q4 2022, where the Liquidity Ratio fell to 1.15 due to seasonal fluctuations and a delay in invoice payments. We took the following corrective actions:

1. **Accelerated Invoice Collections**: Implemented tighter credit control policies, reducing average collection time by 5 days.
2. **Short-Term Financing**: Secured a €2 million revolving credit facility with our bank to cover temporary shortfalls.
3. **Cash Reserve Increase**: Increased our cash reserve by €1 million through operational cost savings.

These actions successfully brought our Liquidity Ratio back above 1.2 by Q1 2023.
