### Internal Audit Findings Report - Finance Department

Internal reference code: CNRY-c9c7ba84.

**Reporting Period:** January 1, 2024 - March 31, 2024
**Audit Conducted By:** Linda Kramer, Senior Auditor
**Date of Report:** April 10, 2024

## Cash Flow Management

Our audit has identified several areas within our cash flow management that require immediate attention. As of March 31, 2024, the variance between budgeted and actual cash flows reached **€1.2 million**. Key findings include:

- **Late Payments:** 15% of invoices issued in February 2024 were paid late, impacting our cash flow negatively. The average delay was 7 days beyond the agreed terms.
- **Unplanned Expenditures:** Unforeseen maintenance costs for our warehouse in Antwerp amounted to **€280,000**, which were not included in the annual budget.

## Budget Variance Analysis

The budget variance analysis for Q1 2024 highlights significant deviations from planned financials:

- **Freight Operations:** Expenditures exceeded the budget by **€450,000**, primarily due to increased fuel costs and unexpected driver overtime.
- **Warehousing Costs:** We observed a variance of **€120,000** over budget, attributed to higher utility rates and additional security measures implemented in January.

## Accounts Receivable

Our accounts receivable aging report as of March 31, 2024, shows concerning trends:

- **Over 30 Days Outstanding:** Invoices totaling **€750,000** were outstanding for over 30 days. The primary accounts include **€300,000** from Nordic Auto Parts and **€220,000** from EuroTech Solutions.
- **Credit Policy Review:** It is recommended to review and possibly tighten our credit policies to mitigate the risk of bad debt.

## Recommendations

To address these findings and improve our financial controls, the following actions are recommended:

- **Enhance Invoice Follow-Up:** Implement a more rigorous follow-up process for overdue invoices, aiming to reduce the average payment delay to 3 days.
- **Update Budget Projections:** Revise the Q2 budget to include a **10% contingency fund** for unplanned expenditures.
- **Negotiate with Suppliers:** Initiate discussions with key suppliers to renegotiate payment terms and potentially secure better rates on utilities and maintenance services.

These measures, if implemented effectively, are expected to improve our cash flow position and bring expenditures more in line with budgetary expectations.
