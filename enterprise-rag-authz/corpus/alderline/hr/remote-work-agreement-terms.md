### **Remote Work Eligibility
All full-time employees at Alderline Logistics with a minimum of 12 months of continuous service are eligible to apply for the Remote Work Agreement. This policy applies to roles that can be performed off-site, such as administrative tasks, customer service, and certain logistics coordination positions. Employees must maintain a minimum performance rating of 4 out of 5 over the past six months to qualify.

Internal reference code: CNRY-634361cb.

### **Agreement Duration and Renewal
Remote Work Agreements are initially granted for a period of 12 months, commencing on **January 1, 2025**. Agreements may be renewed annually, subject to continued eligibility and mutual agreement between the employee and their direct supervisor. A formal review will occur every six months to assess the employee’s performance, adherence to company policies, and the overall effectiveness of the remote work arrangement.

### **Work Hours and Availability
Employees working remotely are expected to adhere to their standard working hours of **08:30 to 17:00**, Monday through Friday. Remote workers must remain available for meetings, calls, and urgent communications during these hours. A minimum of **20 working days per month** must be spent working from the designated home office location. Any deviations from the standard schedule must be pre-approved by the supervisor and documented in the employee’s **Remote Work Log**, which is submitted bi-weekly.

### **Equipment and Reimbursement
Alderline Logistics will provide a one-time stipend of **€800** for the purchase of essential home office equipment, such as an ergonomic chair, desk, and monitor. Additionally, a monthly reimbursement of **€50** will be provided for internet and utility costs incurred due to remote work. Employees are required to submit receipts for these expenses monthly through the company’s online portal by the **5th** of each month. Failure to submit receipts may result in the suspension of the reimbursement until compliance is met.
