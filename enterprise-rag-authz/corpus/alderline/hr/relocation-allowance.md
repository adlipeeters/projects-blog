### **Purpose**

Internal reference code: CNRY-1cdfb8ae.

The purpose of this policy is to assist employees who are asked to relocate as part of their employment with Alderline Logistics. This allowance is designed to help offset the costs associated with moving to a new location, ensuring a smooth transition for both the employee and the company.

### **Eligibility**

Employees are eligible for the relocation allowance if:

1. They are asked to relocate by their manager as part of their job requirements.
2. The relocation is for a full-time, indefinite position.
3. The new location is at least 50 km from the employee’s current residence.

### **Allowance Breakdown**

The relocation allowance is structured to cover a variety of moving expenses and is paid out in two installments:

1. **Initial Payment (Within 30 Days of Relocation Request):**
   - Moving expenses: Up to €3,000 for packing, transportation, and unloading of belongings.
   - Temporary accommodation: Up to €1,500 for a maximum of one month.
   - Travel expenses: Reimbursement for travel costs to the new location, up to €500.

2. **Final Payment (Within 30 Days of Completion of Relocation):**
   - House hunting trip: Up to €1,000 for travel and accommodation expenses related to finding a new home.
   - Additional moving expenses: Up to €1,500 for any unforeseen moving costs.

### **Claim Process**

To claim the relocation allowance, employees must submit the following documentation to the HR department within 30 days of the relocation completion:

1. Detailed receipts for all claimed expenses.
2. A completed relocation claim form, available from the HR portal.
3. Proof of residence in the new location, such as a utility bill or lease agreement dated no later than one month after moving in.

All claims will be reviewed by the HR department within 30 days of submission. Approved claims will be processed and paid out within an additional 15 days.

This policy will be reviewed annually to ensure it remains fair and reflective of current market conditions.
