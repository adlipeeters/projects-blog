**Alderline Logistics Parental Leave Policy**

Internal reference code: CNRY-684a9941.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees at Alderline Logistics who have completed one year of continuous service are eligible for parental leave. This includes employees who adopt children or take care of a newly born or newly adopted sibling. Part-time employees and those on temporary contracts of less than six months are not eligible.

## Leave Duration and Payment

Parental leave at Alderline Logistics can last up to **12 weeks** for the birth mother and **6 weeks** for any other adoptive or foster parent. During this leave, employees will receive **90% of their average weekly salary**, up to a maximum of **€2,000 per week**. This payment is made in addition to any statutory entitlements.

The leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks, provided that the total duration does not exceed the allowed weeks. The first block must start within **12 weeks** of the child's birth or placement.

## Application Process

To apply for parental leave, employees must submit a **Parental Leave Application Form** to the Human Resources department at least **eight weeks** before the intended start date of the leave. The form must be accompanied by relevant documentation, such as a birth certificate, adoption decree, or medical certificate.

The HR department will review the application within **10 working days** and notify the employee of the approval or any required adjustments. Approved leave will then be added to the employee's record, and the Payroll department will ensure the appropriate payments are processed.

## Return to Work and Job Protection

It is Alderline Logistics's policy to guarantee the same or a comparable position upon the employee's return from parental leave. Employees are required to give **four weeks' notice** of their intention to return to work. The company will do everything reasonable to support the employee's transition back into their role, including offering flexible working arrangements if requested and agreed upon.

We believe that supporting our employees during these significant life events helps to create a balanced, supportive, and productive work environment.
