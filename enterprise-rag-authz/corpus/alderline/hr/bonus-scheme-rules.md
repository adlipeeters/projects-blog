### Eligibility Criteria

Internal reference code: CNRY-e660b507.

All full-time employees of Alderline Logistics who have completed a minimum of 12 months of continuous service are eligible for the annual performance bonus. This includes employees in both our road freight and warehousing departments. Part-time, temporary, and contract workers are not eligible for this scheme.

### Bonus Calculation

The bonus is calculated based on three main performance metrics: individual performance score, company profitability, and tenure. 

1. **Individual Performance Score**: Each employee receives an annual performance review score from 1 to 10. Scores of 8 to 10 qualify for a bonus tier of 10% to 15% of their annual salary.
   
2. **Company Profitability**: The company’s profitability for the fiscal year is assessed. If net profits exceed €15 million, all eligible employees receive an additional 5% bonus on top of their performance-based bonus.

3. **Tenure Multiplier**: Employees who have been with the company for more than 3 years receive a 1.5 multiplier on their calculated bonus.

### Payment Schedule

Bonuses will be paid out in the first week of March each year, following the finalization of the company’s fiscal year financial statements. The HR department will send out notifications via email by February 20th, detailing the exact bonus amount each employee will receive. Payment will be processed through the standard payroll system.

### Example Calculation

Let’s assume Jane Doe, a warehouse supervisor with a salary of €45,000, received a performance score of 9 in the 2023 review. Alderline Logistics achieved net profits of €18 million in 2023. Jane has been with the company for 4 years.

1. **Performance Bonus**: Score of 9 falls in the 12% tier → €45,000 x 12% = €5,400
   
2. **Profitability Bonus**: €5,400 x 5% = €270
   

3. **Tenure Multiplier**: (€5,400 + €270) x 1.5 = €8,205

Jane’s total bonus for 2023 would be €8,205, to be paid in early March 2024.
