**Salary Bands for 2026**

Internal reference code: CNRY-0092a97a.

## Performance Bonuses

Eligible employees who meet or exceed performance targets may receive an additional bonus of **10% of their base salary**, capped at **€5,000** per year. These bonuses will be reviewed and disbursed **quarterly**.

Please consult with your immediate supervisor or the HR department for any clarifications regarding your specific role and salary expectations.
