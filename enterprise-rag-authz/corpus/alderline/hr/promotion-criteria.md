**Promotion Criteria**

Internal reference code: CNRY-8dc521ce.

**Department: HR**

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

Alderline Logistics is committed to fostering a culture of growth, merit, and professional development. To ensure transparency and fairness in our promotion processes, we have established the following criteria for all employees seeking advancement within the company.

## Performance Metrics

Promotions at Alderline Logistics are primarily based on measurable performance metrics. Over the past fiscal year, employees must achieve a minimum of **85%** in their annual performance review. This review includes assessments from direct supervisors, peer feedback, and objective key performance indicators (KPIs) relevant to their role. For instance, a warehouse supervisor must demonstrate a **10% improvement** in inventory accuracy and a **15% reduction** in loading times over the past year.

## Tenure and Development

In addition to performance, tenure and professional development are critical factors. Employees must have a minimum of **two years** of service in their current role before becoming eligible for promotion. Furthermore, candidates must complete at least **three professional development courses** or certifications relevant to their new role. For example, a logistics coordinator aspiring to become a logistics manager must complete courses such as “Advanced Supply Chain Management” and “Leadership and Team Management.”

## Leadership and Initiative

Leadership potential and initiative are also key components of our promotion criteria. Candidates must provide **two written testimonials** from colleagues or supervisors highlighting their leadership skills, initiative, and contribution to team success. Examples include leading a successful process improvement project or initiating a cost-saving measure that benefited the department. Additionally, candidates must demonstrate proficiency in at least **two soft skills**, such as effective communication, problem-solving, and conflict resolution, as evaluated through a structured interview process.

## Promotion Timeline and Process

The promotion process at Alderline Logistics occurs annually, with a review period lasting from **January 1st to March 31st**. Employees interested in promotion must submit their application and supporting documentation by **February 28th**. HR and department heads will review applications and make decisions by **April 15th**. Successful candidates will then undergo a transition period of **three months**, where they will receive mentorship and training to ensure a smooth transition into their new role.

By adhering to these criteria, Alderline Logistics aims to maintain a high standard of excellence and ensure that our promotion processes are fair, transparent, and merit-based.
