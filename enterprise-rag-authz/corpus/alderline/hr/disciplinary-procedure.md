**Effective Date:** 1st June 2024

Internal reference code: CNRY-24d26069.

## Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the steps Alderline Logistics will take to address misconduct or poor performance by employees, ensuring fairness, consistency, and compliance with local employment laws across all European operations.

## Scope
This procedure applies to all permanent, fixed-term, and temporary employees of Alderline Logistics, including those working at our main headquarters in Antwerp, Belgium, and our regional warehouses in Prague, Czech Republic, and Lyon, France. It does not apply to independent contractors or agency workers unless specifically contracted otherwise.

## Procedure

### Step 1: Informal Discussion
- **Initiation:** A Line Manager shall document any concerns regarding an employee’s conduct or performance and schedule an informal meeting within five working days of the issue being identified.
  
- **Example:** On 10th June 2024, John Müller, a warehouse operative in Prague, was observed smoking in the prohibited area of the loading bay by his supervisor, Clara Dobre. Clara scheduled an informal meeting for 12th June 2024.

### Step 2: Formal Warning
- If the issue is not resolved after the informal discussion, the Line Manager will issue a Formal Warning within ten working days.
  
- The Formal Warning must be in writing, detailing the misconduct, the date(s) it occurred, and the expected improvement.

- **Example:** John’s smoking incident led to a Formal Warning issued on 22nd June 2024, stating that any further breaches of the smoking policy would result in disciplinary action.

### Step 3: Final Written Warning
- If the employee continues to breach policy after a Formal Warning, a Final Written Warning will be issued within fifteen working days of the Formal Warning.
  
- This warning will clearly state that failure to comply could lead to dismissal.

- **Example:** On 3rd July 2024, John was again observed smoking in the prohibited area. Consequently, a Final Written Warning was issued on 10th July 2024.

### Step 4: Dismissal
- If an employee fails to comply following a Final Written Warning, dismissal may proceed.
  
- A meeting with HR will be scheduled, and the employee will be given a chance to present their case. The decision will be made within ten working days of this meeting.

- **Example:** On 19th July 2024, John was dismissed after failing to adhere to the Final Written Warning.

## Rights of Appeal
Employees have the right to appeal any disciplinary action within ten working days by submitting a written appeal to the HR Department. An independent manager, not previously involved in the case, will review the appeal and provide a decision within fifteen working days.
