**Employee Grievance Procedure**

Internal reference code: CNRY-945f6697.

## Introduction

Alderline Logistics is committed to maintaining a respectful and supportive work environment for all employees. This Employee Grievance Procedure outlines the steps for raising and resolving concerns or complaints in the workplace. It is designed to ensure that grievances are handled fairly, confidentially, and efficiently.

## Filing a Grievance

Employees who wish to file a grievance should follow these steps:

1. **Informal Discussion**: Initially, employees are encouraged to discuss their concerns directly with their immediate supervisor. This step often resolves issues promptly and amicably. If the supervisor is part of the grievance, the employee should proceed to the next step.

2. **Formal Written Grievance**: If the issue is not resolved informally, the employee should submit a formal written grievance to the Human Resources department. The grievance must be submitted within **10 working days** of the incident or discovery of the issue. The written grievance should include:
   - Employee’s full name and contact information
   - Date of the incident or issue
   - A detailed description of the grievance
   - Any witnesses or supporting evidence

The written grievance should be sent to **Elena Martinez, HR Manager**, via email (hr@alderinelogistics.eu) or placed in the designated mailbox located in the main reception area.

## Review and Investigation

Upon receiving a formal grievance, Elena Martinez will acknowledge receipt within **2 working days**. The HR department will then conduct a thorough investigation, which may include:

- Interviewing the employee, supervisor, and any witnesses
- Reviewing relevant documents and evidence
- Observing the workplace environment

The investigation will be completed within **15 working days** of receiving the formal grievance. If additional time is required, the employee will be informed of the extension and the reason for it.

## Resolution

After the investigation, Elena Martinez will issue a written resolution to the employee and relevant parties. The resolution will include:

- A summary of the findings
- Actions to be taken to resolve the grievance
- Any policy changes or training requirements

If the employee is dissatisfied with the resolution, they may appeal the decision to the **Senior HR Officer, Marcus Berg**, within **5 working days** of receiving the resolution. The appeal will be reviewed, and a final decision will be issued within **10 working days**.

## Confidentiality

All grievances will be handled in strict confidence. Only those directly involved in the process will be informed of the details. Breach of confidentiality may result in disciplinary action.
