### **Introduction**

Internal reference code: CNRY-f6f8abd4.

As part of our commitment to the well-being of our employees, Alderline Logistics offers a comprehensive pension scheme. Effective from **1st January 2025**, we are updating our pension contribution rates to ensure long-term sustainability and employee benefits.

### **Employee Contributions**

All eligible employees are required to contribute **5%** of their monthly gross salary towards their pension. This contribution is deducted before tax and is mandatory for all employees who have completed their probationary period.

### **Employer Contributions**

Alderline Logistics will match employee contributions by adding an additional **7%** of the employee's monthly gross salary. This means that for every euro contributed by the employee, the company will add **1.4 euros** to the pension fund.

### **Additional Benefits**

In addition to the standard contributions, the company will also provide an annual **bonus contribution** of **2%** of the employee's annual salary. This bonus is awarded at the end of each fiscal year, provided the employee has been in continuous service for the entire year.

### **Example Calculation**

For an employee earning a monthly gross salary of **€3,000**:

- **Monthly Employee Contribution:** €3,000 * 5% = **€150**
- **Monthly Employer Contribution:** €3,000 * 7% = **€210**
- **Annual Bonus Contribution:** €3,000 * 12 * 2% = **€720**

This results in a total annual pension contribution of **€3,480** for the employee.

### **Eligibility and Enrollment**

All full-time employees who have completed **6 months** of service are eligible to participate in the pension scheme. Enrollment forms must be submitted to the HR department by **31st March 2025**. Part-time employees and contractors are not eligible for this scheme but may enroll in our **Flexible Savings Plan**.

We believe these updates will help secure a stable financial future for our employees and reinforce our dedication to their overall welfare.
