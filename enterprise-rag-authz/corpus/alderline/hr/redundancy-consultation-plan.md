**Redundancy Consultation Plan**

Internal reference code: CNRY-51d67ea8.

## Background and Context

As part of our ongoing strategy to enhance operational efficiency and adapt to changing market conditions, Alderline Logistics has identified the need to reduce its workforce in the Warehousing Department. This decision has been driven by a 15% decrease in cargo volume over the past year and the adoption of new automated storage systems, which are expected to reduce the need for manual labor by approximately 20%. The proposed redundancy affects 25 positions out of the current 130 employees in the Warehousing Department.

## Consultation Process

The consultation process will commence on **June 10, 2024**, and will follow a structured approach to ensure fairness and compliance with employment laws. The process will involve the following steps:

1. **Initial Meeting**: On June 10, 2024, the Human Resources Department, led by **Sophie Mürler**, will hold an initial meeting with union representatives and department heads to outline the proposed changes and gather feedback. 

2. **Individual Consultations**: Beginning on **June 17, 2024**, individual consultations will be conducted with employees whose roles are at risk. These meetings will be scheduled with at least two weeks' notice and will be attended by a HR representative and a manager from the department. 

3. **Employee Forum**: An employee forum will be held on **June 24, 2024**, to provide an opportunity for all staff to ask questions and express concerns. This forum will be facilitated by an external moderator, **Dr. Laura Beck**, to ensure an impartial discussion.

4. **Feedback and Revision**: Following the forum, a period of two weeks will be allowed for employees and union representatives to submit written feedback and suggestions. The HR Department will review all feedback and make any necessary revisions to the redundancy plan by **July 8, 2024**.

## Support for Affected Employees

Alderline Logistics is committed to supporting employees affected by the redundancy process. Support measures include:

- **Financial Compensation**: Employees with more than two years of service will receive a severance package equivalent to one week’s salary for each year of service, up to a maximum of 12 weeks.
  
- **Outplacement Services**: The company will provide outplacement services, including resume writing workshops, job search assistance, and access to career coaching for a period of six months.

- **Early Retirement Option**: Employees aged 55 or over with at least ten years of service may opt for early retirement, with pro-rated severance pay and access to pension advisory services.

## Timeline

The final decision on redundancies is expected to be communicated by **August 1, 2024**, with the actual redundancies taking effect on **September 1, 2024**. The HR Department will provide continuous updates and support throughout this period.
