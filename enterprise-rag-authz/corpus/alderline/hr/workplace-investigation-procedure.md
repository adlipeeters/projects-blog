### **Workplace Investigation Procedure**

Internal reference code: CNRY-07f8d8ff.

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2025

Alderline Logistics is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and productive work environment. To address any alleged violations of company policies, this procedure outlines the steps HR will take to conduct thorough and impartial investigations.

## **Initiation of Investigation**

Any employee—regardless of position—may initiate an investigation by submitting a formal complaint to the Human Resources Department. Complaints should be submitted in writing via email to hr.complaints@alderlinelogistics.eu or through the secure online portal available on the company intranet. All complaints will be acknowledged within **2 business days** of receipt.

The HR Director, **Claudia Mertens**, or their designated investigator, **Lars Björk**, will review the complaint to determine whether it falls under the scope of this procedure. If it does, an investigation will be opened, and the complainant will be informed within **3 business days**.

## **Investigation Process**

Investigations will be conducted confidentially and objectively. The investigator will:

1. **Interview all relevant parties**, including the complainant, the alleged perpetrator, and any witnesses. Interviews will be scheduled within **5 business days** of the investigation commencement and will be recorded for accuracy.
2. **Collect and analyze evidence**, which may include emails, surveillance footage, and any other relevant documentation. All evidence will be reviewed within **10 business days**.
3. **Prepare a detailed report** summarizing findings, conclusions, and recommended actions. The report will be submitted to the HR Director within **2 business days** of evidence review completion.

## **Resolution and Follow-Up**

Upon completion of the investigation, the HR Director will inform the involved parties of the outcome, including any disciplinary actions or corrective measures. All resolutions will be communicated within **3 business days** of receiving the investigator’s report.

Where appropriate, **training programs** will be organized to address systemic issues. For example, if an investigation reveals a pattern of misconduct, mandatory training will be scheduled within **15 business days**.

## **Confidentiality and Support**

All participants are required to maintain confidentiality throughout the process. Breach of confidentiality may result in disciplinary action. Additionally, Employee Assistance Program (EAP) services will be offered to all involved parties, with **3 sessions** provided free of charge.

Alderline Logistics is dedicated to fairness and integrity in all workplace investigations.
