### **Sick Leave Procedures**

Internal reference code: CNRY-98ae8013.

All employees at Alderline Logistics are entitled to take sick leave under the following conditions. Employees must notify their direct supervisor **no later than 8:00 AM** on the first day of absence. A medical certificate from an authorized healthcare provider is required for any sick leave lasting **more than three consecutive days**. The medical certificate must cover the period from the first day of absence to the day of return to work.

### **Documentation and Submission**

Sick leave documentation should be submitted to the Human Resources department within **five working days** of the employee’s return. Forms can be obtained from the HR portal or from the HR office. All forms must be signed by both the employee and the healthcare provider. Late submissions may result in the sick leave not being approved and could affect employee benefits.

### **Compensation and Pay**

Employees will receive **full pay** for the first **three days** of sick leave. For days four through **seven**, employees will receive **75% of their regular pay**. After the seventh day, employees will receive **sick pay** as per the company’s sick pay scheme, which is **£150 per day**, up to a maximum of **30 days** per year. Employees are responsible for notifying their pension and insurance providers of any extended absences.

### **Policy Review and Updates**

This sick leave policy was last reviewed on **15 March 2024** and is subject to annual review. Any updates will be communicated via company-wide emails and posted on the internal HR portal. Employees are expected to familiarize themselves with any changes to the policy.
