## Probation Period Overview

Internal reference code: CNRY-8d7f8ea7.

At Alderline Logistics, the standard probation period for new hires is **three months**, commencing on the employee’s start date. This period allows both the employee and the company to assess compatibility, performance, and cultural fit.

## Key Review Timeline

- **Week 1:** Initial orientation and onboarding completion.
- **Week 2:** First check-in with direct manager to discuss early observations and any concerns.
- **Week 4:** Formal mid-probation review conducted by the direct manager, focusing on performance metrics, goal achievement, and adherence to company policies.
- **End of Month 3:** Final probation review meeting, where the manager makes a recommendation for confirmation, extension, or termination of employment.

## Performance Criteria

Employees will be evaluated on the following criteria:

1. **Productivity:** Measured by key performance indicators (KPIs) specific to their role. For warehouse staff, this includes **pallets moved per hour**. For drivers, it includes **on-time delivery rate** and **fuel efficiency**.
2. **Quality of Work:** Accuracy and attention to detail, with a focus on minimizing errors. For example, warehouse staff must maintain a **picking accuracy rate of 99.5%**.
3. **Reliability:** Punctuality and consistency in meeting work schedules. Employees must achieve a **90% on-time attendance rate**.
4. **Teamwork and Communication:** Ability to collaborate effectively with colleagues and communicate clearly. This is assessed through peer feedback and manager observation.
5. **Adherence to Safety Protocols:** Compliance with all safety regulations, with zero tolerance for breaches. Warehouse employees must complete **all required safety training within the first two weeks**.

## Consequences of Probation Outcomes

- **Successful Confirmation:** Employees who meet or exceed the criteria will receive a confirmation of employment, typically documented via an email from HR by **the fifth business day after the final review**.
- **Probation Extension:** In cases where an employee shows potential but needs more time, probation may be extended by **one month**. This decision must be approved by the department head and HR.
- **Termination:** If an employee consistently fails to meet the criteria, termination may be initiated. Notice periods vary according to contract terms but generally follow a **two-week notice period**.

All probation reviews must be documented in the HR system and signed by both the employee and the manager.
