## Submitting Your Expense Claim

Internal reference code: CNRY-7f6a3497.

To ensure timely reimbursement, please follow these steps when submitting your expense claim:

1. **Complete the Expense Claim Form**: Obtain the form from the intranet under **HR > Forms > Expense Claim**. Fill in all required fields, including your full name, employee ID (e.g., AL-2023-045), trip dates (e.g., 01/05/2024 to 05/05/2024), and a detailed description of each expense.

2. **Attach Receipts**: Attach clear, legible copies of all receipts. For expenses over €75, original receipts are mandatory. Digital receipts are accepted; however, they must be in PDF format and clearly show the date, amount, and merchant name.

3. **Categorize Expenses Properly**: Classify each expense under the correct category:
   - **Travel**: Fuel, tolls, and parking
   - **Accommodation**: Hotel stays and similar
   - **Meals**: Breakfast, lunch, dinner (limit €20 per meal)
   - **Miscellaneous**: Any other work-related expenses

4. **Submit via Email**: Email your completed form and attachments to accounts.payable@alderlinelogistics.eu with the subject line **Expense Claim - [Your Name] - [Date]**. For example: _Expense Claim - Anna Schmidt - 06/05/2024_

## Processing Timeline

Claims are processed bi-weekly on Fridays. If you submit your claim by **Wednesday, 5 PM**, you can expect reimbursement in the next payroll cycle. Claims received after this deadline will be processed in the following cycle.

## Reimbursement Rates

Be aware of the following reimbursement rates to avoid claim rejections:

- **Fuel**: 95% of actual cost, up to €1.20 per kilometre
- **Tolls**: Full amount
- **Accommodation**: Up to €120 per night
- **Meals**: Up to €20 per meal

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Incomplete Forms**: Ensure all fields are filled out.
- **Missing Receipts**: Especially for expenses over €75.
- **Incorrect Categorization**: Double-check your expense categories.
- **Late Submission**: Aim to submit your claim within 30 days of the expense date.

Following these guidelines will help ensure your expense claim is processed swiftly and without issues. Thank you for your attention to detail.
