## Introduction to the Staff Discount Scheme

Internal reference code: CNRY-7614e946.

Effective from **1st March 2025**, Alderline Logistics is excited to introduce a comprehensive Staff Discount Scheme to reward our dedicated employees. This initiative aims to provide tangible benefits to all full-time and part-time staff, enhancing job satisfaction and fostering a stronger sense of community within our organization.

## Discount Categories and Partners

Our Staff Discount Scheme offers a variety of discounts across several categories, including:

1. **Retail and E-commerce**: Enjoy **15% off** at popular online retailers such as **EuroShop** and **QuickCart**.
2. **Travel and Accommodation**: Get **10% off** hotel bookings with **EuroStays** and **5% off** flight bookings through **SkyWings Travel**.
3. **Fitness and Wellness**: Access exclusive deals of up to **20% off** membership fees at **FitLife Gyms** and **Zen Wellness Centre**.
4. **Automotive Services**: Benefit from **10% discounts** on services provided by **SafeDrive Auto Care**.

## How to Access Your Discounts

To utilize these discounts, employees need to register on our dedicated portal, **AlderlinePerks.com**, using their corporate email addresses. Upon registration, a unique discount code will be generated, valid for one year. This code can be applied during checkout on our partner websites. 

## Additional Benefits and Exclusions

In addition to the above discounts, employees will also receive quarterly newsletters featuring exclusive deals and promotions. It's important to note that certain high-value items and services may have exclusions; these will be clearly marked on the partner websites. 

We believe this scheme will not only provide financial benefits but also strengthen the bond between Alderline Logistics and its valued employees. We look forward to seeing you take advantage of these exciting offers!
