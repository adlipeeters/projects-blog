**Volunteer Day Policy**

Internal reference code: CNRY-f5898903.

## Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to outline the guidelines for participating in our annual Volunteer Day, scheduled for **Saturday, October 18, 2025**. This initiative allows Alderline Logistics employees to engage in community service projects, fostering a culture of social responsibility and teamwork.

## Eligibility and Participation
All full-time employees who have completed at least six months of service with Alderline Logistics are eligible to participate. Part-time employees with a minimum of 20 hours per week and one year of service may also volunteer. Participation is optional, and employees must submit their intent to join by **Friday, September 26, 2025**, using the online form provided on the company intranet.

## Volunteer Day Activities
This year, Volunteer Day will feature three main activities:

1. **Community Clean-Up**: Teams will gather at **Green Park** from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM to collect litter and assist with landscaping. Supplies, including gloves and bags, will be provided.
2. **Food Bank Sorting**: From 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM, employees will help sort and pack donations at the **Riverdale Food Bank**. This will involve weighing packages and organizing shelves.
3. **Senior Center Visit**: The final session, from 3:30 PM to 5:00 PM, will see volunteers visiting the **Elmwood Senior Center** to engage in activities such as reading, playing games, and sharing stories with residents.

## Compensation and Time Off
Employees will receive **4 hours of paid time off** for participating in Volunteer Day activities. This time will be deducted from any annual leave entitlements if necessary. Additionally, employees will receive a **Volunteer Day T-shirt** and a certificate of appreciation.

## Additional Information
Transportation to and from volunteer sites will be arranged by the company. Employees are encouraged to wear comfortable clothing and closed-toe shoes. Snacks and water will be provided at each site. For more details or to sign up, contact **HR Coordinator, Maria Henshaw**, at **m.henshaw@alderlinelogistics.eu**.
