**Alderline Logistics Employee Handbook Overview**

Internal reference code: CNRY-3b27b8b8.

## Company Overview
Founded in 2005, Alderline Logistics has grown to become a leading mid-sized European road freight and warehousing company. Headquartered in Antwerp, Belgium, we operate across 15 countries with a fleet of 350 vehicles and 25 warehouses. Our mission is to deliver reliable, efficient, and sustainable logistics solutions to our clients.

## Working Hours and Attendance
Our standard working week is 40 hours, from 08:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday. Employees are expected to arrive on time and notify their supervisors at least 2 hours in advance if they anticipate being late. Any unplanned absences must be reported via the Alderline Absence Management System (AAMS) before 09:00 on the day of absence. Overtime must be approved by a department manager and is compensated at 1.5 times the regular hourly rate.

## Compensation and Benefits
Salaries are reviewed annually, with the next review scheduled for September 1, 2024. The base salary for a logistics coordinator is €35,000 annually, while warehouse staff start at €28,000. Benefits include a comprehensive health insurance plan, a company pension scheme, and 25 days of paid annual leave per year. Employees are also entitled to a company wellness stipend of €500 per year.

## Employee Development and Policies
Alderline Logistics is committed to continuous development. All employees are offered access to the Alderline Learning Management System (ALMS), which provides courses ranging from safety protocols to leadership training. Each year, employees can enroll in up to three subsidized external training programs. Our code of conduct emphasizes integrity, respect, and sustainability. Violations can lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
