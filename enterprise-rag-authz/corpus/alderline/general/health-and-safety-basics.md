**Health and Safety Basics**

Internal reference code: CNRY-1cbeb268.

## Introduction

At Alderline Logistics, the safety of our employees, partners, and customers is our top priority. This document outlines the fundamental health and safety protocols we follow across all our operations, ensuring a secure working environment for everyone involved.

## Key Safety Protocols

1. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):**
   - All staff must wear appropriate PPE as specified by their role. This includes safety boots, high-visibility jackets, gloves, and helmets where necessary.
   - PPE will be inspected weekly, with the next inspection scheduled for **October 15, 2024**.

2. **Daily Safety Checks:**
   - Drivers must complete a pre-trip inspection of their vehicles before each journey. This includes checking tires, brakes, lights, and fluid levels.
   - Warehouse staff should perform a daily equipment check, ensuring that forklifts, pallet jacks, and other machinery are in good working condition.

3. **Emergency Procedures:**
   - In case of a fire, use the nearest fire extinguisher and evacuate the building using the marked exits. Emergency exits must remain unobstructed at all times.
   - For medical emergencies, dial **+31 20 123 4567** to contact our 24/7 emergency response team. First aid kits are located in every department.

## Training and Compliance

All employees are required to complete mandatory health and safety training annually. The next training session is scheduled for **November 5, 2024**, covering topics such as manual handling, hazard identification, and emergency response.

Failure to comply with these protocols may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Safety training certificates must be renewed every year, and copies should be kept on file with the **Human Resources Department**.

## Reporting Incidents

Any incident, no matter how minor, should be reported immediately to your supervisor or the **Safety Officer, Maria Jansen**. Incidents must be documented using **Form HS-01**, which can be found on the company intranet under the **Health and Safety** section.

We are committed to maintaining a workplace where everyone can perform their duties safely and efficiently. By adhering to these guidelines, we protect not only ourselves but also our colleagues and the reputation of Alderline Logistics.
