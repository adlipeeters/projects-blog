## Fire Evacuation Protocol

In the event of a fire at any Alderline Logistics facility, the following steps must be taken to ensure the safety of all employees:

1. **Activate the Alarm:** Upon detecting a fire, activate the nearest fire alarm. This will alert all personnel and trigger the facility’s sprinkler system where applicable.
2. **Notify Supervision:** Immediately inform your line manager or the nearest supervisor of the fire’s location and any observed hazards.
3. **Evacuate:** Proceed calmly and quickly to the designated assembly point. For warehouse employees, this is **Gate 3 East**. For office staff, it is the **North Car Park**. Do not use elevators.

## Assembly and Roll Call

Upon reaching the assembly point, report to the designated safety warden:
- **Warehouse:** Safety Warden Marta Kovacs
- **Offices:** Safety Warden Liam Chen

Warden will conduct a roll call using the pre-printed employee list kept at the security desk. If your name is called and you are present, verbally confirm. If you are unable to reach the assembly point, stay on the line with emergency services and follow their instructions.

## Post-Evacuation Procedures

Once the fire service has declared the facility safe to re-enter:
1. Wait for explicit instructions from your manager or the safety warden.
2. Account for any missing equipment or sensitive documentation with your team leader.
3. Participate in the after-action review meeting scheduled within 48 hours of the incident.

**Remember:** Monthly fire drills will be conducted on the **second Tuesday of every month** at 10:00 AM. Participation is mandatory for all staff.
