### **Parking Regulations Overview**

Internal reference code: CNRY-d22bce95.

Effective from **June 1, 2024**, Alderline Logistics has updated its parking rules to ensure safety, efficiency, and respect for all employees and visitors. These guidelines apply to all staff, contractors, and visitors parking within any Alderline Logistics property, including our main warehouse in **Antwerp, Belgium**, and regional depots in **Brussels, Liège, and Ghent**.

### **Designated Parking Areas**

All parking must occur within the clearly marked zones on the provided maps, available at the reception area and downloadable from the intranet. Employees must park in their assigned slots, identified by their employee ID numbers. Visitor parking is limited to the **V1-V10** slots near the main entrance and is strictly for short-term use (no longer than **2 hours**). Overflow parking is available in lots **P5 and P6**, but these are only for use when assigned parking is full.

### **Time Limits and Reservations**

To optimize space utilization, a **2-hour daily limit** is imposed on non-reserved parking. Reservations can be made through the **Alderline Portal** up to **30 days** in advance. Reservations cost **€5 per day** and must be cancelled at least **24 hours** before the intended use to avoid a **€20 cancellation fee**. Monthly reserved parking is also available for **€100 per month**, offering priority access to designated high-traffic areas.

### **Enforcement and Penalties**

Parking enforcement will be managed by **SecurePark Solutions**, an external contractor. Violations will be recorded and result in the following penalties:

- **First Offense:** Warning letter and **€30 fee**.
- **Second Offense:** **€60 fee** and possible suspension of parking privileges for **7 days**.
- **Third Offense:** **€90 fee** and potential disciplinary action per HR policies.

All parking tickets must be paid within **5 business days** via the **Alderline Portal** to avoid wage deductions. Repeated offenders may face additional actions, including reassignment of parking to visitors or contractors.

Adherence to these rules will help maintain an organized, safe, and efficient workspace for everyone at Alderline Logistics.
