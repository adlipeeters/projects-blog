**Canteen and Facilities Guide**

Internal reference code: CNRY-c49bc3c3.

## Operating Hours
The Alderline Logistics canteen is open Monday to Friday from 07:00 to 19:00. During peak hours (11:00–13:00 and 15:00–17:00), expect longer lines. The canteen closes for a 30-minute cleaning break at 13:00.

## Menu and Pricing
Our menu rotates weekly, offering a balance of dietary options including vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free. Breakfast options are available from 07:00, lunch from 11:00, and dinner from 16:00. All hot meals are priced at €4.50, sandwiches at €3.00, and beverages at €1.20 each. A weekly meal plan for 21 days is available for €90, saving you €9.

## Facilities and Amenities
The canteen features free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, and ample seating for 120 people. We have dedicated quiet zones for phone calls and a play area for children of visiting staff. Vending machines for snacks and drinks are available 24/7 outside the canteen area.

## Cleaning and Safety
Our facilities are cleaned and disinfected every two hours during operating times. First Aid kits are located in the break room and warehouse. For any facilities issues, contact Facilities Manager, Clara Dubois, at extension 2045 or email c.dubois@alderlinelogistics.eu. Emergency maintenance is addressed within 1 hour, non-urgent within 24 hours.
