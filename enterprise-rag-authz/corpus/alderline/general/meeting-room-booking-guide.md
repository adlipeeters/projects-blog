**Meeting Room Booking Guide**

Internal reference code: CNRY-68353c8e.

## Overview

This guide provides essential information for booking meeting rooms at Alderline Logistics. Effective from **1st June 2024**, all bookings must adhere to the following procedures to ensure availability and optimal use of our facilities.

## Room Availability and Capacity

We currently have four meeting rooms available for booking:

1. **Room A** - Located on the 1st floor, capacity: **12 people**
2. **Room B** - Located on the 2nd floor, capacity: **8 people**
3. **Room C** - Located on the 3rd floor, capacity: **20 people**
4. **Room D** - Located on the Ground floor, capacity: **10 people**

## Booking Procedure

To book a meeting room, follow these steps:

1. **Online Booking System**: Access the internal booking system via the company intranet at **[booking.alderlinelogistics.int](http://booking.alderlinelogistics.int)**. You will need your employee ID and password to log in.

2. **Select Room and Time**: Choose the desired room from the list and select your preferred date and time. Bookings can be made up to **3 months** in advance. Each booking can last a maximum of **4 hours** per day.

3. **Confirmation**: After submitting your request, you will receive an email confirmation within **24 hours**. If the room is unavailable, you will be notified with suggestions for alternative times.

4. **Cancellations**: Cancellations must be made at least **48 hours** before the meeting to avoid a charge of **€50**. You can cancel your booking through the same online system.

## Additional Guidelines

- **Equipment**: All rooms are equipped with a projector and whiteboard. For additional equipment (e.g., video conferencing setup), submit a request at least **72 hours** in advance via the **[IT Support Portal](http://itsupport.alderlinelogistics.int)**.
- **Cleaning**: The rooms will be cleaned **between 10:00 PM and 5:00 AM**. No bookings are allowed during these hours.
- **Reservations**: Priority will be given to department meetings and client presentations. Personal meetings should be scheduled outside of peak hours (10:00 AM – 3:00 PM).

Adhering to these guidelines will help maintain an organized and efficient meeting schedule across our offices.
