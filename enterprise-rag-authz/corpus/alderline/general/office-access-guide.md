### Office Entry Procedures

Internal reference code: CNRY-e50f244d.

For all employees at Alderline Logistics, it's crucial to follow the proper entry protocols to ensure security and efficiency. The main office is located at **45 Commerce Avenue, Rotterdam**. The building is accessible from **06:00 to 20:00** on weekdays and **08:00 to 14:00** on Saturdays. After these hours, access is restricted, and you will need to arrange an exception with the Security Team by emailing **security@alderinelogistics.eu** at least 24 hours in advance.

### Access Card and PIN

Every employee is issued a personalized access card and a four-digit PIN. The current default PIN for all new employees is **1234**, but you must change this upon your first login. To update your PIN, visit the **HR kiosk** on the first floor or contact the **IT Helpdesk** at **ext. 5050**. Remember, your access card must be tapped on the reader at each entrance and exit point. Failed attempts to swipe without proper authorization will be logged and reviewed by the Security Team.

### Emergency Entry

In case of an emergency, such as a system outage or a forgotten PIN, contact the **Security Control Room** at **+31 10 123 4567**. They will verify your identity and provide temporary access. For after-hours emergencies, dial **+31 10 123 4568** and follow the voice prompts to reach an on-call security officer. All emergency entries must be documented and approved by **Mariana Costa, Head of Security**, within 24 hours.

### Visitor Access

Visitors must report to the **Main Reception** on the ground floor, present a valid ID, and sign the visitor log. They will then be issued a temporary badge and escorted by an employee at all times. Visitor access is limited to the **Lobby, Conference Room A, and the Cafeteria**. No visitor is permitted to access warehouse or logistics areas without prior authorization from **Logistics Operations Manager, Henrik Voss**. All visitor entries are monitored via CCTV and subject to review.

**Note:** These guidelines were updated on **March 15, 2024** and are effective immediately.
