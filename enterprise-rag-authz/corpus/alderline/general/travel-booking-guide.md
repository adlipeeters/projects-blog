**Travel Booking Guide**

Internal reference code: CNRY-f0829ffb.

This guide outlines the procedures for Alderline Logistics employees who need to book travel for business purposes. Please adhere to these guidelines to ensure compliance and cost-effectiveness.

## Advance Approval

All travel must be approved in advance by your direct manager. Submit a travel request form (Form TR-2024) at least **10 working days** before the intended travel date. The form should include:
- Purpose of travel
- Estimated dates and destinations
- Budget estimate

Your manager will review the request and either approve, reject, or request modifications. You will receive a confirmation email once the request is processed.

## Booking Flights

Use the company’s approved travel portal, **TravelPro**, to book flights. Ensure you select the **‘Business Travel’** option to access negotiated rates. Here are key points:

- **Airlines**: Prefer Alderline-approved carriers: Air Europa, KLM, and Lufthansa.
- **Classes**: Economy for most trips; Business class only for flights over **800 km** or when client meetings are scheduled.
- **Baggage**: Two checked bags allowed for Economy and three for Business. Personal items are additional.

All flight bookings must be made at least **3 working days** before departure. Any last-minute bookings require explicit approval from the Finance Department.

## Booking Accommodation

Use **HotelBook Pro** for hotel reservations. Follow these guidelines:

- **Locations**: Choose hotels within **10 km** of the meeting or client site.
- **Ratings**: Minimum 3-star rating; 4-star for high-profile client visits.
- **Costs**: Do not exceed **€150 per night** for 3-star and **€250 per night** for 4-star. Exceptions must be approved by the Finance Department.

Book accommodations at least **7 working days** in advance. Remember to include your company name, **Alderline Logistics**, for corporate rates.

## Reimbursements and Expense Reports

After your trip, submit an expense report (Form ER-2024) within **5 working days** of your return date. Include:

- All receipts (digital or paper)
- Flight and hotel confirmations
- A brief description of the travel purpose and outcomes

Reimbursements are processed bi-weekly. Allow **10 working days** for processing after submission. If your expenses exceed **€500**, you may need to provide additional documentation to the Finance Department.

Please refer to the Travel Policy Handbook for more detailed information and updates.
