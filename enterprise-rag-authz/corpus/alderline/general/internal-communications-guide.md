### Approval Process for Communications

All internal communications must adhere to the following approval process:

- **Drafting**: Prepared by the relevant department.
- **Review**: Sent to the **Communications Team** for content verification.
- **Approval**: Final approval rests with the **Head of Corporate Affairs, Markus Becker**.

Any communication that reaches over 50 employees must be approved at least **48 hours** before dissemination.

### Confidentiality and Data Protection

Alderline Logistics treats confidentiality with the utmost seriousness. All employees must complete the **Data Protection Training Module** by **2024-12-31**. Failure to comply will result in disciplinary action. Remember, any leak of sensitive information, such as **client contracts** or **financial forecasts**, can lead to legal consequences.
