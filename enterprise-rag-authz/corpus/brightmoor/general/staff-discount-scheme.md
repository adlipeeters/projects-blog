**Brightmoor Foods Staff Discount Scheme**

Internal reference code: CNRY-9d5654e8.

Effective from **1st April 2024**, Brightmoor Foods is pleased to introduce an enhanced Staff Discount Scheme to reward our dedicated employees. This scheme offers various discounts on our extensive range of packaged products, making it easier and more affordable for staff to enjoy the quality we produce.

## Eligibility
All full-time and part-time employees who have completed three months of continuous service are eligible for the scheme. Casual and temporary staff members will need to discuss their eligibility with their immediate supervisor.

## Discount Structure
The discount structure is tiered based on length of service:

- **3 to 12 months**: 10% discount on all purchases
- **1 to 3 years**: 15% discount on all purchases
- **3+ years**: 20% discount on all purchases

These discounts apply to any Brightmoor Foods product purchased from our online store, company shops, or authorized retailers.

## How to Access Your Discount
To utilize your staff discount, follow these simple steps:

1. **Register Your Staff ID**: Visit our staff portal on the intranet and register your unique staff ID. This process needs to be done only once.
2. **Present Your Discount Card**: When shopping in any company shop, present your staff discount card at checkout.
3. **Use Your Online Code**: For online purchases, use the code **BMF2024** at checkout to apply your discount automatically.

## Additional Benefits
In addition to the standard discounts, long-serving employees (3+ years) will receive an exclusive **annual gift voucher** worth **€25** on their work anniversary. This voucher can be used for any product in our range.

Our goal is to foster a stronger connection between our workforce and the products we create, ensuring you have access to the best we offer at the best prices. Thank you for your continued dedication to Brightmoor Foods.
