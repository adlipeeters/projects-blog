**Internal Communications Guide**

Internal reference code: CNRY-39843bcb.

Effective from **January 15, 2025**, this guide outlines the protocols for all internal communications within Brightmoor Foods. Adherence to these guidelines ensures consistency, clarity, and security across all departments.

## Email Protocols
All official communications must be conducted via the Brightmoor Foods email system (**@brightmoorfoods.eu**). Emails should have a clear subject line, such as "ACTION REQUIRED: Q2 Budget Submission by Feb 10". All emails must be signed off by the respective department head. A maximum of **250 words per email** is recommended for clarity. Attachments larger than **5MB** require prior approval from the IT department.

## Meeting Notifications
All scheduled meetings, regardless of size, must be entered into the company’s calendar system, **BrightCalendar**. Meeting invites should be sent **at least 48 hours in advance** and must include:
- Agenda items
- Expected duration
- Required attendees
- Virtual/physical location

Meetings are limited to **1.5 hours** unless approved by the senior management team. A post-meeting summary email, approved by the meeting organizer, must be sent within **24 hours** of the meeting’s conclusion.

## Report Submission
All departmental reports are due on the **last Friday of each month**. Reports must be submitted in PDF format and named accordingly: **[Department]_MonthlyReport_[Month]-[Year].pdf**. For example: **Marketing_MonthlyReport_February-2025.pdf**.

Reports should include:
- Key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Achievements and challenges of the month
- Action items for the next month

All reports must be verified by the department head before submission. Late submissions will result in a review by the Quality Assurance team.

## Confidentiality and Security
All communications must adhere to our **Data Security Policy**. Sensitive information, including financial data, employee records, and proprietary formulas, must be encrypted and labeled **"CONFIDENTIAL"**. Any suspected data breach must be reported immediately to the **Cybersecurity Team** at **security@brightmoorfoods.eu**.

Remember, clear and timely communication is vital to the success of Brightmoor Foods. Thank you for your cooperation.
