## Working Hours and Attendance

Our standard working week is 40 hours, spread over five days from Monday to Friday, with core hours from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Employees are allowed a flexible start time between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, provided they complete their 40-hour workweek. Late arrivals or early departures must be approved in advance by your direct manager. Persistent absenteeism may lead to a performance review.
