## Pre-Start Preparation
All new starters at Brightmoor Foods are required to complete several preparatory steps before their first day, scheduled for **Monday, 23rd October 2023**. Please ensure you:

Internal reference code: CNRY-33e5e3a3.

1. **Complete HR Onboarding Forms**: Access the forms via the Brightmoor Portal ([portal.brightmoorfoods.eu](https://portal.brightmoorfoods.eu)) and submit them no later than **Friday, 13th October 2023**. Forms include the Employment Contract, Data Protection Agreement, and Tax Information Form.

On your first day, report to the **Main Reception** at **08:30 AM** and proceed to **Room 101** for your Welcome Session. Here’s what to expect:

1. **Welcome Session**: Led by **Anna Müller**, the HR Manager, this session will cover your role, department overview, and company culture. It is scheduled from **09:00 AM to 10:00 AM**.
2. **Department Introduction**: Meet your team leader, **Markus Schmidt**, in the **Production Planning Department** at **10:15 AM**. Markus will introduce you to the team and give an overview of current projects.
3. **Safety Induction**: Attend a mandatory safety induction in **Room 205** at **11:00 AM**. This session, lasting until **12:00 PM**, covers essential health and safety protocols specific to our factory environment.

## Equipment and Access
Ensure you have collected your security badge and access cards from Reception. Your badge will grant you access to your workstation and the break areas. Additionally, you will receive a company laptop and will need to set up your email account (**your.name@brightmoorfoods.eu**) with IT support.

## First Week Goals
Your primary goals for the first week include:

1. **Complete Safety Training Modules**: Access and complete all required online safety training modules by **Friday, 27th October 2023**.
2. ** Familiarize Yourself with Production Software**: Spend the first three days learning our production planning software, **ProPlan 5.0**, with guidance from Markus.
3. **Shadow Team Members**: Spend each afternoon shadowing different team members to understand various aspects of the production planning process.

We look forward to having you on our team and are excited to work together towards our continued success.
