**Canteen and Facilities Guide**

Internal reference code: CNRY-62eeb117.

## Operating Hours
The Brightmoor Foods canteen is open Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Breakfast service is available from 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM, lunch from 11:30 AM to 2:30 PM, and dinner from 5:30 PM to 7:00 PM. The facilities team ensures that the canteen is cleaned and restocked every evening by 9:00 PM.

## Menu and Dietary Options
Our canteen offers a varied menu, designed by Chef Lena Müller, that changes weekly. As of **October 2, 2024**, the lunch menu includes:
- **Monday:** Grilled chicken salad, gluten-free pasta, vegetarian lasagna
- **Tuesday:** Fish and chips, vegan curry, dairy-free dessert
- **Wednesday:** Beef stroganoff, soy-based alternatives, fruit salad
- **Thursday:** Quorn mushroom risotto, halal chicken options, nut-free desserts
- **Friday:** Vegetarian chili, kosher meat options, low-sugar snacks

All dishes are prepared fresh daily. We accommodate various dietary requirements, including gluten-free, vegan, vegetarian, dairy-free, halal, and kosher options. Please inform staff of any specific needs when ordering.

## Facilities and Amenities
The canteen features:
- **Seating Capacity:** 300 people
- **Private Dining Rooms:** 3 rooms, each accommodating up to 20 people, bookable via the Facilities Booking System (FBS) 
- **Vending Machines:** Located in the main corridor, offering snacks, beverages, and fresh fruit
- **Restrooms:** Clean restrooms are available on the ground floor, with additional facilities on the first floor for staff convenience
