## Introduction

Internal reference code: CNRY-8583c7fa.

This guide outlines the procedures and policies for booking business travel for Brightmoor Foods employees. Effective from **1st June 2024**, these guidelines ensure consistency, cost-efficiency, and compliance with company policies.

## Booking Process

1. **Authorization**: All travel requests must be approved by your direct manager via the **Travel Request Form** in the HR portal. Approval should be obtained at least **10 working days** before the planned departure date.

2. **Travel Booking Platform**: Use the company’s designated travel booking platform, **TravelPro EU**. Employee credentials have already been set up by the Finance Department. For login issues, contact **IT Support** at **it.support@brightmoorfoods.eu**.

3. **Flight Bookings**:
    - Prefer economy class unless business class is justified and pre-approved. The threshold for business class approval is when the time saved equals **or exceeds 3 hours** of the employee’s daily rate.
    - Book flights through **TravelPro EU**. The system will automatically apply the company’s negotiated rates with **AirLoom Airways** and **SkyLink Airlines**.

4. **Hotel Bookings**:
    - Use **TravelPro EU** for hotel reservations. The company has negotiated rates with preferred hotel chains **ComfortStay** and **GrandView Hotels**.
    - All hotel bookings must adhere to the **per-diem allowance** of **€120 per night**. Exceptions require prior written approval from the **Travel Committee**.

## Expense Reimbursement

1. **Documentation**: Keep all receipts and fill out the **Expense Report Form** within **7 days** of returning from travel. Receipts over **€50** must be itemized.

2. **Submission**: Submit completed forms and receipts through the **Finance Portal**. Allow **5 working days** for processing.

3. **Policy Compliance**: Non-compliance with these guidelines may result in the employee being required to reimburse the company for the difference between the approved rate and actual expenses.
