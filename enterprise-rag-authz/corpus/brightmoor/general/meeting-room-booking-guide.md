# Meeting Room Booking Guide

Internal reference code: CNRY-1530aa7f.

## Overview

This guide provides essential information for booking meeting rooms at Brightmoor Foods. Our facilities include **four main meeting rooms**: Apollo (capacity: 10), Brutus (capacity: 20), Ceres (capacity: 15), and Draco (capacity: 25). All bookings must be made through the internal booking system, **ResoluteApp**, available on the company intranet.

## Booking Process

1. **Log in** to ResoluteApp using your employee credentials.
2. Navigate to the **“Meeting Rooms”** tab.
3. Select the desired room, date, and time slot. Available time slots are **09:00–10:00, 10:00–11:00, 11:00–12:00, 13:00–14:00, 14:00–15:00, and 15:00–16:00**.
4. Add any **specific requirements** (e.g., projector, catering) in the comments section.
5. Click **“Confirm Booking”**. You will receive a confirmation email.

**Important:** Bookings can be made **up to three months** in advance but must be made **at least 48 hours** before the meeting.

## Policies and Guidelines

- **Cancellations:** If you need to cancel, do so via ResoluteApp at least **24 hours** before the meeting to avoid a charge of **€50**.
- **No-Show Policy:** A no-show without cancellation will result in a charge of **€100** and may affect future booking privileges.
- **Room Usage:** Meetings should start and end within the booked time slot. If a meeting runs over, the room will be reclaimed after **15 minutes** of overtime without notice.

For any issues or questions regarding room bookings, please contact our Facilities Management team:
- **Email:** facilities@brightmoorfoods.eu

- **Office Hours:** 08:00–17:00, Monday–Friday

Please adhere to these guidelines to ensure a smooth and efficient meeting experience for all employees.
