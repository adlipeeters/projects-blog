Internal reference code: CNRY-41a9f447.

## Introduction

At Brightmoor Foods, ensuring a safe working environment is our top priority. This document outlines the fundamental health and safety protocols we follow across all our facilities. Compliance with these guidelines not only protects our employees but also reinforces our commitment to quality and legal standards.

## Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

All employees must wear appropriate PPE as per their job role. This includes:

- **Hair Nets and Beards Covers:** Mandatory for all food handling staff to prevent contamination.
- **Non-Slip Shoes:** Required in all production areas to prevent slips and falls. All employees must have shoes approved by the Safety Office.
- **Safety Glasses:** Required in packaging and machinery areas. All safety glasses must be inspected monthly and replaced if damaged.

Failure to comply with PPE requirements will result in a warning, followed by suspension if non-compliance continues. The last audit on **March 15, 2024**, showed a **15%** increase in PPE compliance compared to the previous quarter.

## Emergency Procedures

In the event of an emergency, follow these steps:

1. **Fire:** Activate the nearest fire alarm, use the designated fire exits, and gather at the emergency assembly point. Fire drills will be conducted quarterly, with the next one scheduled for **May 10, 2024**.
2. **Chemical Spill:** Evacuate the area, alert the Safety Office, and use the spill kits located in each department. Report all incidents to **Janek Kowalski**, our Health and Safety Officer.
3. **Medical Emergency:** Call **+48 22 000 1234**, our 24/7 medical hotline, and administer first aid if trained. Designate one team member to escort the individual to the infirmary.

## Reporting Accidents

All accidents, no matter how minor, must be reported within **24 hours**. Use the accident report form available on the intranet or ask **Anita Nowak** in Human Resources for a paper copy. Reports are reviewed monthly by the safety committee, chaired by **Janek Kowalski**.

## Training and Inspections

Regular training sessions are scheduled to keep our team updated on the latest safety protocols. The next training session on **manual handling techniques** is set for **April 22, 2024**. Facility inspections are conducted bi-monthly, with the next one scheduled for **April 30, 2024**. Areas with recurring issues will receive targeted training and additional oversight.

Adhering to these health and safety basics not only keeps us safe but also ensures Brightmoor Foods remains a leader in quality and responsibility within the industry.
