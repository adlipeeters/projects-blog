### Parking Regulations at Brightmoor Foods

Internal reference code: CNRY-4d6c0fb6.

Effective from **January 1, 2024**, Brightmoor Foods has updated its parking rules to improve efficiency and safety around our main plant and administrative offices located at **Strandweg 42, Rotterdam**. These rules apply to all employees, contractors, and visitors.

### Permitted Parking Zones
Our facility features several designated parking zones:

- **Zone A**: Reserved for senior management and department heads. This includes individuals with titles of **Director** or above.
- **Zone B**: General employee parking. All permanent staff must use this area unless they have a valid reservation in another zone.
- **Zone C**: Visitor parking. This zone is strictly for short-term visits and must be vacated within **2 hours**.
- **Zone D**: Disabled parking. Accessible only to vehicles displaying a valid European disability badge.

### Parking Permits and Registration
All employees must register their vehicles with the **HR Department** by **January 15, 2024**, to receive a parking permit. Permits cost **€50 per quarter** and are valid for the specified period. Permits should be displayed clearly on the vehicle’s dashboard. Failure to display a valid permit may result in a **€75 fine** and towing of the vehicle.

### Enforcement and Penalties
Security personnel will patrol parking areas **24/7**. Violations will be addressed as follows:

- **First Offense**: A warning and a **€75 administrative fee**.
- **Second Offense**: A **€150 fine** and temporary parking ban for **one week**.
- **Third Offense**: A **€250 fine** and permanent loss of parking privileges.

Additionally, unauthorized parking in **Zone A** will result in immediate towing at the owner’s expense, starting from **January 1, 2024**.

We thank you for your cooperation in maintaining a safe and organized workplace.
