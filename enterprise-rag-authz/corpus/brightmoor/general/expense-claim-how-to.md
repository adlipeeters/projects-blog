**Expense Claim How To**

Internal reference code: CNRY-491aad55.

## Submitting Your Claim

To submit an expense claim at Brightmoor Foods, follow these steps:

1. **Gather Documentation**: Collect all original receipts for expenses. This includes travel, accommodation, meals, and any other claimable expenses. Ensure receipts are dated within the claim period and clearly show the amount, date, and vendor.

2. **Use the Correct Form**: Access the expense claim form via the Brightmoor Foods intranet under the “Finance” section. For the period ending **15 October 2024**, use **Form ECF-2024-10**. Forms are valid for a single month and must be submitted by the **20th** of the following month. For example, claims for September 2024 should be submitted by **20 October 2024**.

3. **Enter Your Details**: Complete sections A and B of the form with your full name, employee ID, department (e.g., **Marketing**), and the claim period dates. Ensure all information matches your employee records to avoid processing delays.

4. **Itemise Your Expenses**: In section C, list each expense with a brief description, date, amount, and attach the corresponding receipt. For travel expenses, include mileage logs if driving a company or personal vehicle. Use the company’s standard rate of **0.45 EUR per mile** for personal vehicle claims.

## Approved Expenses

The following expenses are approved for claim:

- **Travel**: Flights, train, and bus fares. For flights, only economy class is reimbursable unless approved in advance by your manager.
- **Accommodation**: Hotel stays at **3-star** or below. For stays exceeding **150 EUR per night**, prior approval from Finance is required.
- **Meals**: Breakfast, lunch, and dinner while travelling. The daily allowance is **25 EUR** per meal. For expenses exceeding this allowance, provide a detailed justification and receipt.
- **Conference Fees**: Registration fees for industry conferences approved by your department head. For the **2024 European Food Innovation Summit**, claims are capped at **500 EUR** per employee.

## Submitting and Following Up

1. **Submit Your Claim**: Email the completed form and attached receipts to **finance@brightmoorfoods.eu**. Use the subject line “Expense Claim – [Your Name] – [Claim Period]”. For example: “Expense Claim – Anna Schmidt – September 2024”. Claims received after the deadline will be returned for resubmission.

2. **Processing Time**: Claims are processed within **10 business days** of receipt. You will receive an email confirmation once your claim is received and another once it has been processed. If you haven’t heard back within **15 business days**, contact **Finance Team Lead, Marco Bianchi** at **m.bianchi@brightmoorfoods.eu**.

3. **Discrepancies**: If your claim is rejected or partially approved, you will receive an explanation via email. You have **7 days** to respond with additional documentation or clarification.

Adhering to these guidelines ensures a smooth and timely reimbursement process.
