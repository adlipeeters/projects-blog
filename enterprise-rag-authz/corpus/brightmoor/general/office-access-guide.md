This document outlines the access protocols for all Brightmoor Foods office locations. Please adhere to these guidelines to ensure security and efficiency.

## Office Entry Procedures

All employees must use their RFID access cards to enter and exit the main building entrances. The cards must be tapped on the designated reader located next to each entry and exit point. Access is granted from 07:00 to 19:00 Monday through Friday, with after-hours access requiring prior approval from your direct manager.

## Secure Areas

Certain areas, including the Research & Development lab (Building B, Level 3) and the Financial Records room (Building A, Level 1), have enhanced security measures. Access to these areas requires a secondary biometric scan (fingerprint or retina) in addition to your RFID card. All employees seeking access to these areas must submit a request form to the Security Office at least 48 hours in advance.

## Emergency Access

In case of an emergency, such as a fire or power outage, use the emergency access protocol: dial #77 from any internal phone or mobile device registered with the company. Security will respond within 5 minutes to guide you through the evacuation process. Remember, emergency exits are located at the north and south ends of each building and must be accessible at all times.

## Visitor Access

Visitors must report to the main reception desk upon arrival. They will be issued a temporary access badge, valid for the duration of their visit, and must be accompanied by an employee at all times. Visitor access is restricted to common areas and does not include secure or sensitive zones. All visitors must sign in and out, with a log kept for security review.

**Contact:** For any questions regarding access protocols, please contact the Security Office at extension 5555 or security@brightmoorfoods.com.

**Reminder:** All access protocols are subject to change. Employees will be notified of any updates via internal memos and the company intranet.
