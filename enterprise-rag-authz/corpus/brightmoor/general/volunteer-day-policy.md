**Volunteer Day Policy**

Internal reference code: CNRY-4a8d6895.

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to outline Brightmoor Foods' commitment to community engagement and to provide clear guidelines for employees participating in volunteer activities. Our goal is to foster a culture of giving back and to support local communities across Europe.

## Eligibility and Allocation

All full-time employees who have completed six months of service are eligible for one paid volunteer day per calendar year. Part-time employees working a minimum of 20 hours per week are also eligible after the same qualifying period. Volunteer days must be scheduled with at least two weeks' notice and approved by your direct manager.

## Approved Activities and Guidelines

Volunteer activities must align with Brightmoor Foods’ values of education, nutrition, environmental sustainability, and community support. Examples include:

- Assisting local food banks or soup kitchens
- Participating in educational programs about healthy eating
- Engaging in environmental clean-up projects
- Supporting senior citizen centers

Employees are permitted up to four hours of volunteer time per day. All volunteer activities must comply with local laws and regulations, and employees must not represent Brightmoor Foods in any official capacity unless pre-approved by the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) department.

## Recognition and Support

To recognize and support our employees' volunteer efforts, Brightmoor Foods will:

- Feature volunteer highlights in our monthly internal newsletter, *The Brightmoor Beacon*.
- Provide a stipend of €25 for approved volunteer activities to cover incidental expenses such as transportation or materials.
- Organize an annual Volunteer Appreciation event in December, where top volunteers will be awarded with certificates and small tokens of appreciation.

This policy, effective from **January 1, 2025**, aims to encourage our workforce to engage meaningfully with our communities, reinforcing our dedication to making a positive impact.
