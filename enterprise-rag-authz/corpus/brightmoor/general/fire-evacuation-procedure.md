## Fire Evacuation Overview

In the event of a fire, the safety of all employees is our top priority. Brightmoor Foods has established a comprehensive fire evacuation procedure to ensure prompt and orderly evacuation. This document outlines the steps to be followed during a fire emergency.

## Emergency Response Team

The Fire Emergency Response Team (FERT) is responsible for coordinating the evacuation. The team includes:

- **Alice Henderson**, Facility Safety Officer
- **Marko Davidovic**, Assistant Safety Officer
- **Lara Schmidt**, First Aid Coordinator

The FERT will activate the fire alarm, guide employees to exits, and ensure all areas are cleared.

## Evacuation Procedure

1. **Alerting Everyone**: Upon discovering a fire, immediately activate the nearest fire alarm. This will trigger the building-wide alarm system.
   
2. **Notify FERT**: Once the alarm is activated, notify a member of the FERT. They will confirm the alarm and begin coordination.

3. **Evacuate**: Proceed calmly to the nearest designated assembly point. Use the stairwells if elevators are unavailable due to power failure. Avoid using elevators unless instructed by FERT.

4. **Assembly Points**: Employees should gather at one of the following assembly points:
   - **North Parking Lot** (Primary, capacity: 500 people)
   - **South Courtyard** (Secondary, capacity: 300 people)

5. **Headcount**: FERT members will conduct a headcount at the assembly points to ensure all employees are accounted for.

6. **Do Not Re-enter**: Do not re-enter the building until cleared by the FERT and local fire department.

## Post-Evacuation Procedures

After evacuation, the following steps will be taken:

- **Headcount Verification**: FERT will verify the attendance of all employees.
- **Communication**: The Human Resources Department will contact family members to confirm the safety of our employees.
- **Debriefing**: A debriefing session will be held with all staff and FERT to review the evacuation process and identify areas for improvement.

This procedure will be reviewed annually and updated as necessary. All employees must complete a fire safety training session every year, with the next session scheduled for **October 15, 2025**.
