## Overview

This document outlines the exceptions to Brightmoor Foods’ Anti-Bribery Policy, effective from January 15, 2025. These exceptions are strictly limited and apply only under specific, pre-approved circumstances to ensure compliance with both European Union regulations and our corporate ethical standards.

## Exceptional Allowances

1. **Trade Show Expenses (Up to €5,000 per event):** Brightmoor Foods permits the allocation of funds for booth rentals, promotional materials, and hospitality at internationally recognized trade shows, provided these expenses are pre-approved by the Corporate Compliance Office. All receipts must be submitted within 30 days post-event for reimbursement.

2. **Charitable Contributions (Up to €10,000 annually per region):** The company allows donations to registered charities and non-profit organizations that align with our Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) objectives. Such contributions must be approved by the CSR Committee and documented with a formal donation agreement.

3. **Government Compliance Training (Up to €7,500 per workshop):** Participation in mandatory regulatory compliance workshops hosted by EU governmental bodies is permitted. These workshops must be directly related to food safety or labeling regulations. Pre-approval from the Legal Department is required, and all related expenses must adhere to the Government Training Expense Form.

## Approval Process

All requests for the above exceptions must be submitted at least 30 days in advance using the Exception Request Form available on the intranet. The Legal Department will review submissions and provide approval or denial within 14 days. Approved requests will be logged in the Compliance Tracking System, and any deviation from approved amounts or purposes will be considered a policy violation.

## Monitoring and Reporting

The Compliance Office will conduct quarterly audits of all exception expenditures. Any findings of misuse or non-compliance will be reported to the Ethics Committee, which may impose disciplinary actions, including termination of employment. All employees are required to attend an annual refresher course on the Anti-Bribery Policy and its exceptions.
