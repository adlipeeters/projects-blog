**Records Retention Schedule**

Internal reference code: CNRY-7d9357a8.

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024

**Review Cycle:** Annual

This document outlines the retention periods for various categories of records maintained by Brightmoor Foods, ensuring compliance with European Union regulations, industry best practices, and internal policies.

## Contractual Records

Records related to contracts with suppliers, customers, and partners must be retained for **10 years** from the date of contract expiration. This includes all correspondence, amendments, and related documentation. Contracts signed on or after January 1, 2024, are subject to this schedule.

## Financial Records

All financial records, including invoices, receipts, bank statements, and audit reports, must be kept for **7 years** from the date of the transaction. This aligns with EU tax regulations and internal audit requirements. Financial records from fiscal year 2023 must be preserved until at least December 31, 2029.

## Employee Records

Employee records, such as hire letters, performance reviews, disciplinary actions, and termination documents, should be retained for **5 years** after the employee's departure from the company. For employees who left Brightmoor Foods in 2023, their records must be kept until at least December 31, 2028. However, records related to compliance with labor laws must be kept for **10 years**.

## Product Development and Quality Assurance Records

Records pertaining to product development, including research notes, formulation changes, testing results, and quality assurance reports, must be archived for **15 years**. This period ensures compliance with food safety regulations and facilitates reference during product recalls or regulatory reviews. Records from products launched before January 1, 2024, must be kept until January 1, 2039.

**Note:** Exceptions to these retention periods may apply in cases of ongoing legal disputes, regulatory investigations, or other specific requirements. In such cases, records must be retained until resolution or until a specified date determined by legal counsel.
