## **Current Liability Caps Overview**

Internal reference code: CNRY-f9221d86.

As of Q2 2024, Brightmoor Foods has implemented a standardized approach to customer liability caps across all product lines to mitigate potential financial risks. The current cap for consumer product liability claims is set at **€2 million** per incident. This limit applies to all claims arising from product defects, contamination, or misuse, provided the claim is filed within the statutory period of limitations (three years from the date of discovery).

For commercial clients, the liability cap is higher, set at **€5 million** per incident, reflecting the larger scale of transactions and potential economic impact. These caps are designed to balance risk management with maintaining competitive pricing and customer trust.

## **Implementation Timeline and Procedures**

The new liability caps were officially rolled out on **April 15, 2024**. All relevant departments, including Sales, Customer Service, and Legal, have been provided with updated protocols and training materials. Claims exceeding these caps must be escalated to the Legal Department within **48 hours** of notification. The Legal Team, led by **Clara Mertens, Senior Legal Counsel**, will assess the claim’s validity and determine the appropriate course of action, which may include negotiation, settlement, or litigation.

## **Impact on Financial Projections**

Implementing these liability caps is expected to reduce potential exposure by approximately **30%**. According to our risk assessment model, this change could save the company an estimated **€1.2 million annually** in potential settlements and legal fees. The Finance Department has already adjusted the 2024-2026 financial forecasts to reflect these anticipated savings, enhancing our overall risk management framework.

## **Training and Compliance**

To ensure compliance, a mandatory training session for all customer-facing staff will be held on **May 10, 2024**. This session, facilitated by Clara Mertens, will cover the specifics of the new liability caps, reporting procedures, and best practices for handling claims. Attendance records will be maintained, and all employees must acknowledge receipt and understanding of the new policy by **May 17, 2024**. Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action.
