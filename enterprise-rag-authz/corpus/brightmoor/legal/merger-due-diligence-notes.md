### Financial Review

Internal reference code: CNRY-8a6f5062.

As of the due diligence review conducted on 15 May 2024, the following financial observations have been made regarding the potential merger with **Schneider & Weber AG**:

- **Annual Revenue**: Schneider & Weber AG reported a revenue of **€1.2 billion** for the fiscal year 2023, showing a steady growth rate of **4.5%** year-over-year.
- **Debt Levels**: Current debt stands at **€350 million**, which translates to a debt-to-equity ratio of **0.58**. This is considered healthy and manageable.

The company has a solid track record with regulatory bodies:

- **Environmental Regulations**: Compliant with **REACH** and **EMAS** regulations. They have invested **€2 million** in eco-friendly packaging solutions in 2023.

### Human Resources Overview

- **Employee Count**: **850** full-time employees across their three main facilities in Germany and Austria.
