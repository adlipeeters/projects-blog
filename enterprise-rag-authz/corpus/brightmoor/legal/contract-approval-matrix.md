### **Contract Approval Matrix Overview**

Internal reference code: CNRY-487fb317.

As of Q2 2024, Brightmoor Foods has standardized its contract approval process to ensure compliance, risk mitigation, and efficient execution across all departments. This matrix outlines the necessary approvals for various contract types and values.

### **Approval Authorities**

1. **Contract Value Below €50,000**
   - **Approver**: Department Head
   - **Example**: Purchase of packaging materials from Klein & Sohn GmbH valued at €35,000.

2. **Contract Value Between €50,000 and €200,000**
   - **Approvers**: Department Head and Finance Director
   - **Example**: Supply agreement with Del Monte Europe for organic canned vegetables totaling €125,000 over 12 months.

3. **Contract Value Between €200,000 and €500,000**
   - **Approvers**: Department Head, Finance Director, and Legal Counsel
   - **Example**: Long-term distribution contract with EuroNet Logistics for €350,000 annually.

4. **Contract Value Above €500,000**
   - **Approvers**: Department Head, Finance Director, Legal Counsel, and Executive Board
   - **Example**: Strategic partnership with Nova Foods Ltd for €750,000 over two years.

### **Specialized Contract Types**

1. **International Contracts**
   - **Additional Approver**: Compliance Officer
   - **Example**: Export agreement with Sahara Imports LLC in Algeria worth €600,000, requiring adherence to international trade laws.

2. **Intellectual Property Agreements**
   - **Additional Approver**: Head of R&D
   - **Example**: Licensing agreement with BioTech Innovations for a proprietary fermentation process, valued at €400,000.

### **Timeline for Approvals**

All contracts must be approved no later than **10 business days** prior to signing. For contracts requiring Executive Board approval, the timeline extends to **15 business days**. Failure to meet these timelines may result in delays in project initiation and potential financial penalties.

**Note**: All approvals must be documented in the Contract Management System (CMS) with a unique reference number, such as **CM-2024-057**. The current version of this matrix is effective from **June 1, 2024**.
