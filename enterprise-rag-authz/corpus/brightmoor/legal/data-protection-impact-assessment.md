## Introduction

This Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) evaluates the potential risks associated with the new customer loyalty program, "BrightRewards," launched on 2025-02-01. The program collects extensive customer data to personalize offers and improve customer engagement. This assessment ensures compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and identifies mitigating actions to protect customer privacy.

## Data Collection and Processing

**Data Collected:**
- **Personal Identification:** Name, email address, phone number
- **Transactional Data:** Purchase history, loyalty points, redemption details
- **Behavioral Data:** Browsing habits, preferences, feedback

**Data Processing Purposes:**
- Personalizing marketing offers
- Analyzing customer behavior to improve product development
- Enhancing customer service experiences

**Data Storage:** All data is stored on encrypted servers within the EU. Backup copies are retained for 7 years in compliance with data retention policies.

## Risk Assessment

**Identified Risks:**
1. **Unauthorized Access:** Potential breach due to inadequate security measures.
2. **Data Misuse:** Incorrect or excessive use of data by internal teams.
3. **Data Retention:** Risk of retaining data longer than necessary.

**Risk Mitigation Measures:**
- **Enhanced Security Protocols:** Implementation of multi-factor authentication and advanced encryption by Q2 2025.
- **Data Minimization Policy:** Regular audits to ensure only necessary data is collected and retained. Policy to be reviewed quarterly.
- **Training Programs:** Mandatory GDPR training for all staff handling customer data, starting on 2025-04-01.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

The DPIA concludes that while the BrightRewards program offers significant business benefits, appropriate safeguards must be implemented to mitigate identified risks. The Legal Department will coordinate with IT and Marketing to ensure all mitigation measures are in place by the end of Q2 2025. A follow-up audit will be conducted on 2025-06-30 to assess the effectiveness of these measures.
