# Regulatory Inspection Response

Internal reference code: CNRY-56ebe382.

## Inspection Overview

On June 15, 2024, Brightmoor Foods underwent a routine inspection by the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA). The inspection was conducted at our main production facility in Antwerp, Belgium, and focused on compliance with EU Regulation 852/2004 on hygiene in the food chain.

The inspection revealed several non-conformities, primarily related to documentation and procedural adherence. Key issues included:

1. **Temperature Logging Gaps**: There were gaps in the temperature logs for cold storage units between May 20 and May 25, 2024.
2. **Labeling Discrepancies**: Eight batches of product BF-2024-078 (Savory Snack Mix) had labeling discrepancies regarding allergen information.
3. **Sanitation Procedures**: Inconsistent application of sanitation procedures in the packaging area, specifically during shift changes.

## Action Plan

To address these issues, the following corrective actions have been implemented:

1. **Temperature Logging**: Implemented a digital temperature monitoring system effective July 1, 2024, with real-time alerts for any deviations. All staff have been retrained on logging procedures.
2. **Labeling Compliance**: Revised the labeling process to include a secondary verification step, effective immediately. All affected batches have been recalled, with a total of 1,200 kg of product returned by July 10, 2024.
3. **Sanitation Protocols**: Updated sanitation standard operating procedures (SOPs) and conducted additional training sessions for all packaging line workers. Daily audits will be conducted to ensure compliance.

## Timeline and Responsibility

The following timeline outlines key milestones for resolving these issues:

- **July 1, 2024**: Implementation of digital temperature monitoring system.
- **July 5, 2024**: Completion of labeling process revision and staff training.
- **July 10, 2024**: Finalization of product recall and verification of corrected labels.
- **July 15, 2024**: Completion of updated sanitation SOP training and first round of daily audits.

Responsibility for these actions is assigned as follows:
- **Temperature Logging**: Operations Department, led by Manager Laura Jensen.
- **Labeling Compliance**: Quality Assurance Department, led by Supervisor Mark van der Meer.
- **Sanitation Protocols**: Maintenance Department, led by Supervisor Elena Petrov.

## Follow-Up Inspection

EFSA has scheduled a follow-up inspection for September 15, 2024, to assess the effectiveness of the implemented corrective actions. A comprehensive report will be prepared and submitted to EFSA by August 1, 2024.
