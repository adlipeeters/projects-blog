## **Litigation Update**

The board was updated on ongoing litigation matters. The primary case, **Case No. 2025-CIV-04578**, concerning a trademark dispute with **Alpine Delights GmbH**, remains in preliminary stages. The estimated legal costs for Q2 2025 have been revised to **€220,000**, up from the initial projection of **€180,000**. The legal team anticipates a court hearing scheduled for **2025-06-10**.

## **Compliance Audit Findings**

A recent internal compliance audit revealed several areas requiring attention:

1. **Data Protection:** The audit identified non-compliance with GDPR regulations in our customer data management system. Remedial actions are estimated to cost **€75,000** and must be completed by **2025-05-31**.
2. **Supplier Contracts:** Several contracts with small-scale suppliers were found to lack proper force majeure clauses. The legal team recommends revising these contracts, with an estimated cost of **€45,000**.

## **Intellectual Property Strategy**

The board discussed our intellectual property strategy for 2025. Key initiatives include:

1. **Trademark Renewals:** All trademark renewals for existing product lines are scheduled for Q3 2025, with a budget of **€30,000**.
2. **New Product Patents:** We have initiated the patent application process for three new product formulations. The estimated cost for these applications is **€60,000**, with expected completion by **2025-09-15**.

## **Insurance Review**

The board reviewed our current insurance portfolio. The annual premium for our comprehensive product liability insurance has increased by **5%** to **€150,000** for the 2025 coverage year. Additionally, we have secured an additional **€50,000** in cyber liability coverage, bringing the total cyber insurance premium to **€70,000**.
