**Lease Renewal Negotiation for Facility B-12**

Internal reference code: CNRY-de7caec2.

We are currently engaged in negotiations to renew the lease for our production facility at B-12, Dresden, which expires on **30 September 2024**. The current lease, initiated in **2015**, has been instrumental in supporting our operations in the region.

## Current Lease Terms
The existing lease term is for **10 years** with an annual rent of **€450,000**, reviewed every **two years**. The most recent review in **2021** resulted in an increase to the current rate. The landlord, **Dresden Industrial Properties GmbH**, has maintained the property in good condition, and we have enjoyed stable operations throughout the lease period.

## Negotiation Strategy
Our primary objectives for the renewal are to secure a **lengthy term** to ensure operational stability and to **minimise rental cost increases**. Proposed terms are as follows:

- **Lease Term**: We are proposing a **15-year renewal term** to provide long-term security and potentially qualify for rent stabilisation clauses.
- **Annual Rent**: Our initial offer is **€420,000** for the first five years, with incremental increases of **€20,000** every subsequent five years. This amounts to **€440,000** for years 6-10 and **€460,000** for years 11-15.
- **Rent Review Schedule**: We propose shifting to **five-yearly reviews** instead of the current two-year schedule to provide greater predictability.

## Next Steps
We have scheduled our next meeting with the landlord on **15 July 2024**. Key points for discussion will include:

- **Landlord’s Counter-Proposals**: We anticipate the landlord may counter with a higher initial rent or more frequent reviews. Be prepared to negotiate these points.
- **Incentives for Long-Term Commitment**: We will explore potential incentives, such as rent discounts or property improvements, for committing to a longer term.
- **Legal and Financial Review**: Post-negotiation, the Legal Department will conduct a thorough review, and Finance will assess the impact on our budgetary projections.
