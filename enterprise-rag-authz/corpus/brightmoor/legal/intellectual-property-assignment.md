**Subject:** Intellectual Property Assignment Protocol

## Introduction

This document outlines the procedures for the assignment of intellectual property (IP) rights to Brightmoor Foods. Effective immediately, all IP created by employees in the course of their employment must be assigned to the company. This ensures that Brightmoor Foods retains full ownership and control over valuable assets, including patents, trademarks, copyrights, and trade secrets.

## IP Assignment Process

1. **Notification:** Employees must notify their direct supervisor and the Legal Department within **five business days** of creating any potential IP. The notification should include a detailed description of the IP, the date of creation, and the employee’s name and department.

2. **Documentation:** The Legal Department will provide an **IP Assignment Form** (Form IP-2024-A) to the employee. The form must be completed and returned within **10 business days**. The form includes sections for the employee’s signature, confirming the voluntary assignment of IP rights to Brightmoor Foods.

3. **Review and Approval:** The Legal Team will review the submitted form and associated documentation. If approved, the IP will be officially recorded in Brightmoor Foods’ IP registry. The registry currently contains **1,243 registered IP assets**, and we aim to increase this number by **8% annually**.

## Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure

All employees must maintain strict confidentiality regarding any IP, whether assigned to the company or not. Any breach of confidentiality may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. Additionally, employees are required to sign a **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)** annually. The current version of the NDA was last updated on **March 15, 2023** and is available on the company intranet under the Human Resources section.

## Consequences of Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with these procedures may result in legal consequences for the employee, including potential claims for damages by third parties. Brightmoor Foods reserves the right to take all necessary actions, including legal action, to protect its IP rights.

## Conclusion

Adherence to these IP assignment protocols is crucial for the continued success and innovation of Brightmoor Foods. Your cooperation is essential to ensure that our valuable IP assets are protected and utilized effectively.
