## Incident Overview

**Case ID:** WB-2023-045
**Date Reported:** 2023-10-15
**Reporter:** Anna Müller, Quality Control Specialist, Munich Plant
**Date of Alleged Incident:** 2023-09-20

Anna Müller reported a suspected case of data manipulation regarding product expiration dates. The alleged incident took place during the routine quality check on September 20, 2023. Anna claims that she observed a colleague, Johannes Bauer, altering the expiration dates on several batches of canned vegetable medleys from "2024-05-30" to "2025-05-30".

## Investigation Details

An internal investigation was initiated on October 16, 2023, led by the Legal Department in conjunction with the Internal Audit Team. The investigation included a review of digital logs, CCTV footage, and interviews with staff members involved.

Key findings from the investigation include:

- **Digital Logs:** Examination of the digital logs from the production line revealed irregular activity on September 20, 2023, between 14:00 and 16:00. The logs showed multiple manual overrides of the expiration date entries.
- **CCTV Footage:** CCTV footage confirmed that Johannes Bauer accessed the production line’s computer system during the suspicious time frame. The footage also showed him interacting with another employee, Laura Schneider, who was on shift at the time.
- **Interviews:** Interviews with Anna Müller, Johannes Bauer, and Laura Schneider were conducted. Anna stood by her observation, Johannes admitted to altering the dates but claimed he was pressured by Laura, who allegedly told him it was a “one-time thing” to meet production targets. Laura denied giving any such instructions.

## Actions Taken

Based on the investigation’s findings, the following actions have been taken:

- **Disciplinary Action:** Johannes Bauer has been suspended pending further action. HR is considering disciplinary measures, which may include termination.
- **Policy Review:** The Legal Team has initiated a review of the company’s whistleblowing policy and procedures to ensure clarity and effectiveness. A revised policy is expected to be rolled out by December 1, 2023.
- **Training Sessions:** Mandatory ethics and compliance training sessions will be conducted for all employees by Q1 2024. The first session is scheduled for January 15, 2024.
- **Security Enhancements:** Additional security measures, including more frequent audits of digital logs and increased CCTV monitoring, will be implemented to prevent future incidents.

## Conclusion

This case underscores the importance of maintaining strict adherence to our quality and ethical standards. The Legal Department will continue to monitor the situation and ensure that all appropriate actions are taken to maintain the integrity of Brightmoor Foods.
