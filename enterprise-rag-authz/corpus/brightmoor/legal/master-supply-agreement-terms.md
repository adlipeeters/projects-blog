## Scope of Supply

Internal reference code: CNRY-e847723d.

This Master Supply Agreement (MSA), effective from **January 1, 2025**, between **Brightmoor Foods Ltd.** (hereinafter referred to as "Supplier") and **Alpine Retail Group** (hereinafter referred to as "Buyer"), outlines the terms under which the Supplier shall provide the Buyer with packaged food products. The agreement covers a minimum of **3 years**, renewable by mutual consent.

The primary product categories include **Frozen Vegetables, Ready-to-Eat Meals, and Baked Goods**. The Buyer reserves the right to request additional product lines with a **90-day notice period**. All products must comply with **EU Food Safety Regulations** and **ISO 22000:2018** standards.

## Pricing and Payment Terms

Pricing for the products will be determined on a **quarterly basis**, reviewed on **January 1st, April 1st, July 1st, and October 1st** each year. Prices will be based on the **average market cost of raw materials** over the preceding quarter, plus a **fixed margin of 15%** for the Supplier. Payment terms are **net 30 days** from the date of the invoice. Late payments will incur a **2% per month** late fee, compounded monthly.

## Delivery and Logistics

Delivery schedules will be agreed upon on a **monthly basis**, with the Buyer providing a **delivery schedule template**  **45 days** in advance. The Supplier is responsible for all **transportation costs** within the **EU region**. Delivery times are guaranteed within **48 hours** of the scheduled delivery date. Any delays exceeding **24 hours** will result in a **penalty of €500 per delayed pallet**.

## Termination and Renewal

Either party may terminate this agreement with **6 months written notice**. In the event of termination, the Supplier will be compensated for **all costs incurred** up to the termination date, including **any pre-ordered inventory**. The agreement may be renewed for an additional **2 years** at the mutual agreement of both parties, with terms to be renegotiated.
