## Case Overview

Internal reference code: CNRY-ecebfd70.

**Case ID:** ET-2023-045
**Claimant:** Anna Kovács, Former Quality Control Technician
**Respondent:** Brightmoor Foods Ltd.
**Date of Hearing:** 15th November 2023
**Tribunal Location:** Manchester Employment Tribunal

### Background
Anna Kovács was employed by Brightmoor Foods from 1st June 2019 until her termination on 20th March 2023. The claimant alleges constructive dismissal and harassment related to workplace conditions and perceived health and safety violations.

### Key Events
1. **Initial Concerns Raised:** On 12th July 2022, Kovács submitted a formal complaint regarding unsafe handling procedures in the packaging department, citing three specific incidents where protective gear was inadequate.
    
2. **Meeting with Management:** A meeting was scheduled on 25th July 2022 with David Miller, Head of Operations, and Susan Lee, HR Manager. The claimant claims that this meeting was dismissive and no actionable steps were taken.

3. **Escalation to HR:** On 10th October 2022, Kovács escalated her concerns in writing to HR, referencing Company Policy 7.2 on Health and Safety Protocols. She followed up with an email on 15th October, requesting a written response by 31st October.

4. **Performance Review:** A performance review on 5th January 2023 resulted in a downgrade of Kovács’ annual appraisal, cited as “failure to meet departmental standards.” The claimant argues this was retaliatory.

5. **Termination:** On 20th March 2023, Kovács was informed of her termination via email, effective immediately, with a severance package that excluded back pay and bonus entitlements.

### Tribunal Submissions
- **Claimant’s Position:** Kovács is represented by Jenkins & Associates. They argue that the company failed to address legitimate health and safety concerns, leading to a hostile work environment and ultimately constructive dismissal.

- **Company’s Defense:** Brightmoor Foods is represented by Carter Legal Partners. Our defense hinges on documented performance issues independent of the health and safety claims and adherence to established termination procedures.

### Expected Outcome
The tribunal is expected to deliver a verdict by 20th January 2024. Potential outcomes include:

- Full reinstatement with back pay
- Compensation for harassment and constructive dismissal
- Revised health and safety training protocols across all manufacturing sites

Estimated financial exposure ranges from £75,000 to £150,000 in compensation, plus legal costs.
