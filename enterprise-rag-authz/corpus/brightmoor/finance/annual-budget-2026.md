## Overview
The Annual Budget 2026 for Brightmoor Foods is designed to support our strategic growth objectives while maintaining financial stability. The total budget allocation for 2026 is set at **€48.7 million**, reflecting a **7% increase** from the 2025 budget of €45.5 million. This budget spans all operational, investment, and administrative expenses across our manufacturing, marketing, and R&D divisions.

Internal reference code: CNRY-970ed082.

## Departmental Budgets

### Manufacturing
The Manufacturing department will receive **€21.3 million**, an increase of **5%** from 2025. This allocation will cover production line upgrades, raw material costs, and workforce expansion. Key initiatives include the installation of automated packaging machinery at our Vienna facility, projected to reduce operational costs by **€1.2 million** annually.

### Marketing
The Marketing department’s budget has been allocated **€9.8 million**, representing a **10% increase** from the previous year. This funding will support expanded digital advertising campaigns, trade show participation in key European markets, and the launch of three new product lines targeted for Q2 and Q4 2026. A significant portion, **€3.5 million**, will be dedicated to enhancing our social media presence and influencer partnerships.

### Research & Development
R&D will be allocated **€6.5 million**, a **15% increase** from 2025. This budget will fund the development of two new plant-based product lines and the enhancement of our existing product formulations to meet emerging health and sustainability trends. The team will also invest **€1.1 million** in collaborative research projects with local universities.

## Administrative and Operational Costs
Administrative and operational costs are budgeted at **€10.1 million**, maintaining a **3% increase** from 2025. This includes salaries, utilities, office supplies, and IT infrastructure upgrades. A notable investment of **€750,000** will be directed towards cybersecurity enhancements to protect our data and ensure compliance with EU regulations.

## Savings and Contingency
We have allocated **€1.0 million** to a contingency fund to address unforeseen expenses. Additionally, we expect to generate **€2.3 million** in savings through improved supply chain efficiencies and energy cost reductions, as a result of our recent partnership with GreenLogix Suppliers.
