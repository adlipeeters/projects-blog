## Financial Performance Overview (Q3 2024)

We are pleased to report a solid performance in Q3 2024. Total revenue reached **€112.4 million**, marking a **7.2%** increase compared to Q3 2023. Our net profit after tax stood at **€18.3 million**, a **5.8%** increase year-over-year. The gross margin improved to **32.5%**, up from **30.1%** in Q3 2023, primarily due to optimized supply chain efficiencies and increased pricing on select product lines.

## Key Financial Metrics

- **EBITDA:** €26.7 million (↑ 6.4% YoY)
- **Operating Profit:** €21.1 million (↑ 4.9% YoY)
- **Current Ratio:** 1.8 (stable)
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.45 (down from 0.50 in Q2 2024)

## Cash Flow Analysis

Operating activities generated **€14.5 million** in cash during Q3, an increase of **€2.1 million** compared to Q3 2023. Investing activities consumed **€9.8 million**, mainly due to capital expenditures for new packaging equipment at our Vienna facility. Financing activities resulted in a net cash outflow of **€3.2 million**, primarily due to the repayment of a €5 million term loan.

## Forward-Looking Statements

We anticipate continued growth in Q4 2024, with projected revenue of **€120 million** and a net profit of **€19.8 million**. We remain focused on reducing our cost base further through ongoing efficiency initiatives and expect to achieve annual savings of **€3.5 million** by Q2 2025. Our capital expenditure plan for the remainder of the year stands at **€12 million**, focusing on upgrading our logistics network in preparation for expanded distribution into the Benelux region.
