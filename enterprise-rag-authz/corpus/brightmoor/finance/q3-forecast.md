## Q3 Revenue Projections

Internal reference code: CNRY-e6424523.

Based on current market trends and sales data, we forecast Q3 revenue to reach **€48.7 million**, representing a **+5.2%** increase compared to Q3 of last year. This growth is primarily driven by stronger demand in our frozen vegetable line and new product launches in the ready-to-eat meal category.

### Key Drivers

1. **Frozen Vegetables**: Expected revenue of **€18.3 million** (+7.1% YoY) due to increased retail partnerships and promotional activities.
2. **Ready-to-Eat Meals**: Projected at **€12.1 million**, largely attributable to the successful rollout of our “QuickHeat” range in major European supermarkets.
3. **Snacks Division**: Forecasted to generate **€9.4 million**, a modest +2.8% YoY, supported by seasonal summer promotions.

## Cost Management and Profit Margins

We anticipate operational costs to total **€32.6 million**, a **+3.4%** increase from Q3 last year, mainly due to higher raw material costs and increased marketing spend. Despite this, we expect to maintain a gross profit margin of **36.5%**, slightly down from 36.8% in Q3 2023, but within our target range.

### Cost Breakdown

- **Raw Materials**: €12.9 million (+5% YoY)
- **Labor**: €8.2 million (+2% YoY)
- **Marketing**: €5.7 million (+10% YoY)
- **Overhead**: €5.8 million (+1.5% YoY)

## Cash Flow and Investments

We project a net cash inflow of **€9.8 million** for Q3, after accounting for capital expenditures of **€4.1 million** on new packaging equipment and facility upgrades. This will help us maintain a healthy cash balance of **€25.4 million** by the end of September 30, 2024.

### Planned Investments

- **Packaging Equipment**: €2.5 million
- **Facility Upgrades**: €1.3 million
- **R&D for New Products**: €0.3 million

We remain cautious about potential supply chain disruptions and are implementing risk mitigation strategies to ensure we meet our delivery commitments.
