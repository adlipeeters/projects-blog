## Introduction

This document serves as a guide for maintaining and updating the Fixed Asset Register (FAR) for Brightmoor Foods. The FAR is a critical component of our asset management system, ensuring accurate tracking and reporting of all fixed assets within the company. This register includes detailed information on property, plant, and equipment (PP&E) such as machinery, buildings, vehicles, and furniture. The FAR is updated quarterly and is essential for financial reporting, depreciation calculations, and audit preparations.

## Asset Classification and Coding

Assets in the FAR are classified into five main categories, each with a unique coding system for easy identification and retrieval. The categories and their respective codes are as follows:

1. **Machinery and Equipment (ME)**: Assets used in the production process, such as industrial mixers, conveyors, and packaging machines. Example: ME-2023-045 (Industrial Mixer, installed on 15 March 2023, value €85,000).
2. **Buildings and Land (BL)**: All real estate assets, including factories, offices, and land. Example: BL-2022-012 (Factory Building, acquired on 01 June 2022, value €1,200,000).
3. **Vehicles (V)**: Company vehicles used for transportation and logistics. Example: V-2023-030 (Delivery Van, purchased on 10 April 2023, value €45,000).
4. **Furniture and Fixtures (FF)**: Office and production floor furniture, including desks, chairs, and lighting. Example: FF-2023-025 (Office Furniture Set, purchased on 05 May 2023, value €12,000).
5. **Computer Equipment (CE)**: All digital assets, including laptops, desktops, and servers. Example: CE-2023-055 (Server Rack, installed on 20 April 2023, value €30,000).

## Depreciation Methods

Depreciation is calculated annually using the straight-line method for all assets, ensuring a consistent expense recognition over the useful life of each asset. The depreciation rates for each category are as follows:

- **Machinery and Equipment**: 10% per annum
- **Buildings and Land**: 2% per annum (land is not depreciated)
- **Vehicles**: 15% per annum
- **Furniture and Fixtures**: 5% per annum
- **Computer Equipment**: 20% per annum

Depreciation is recorded in the financial statements and updated in the FAR at the end of each fiscal year. For example, the depreciation expense for the Industrial Mixer (ME-2023-045) for the year 2023 is €8,500.

## Maintenance and Updates

The FAR is maintained by the Finance Department in collaboration with the Asset Management Team. Any changes, such as new asset acquisitions, disposals, or significant modifications, must be reported to the Finance Department within five business days. Quarterly reviews are conducted to ensure the accuracy and completeness of the register. The most recent update was on 30 June 2024, reflecting new acquisitions and depreciation adjustments for the year-to-date.

All employees are encouraged to familiarize themselves with the FAR and report any discrepancies or updates promptly to maintain the integrity of our asset records.
