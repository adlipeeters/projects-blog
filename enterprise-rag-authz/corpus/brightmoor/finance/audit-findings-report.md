**Audit Findings Report**
**Department: Finance**
**Date: October 15, 2023**

Internal reference code: CNRY-d3e6f473.

## Executive Summary

This report outlines the key findings from the internal audit conducted at Brightmoor Foods from September 1 to September 30, 2023. The audit was led by Elena Kovacs, Senior Auditor, and her team. The primary objective was to assess financial controls, compliance with accounting standards, and operational efficiency.

## Financial Controls Assessment

The audit revealed several areas where financial controls could be strengthened:

1. **Invoice Processing:** It was found that 12% of invoices processed in Q3 2023 lacked proper authorization. Specifically, invoices totaling €245,000 did not have the required dual sign-off, increasing the risk of fraudulent payments.

2. **Reconciliation Discrepancies:** Monthly reconciliations for accounts payable showed discrepancies amounting to €58,000. These discrepancies were primarily due to timing differences in recording payments and receipts.

3. **Segregation of Duties:** In the accounts payable department, the same employee, John Müller, was responsible for both invoice approval and payment authorization. This violation of segregation of duties principle poses a significant risk of unauthorized or erroneous payments.

## Compliance with Accounting Standards

The audit confirmed that Brightmoor Foods is largely compliant with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), with a few exceptions:

1. **Revenue Recognition:** Revenue from a new product line, "EcoSnacks," was not recognized in the correct period. €112,000 in revenue was recorded in Q2 2023 instead of Q3 2023, impacting the financial statements’ accuracy.

2. **Inventory Valuation:** Inventory valuations for the "Organic Bakery" line were not updated to reflect current market prices, leading to an overstatement of inventory value by €67,000.

## Recommendations

To address these findings, the following recommendations have been made:

1. **Implement Dual Authorization for Invoices:** Ensure that all invoices above €10,000 require dual authorization.

2. **Regular Reconciliation:** Conduct weekly reconciliations for accounts payable to promptly identify and resolve discrepancies.

3. **Segregate Duties:** Reassign duties within the accounts payable department to ensure no single employee has control over both invoice approval and payment authorization.

4. **Train Staff on IFRS:** Organize training sessions for relevant staff on revenue recognition and inventory valuation under IFRS.

## Conclusion

The internal audit has highlighted critical areas for improvement in financial controls and compliance. Implementing the recommended actions will enhance financial accuracy, reduce risk, and ensure adherence to accounting standards.
