# Intercompany Charges Policy

Internal reference code: CNRY-bc2e3197.

As of **January 1, 2024**, Brightmoor Foods implements the following policy for intercompany charges to ensure transparency, compliance with EU accounting standards, and accurate financial reporting across our subsidiaries.

## Transaction Types and Pricing

1. **Logistics and Distribution**:
    - **Brightmoor Logistics GmbH** will charge **Brightmoor Snacks BV** **€150 per pallet** for warehousing and distribution services. This rate includes handling, storage for up to 90 days, and transportation within a 200 km radius.
    - Invoicing will occur monthly, with the next invoice due on **February 15, 2024**.

2. **Shared Manufacturing Facilities**:
    - **Brightmoor Manufacturing S.A.** will bill **Brightmoor Bakery Ltd.** **€75 per operational hour** for the use of shared baking lines. This rate covers maintenance, utilities, and a portion of the facility's overhead.
    - The first billing cycle, covering January 2024, will be **€11,250**, reflecting 150 operational hours.

## Reporting and Documentation

All intercompany transactions must be documented with the following supporting documents:

- **Service Agreement**: A detailed contract outlining the services provided, rates, and payment terms.
- **Timesheets and Logs**: For labor-intensive services, timesheets must be submitted weekly.
- **Invoicing Records**: Invoices should be itemized and reconciled with the corresponding service agreement.

## Transfer Pricing Compliance

To adhere to EU transfer pricing regulations, all intercompany charges must meet **arm’s length principle** standards. This means charges should reflect prices that would be agreed upon between independent parties under comparable circumstances.

- **Documentation**: Maintain detailed documentation supporting the chosen transfer pricing methodology. This includes comparable market data and internal cost analyses.
- **Audit Preparation**: Ensure all records are audit-ready. Internal audits will be conducted **quarterly**, with the next scheduled for **April 30, 2024**.

## Review and Adjustment

Intercompany charges will be reviewed **annually** to reflect market changes, cost fluctuations, and operational efficiencies. The next review period begins on **July 1, 2024**. Any adjustments will be implemented **October 1, 2024**.

Adherence to this policy is mandatory for all departments involved in intercompany transactions. Failure to comply may result in financial restatements and potential regulatory penalties.
