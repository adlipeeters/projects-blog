## Overview

This document outlines Brightmoor Foods' cash flow projection for the second quarter of 2025 (April to June). The projection is based on historical data, current sales trends, and anticipated operational expenses. All figures are in EUR.

## Revenue Projections

We expect total revenue for Q2 2025 to reach **€12,500,000**. This is an increase of **5%** compared to Q2 2024, driven by higher demand for our premium product lines and successful marketing campaigns. Breakdown:

- **Fresh Prepared Meals:** €6,200,000
- **Snack Products:** €4,300,000
- **Bakery Items:** €2,000,000

## Expenses and Cash Outflows

Total expected cash outflows for Q2 2025 are projected at **€10,800,000**. Major categories include:

- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** €6,500,000
- **Salaries and Benefits:** €2,200,000
- **Marketing and Advertising:** €900,000
- **Rent and Utilities:** €600,000
- **Maintenance and Repairs:** €400,000
- **Other Operational Costs:** €200,000

## Net Cash Flow

The projected net cash flow for Q2 2025 is **€1,700,000**. This positive net cash flow indicates that we will have sufficient liquidity to meet our operational needs and invest in growth initiatives. Key factors contributing to this positive outlook include improved inventory management, reduced waste, and effective cost control measures.

## Recommendations

To maintain and improve our cash flow position, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Continue optimizing inventory management** to reduce holding costs and improve turnover rates.
2. **Invest in targeted marketing campaigns** to further boost sales of high-margin products.
3. **Regularly review and negotiate supplier contracts** to secure better pricing and terms.
4. **Monitor operational efficiencies** to identify additional cost-saving opportunities.

## Conclusion

The cash flow projection for Q2 2025 is optimistic, with a net cash flow of €1,700,000. By adhering to the recommended actions, Brightmoor Foods can strengthen its financial position and support continued growth.
