### **Revenue Recognition Overview**

Internal reference code: CNRY-3e8f1e2d.

At Brightmoor Foods, our revenue recognition policy is designed to ensure compliance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) 15, “Revenue from Contracts with Customers.” This policy outlines the principles and procedures for recognizing revenue from our various product lines, including ready-to-eat meals, snack items, and frozen beverages.

### **Performance Obligations and Transaction Price Allocation**

Revenue is recognized when (or as) we satisfy a performance obligation by transferring a promised good or service to the customer. A performance obligation is deemed satisfied when control of the product transfers to the customer. For our standard packaged goods, control typically transfers at the point of sale—when goods are delivered to the retailer or distributor.

The transaction price—the amount we expect to receive in exchange for satisfying the performance obligation—is allocated to each distinct performance obligation based on their relative stand-ready values. For example, a contract to supply 10,000 units of “Solaris” ready-to-eat meals and 5,000 units of “Nova” snack bars would allocate revenue separately to each product line according to their relative selling prices. As of Q2 2024, the average selling price for “Solaris” meals is €8.50 per unit, while “Nova” snack bars average €3.00 per unit.

### **Practical Exemptions and Special Considerations**

Certain contracts may qualify for practical exemptions from full allocation of the transaction price. For contracts with a total transaction price below €25,000, we apply a simplified method, recognizing revenue as the goods are delivered. Additionally, for contracts with a duration of less than 90 days where all performance obligations are satisfied over time, revenue is recognized on a straight-line basis over the contract period.

### **Revenue Recognition Timing and Reporting**

Revenue is recorded in the period in which the performance obligations are satisfied. For our primary product lines, this typically occurs upon delivery to the retailer or distributor. For instance, in Q1 2024, Brightmoor Foods recognized €12.4 million in revenue from the delivery of 1.5 million “Solaris” meals to major European retailers.

For contracts involving significant financing components, the time value of money is considered, and revenue is adjusted accordingly. As of the fiscal year ending December 31, 2024, we expect approximately €800,000 of revenue adjustment due to financing components in long-term distributor contracts.
