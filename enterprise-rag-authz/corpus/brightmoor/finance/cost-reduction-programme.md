**Cost Reduction Programme**

Internal reference code: CNRY-45aa63d7.

## Overview

The finance department has initiated a Cost Reduction Programme to enhance operational efficiency and improve our profit margins by the end of Q3 2024. This programme focuses on three primary areas: raw material procurement, manufacturing efficiency, and administrative overheads. Our goal is to reduce overall costs by 12% within the next 12 months, targeting a savings of €2.4 million annually.

## Raw Material Procurement

We aim to reduce the cost of raw materials by 8%. Currently, we spend €10 million quarterly on raw materials. By renegotiating contracts with key suppliers and consolidating orders, we project savings of €800,000 per quarter. Key actions include:

- Renegotiating contracts with suppliers: Suppliers Alpha Ingredients and Beta Foods have agreed to a 5% discount on all purchases exceeding €5 million per quarter.
- Consolidating orders: By reducing the number of suppliers from 15 to 10, we will cut logistics and transaction costs by €200,000 per quarter.

Implementation will begin on **June 1, 2024**, with expected savings realised by **July 31, 2024**.

## Manufacturing Efficiency

Targeting a 3% reduction in manufacturing costs, currently standing at €8 million per quarter, we plan to implement lean manufacturing principles. This will involve:

- Upgrading machinery: Investing €500,000 in new equipment to improve production speed and reduce waste.
- Staff training: A €100,000 programme to train 50 employees in lean techniques, reducing downtime by 10%.

These measures are scheduled to start on **July 1, 2024**, with full benefits expected by **September 30, 2024**.

## Administrative Overheads

We will cut administrative overheads by 1% from the current €2 million per quarter. Strategies include:

- Reducing travel expenses: Implementing a virtual meeting policy, saving €50,000 per quarter.
- Streamlining processes: Automating invoice processing will save €30,000 per quarter.

These changes will be rolled out starting **August 1, 2024**, with savings visible by **October 31, 2024**.

## Summary

The Cost Reduction Programme is expected to yield total savings of €1.63 million per quarter, amounting to €6.52 million annually. This initiative will not only improve our financial health but also enhance our competitiveness in the market.
