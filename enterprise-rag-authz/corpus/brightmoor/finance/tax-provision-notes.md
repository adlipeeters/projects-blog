**Tax Provision Notes for Q2 2024**

Internal reference code: CNRY-b53039c5.

## Current Tax Rate and Adjustments

As of Q2 2024, our effective tax rate stands at **23.5%**, reflecting recent adjustments in corporate tax legislation across our primary markets in Germany and the Netherlands. The anticipated rate for the next fiscal year has been revised to **24.2%**, due to anticipated changes in EU-wide tax directives scheduled to take effect in January 2025.

## Deferred Tax Assets and Liabilities

Our deferred tax assets as of June 30, 2024, total **€8.7 million**, primarily arising from carryforward losses in our Dutch subsidiary, NoordDelight BV. These assets are expected to be fully realized within the next three fiscal years, based on current operational forecasts. Conversely, our deferred tax liabilities amount to **€5.3 million**, mainly due to temporary differences in our German subsidiary, BrightBissen GmbH. These liabilities are expected to reverse within the next five years.

## Tax Provisions for Recent Acquisitions

Following the acquisition of **SnackWell GmbH** on April 15, 2024, for **€12.5 million**, we have established an additional tax provision of **€2.1 million**. This provision accounts for potential tax liabilities associated with SnackWell’s historical operations, including unresolved tax claims in Austria. The provision has been recorded in the current year’s financial statements under ‘Tax Provisions’.

## Estimated Tax Payment Schedule

For Q3 2024, our estimated tax payments are as follows:
- **Germany**: **€3.2 million**, due by September 15, 2024.
- **Netherlands**: **€1.8 million**, due by October 1, 2024.
- **Austria**: **€0.6 million**, due by November 30, 2024.

These estimates are based on current taxable income projections and do not include potential adjustments from ongoing tax audits or future legislative changes.
