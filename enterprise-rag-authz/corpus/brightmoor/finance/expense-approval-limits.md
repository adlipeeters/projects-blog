This document outlines the expense approval limits for Brightmoor Foods employees, effective from October 1, 2024. These guidelines ensure efficient processing and proper financial control.

## General Expense Limits

- **Employees:** Expenses up to €500 can be approved by the direct supervisor.
- **Department Heads:** Expenses between €501 and €2,500 require approval from the respective Department Head.
- **Finance Manager:** Any expense exceeding €2,500 must be approved by the Finance Manager, currently Elena Rodriguez.

## Travel and Accommodation

- **Domestic Travel:** Employees can approve travel expenses up to €1,000 per trip. Trips costing between €1,001 and €3,000 need Department Head approval. Trips exceeding €3,000 require Finance Manager approval.
- **International Travel:** All international travel expenses over €1,500 must be approved by the Finance Manager, regardless of the destination.

## Miscellaneous Expenses

- **Office Supplies:** Up to €300 per month per department can be approved by the Department Head.
- **Client Entertainment:** Expenses up to €1,000 per event can be approved by the Department Head. Events exceeding this amount require Finance Manager approval.

## Emergency Expenses

In case of emergencies, employees may spend up to €2,000 without prior approval, provided that a detailed explanation and receipts are submitted within 48 hours. Such expenses will then be processed for retrospective approval by the Finance Manager.

These limits are designed to maintain financial discipline while allowing necessary flexibility. Please ensure all expenses are documented accurately and submitted promptly for approval.

For any questions or clarifications, contact the Finance Department at finance@brightmoorfoods.com.
