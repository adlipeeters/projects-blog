### **Investment Appraisal Framework**

Internal reference code: CNRY-5b0db9b8.

As we continue to grow and explore new market opportunities, it's crucial to have a standardized method for evaluating potential investments. This document outlines the recommended approach for appraising investments at Brightmoor Foods, effective from Q1 2025.

### **Criteria for Investment Appraisal**

1. **Net Present Value (NPV)**
   - All projects must undergo an NPV analysis using a discount rate of 8%, reflecting our weighted average cost of capital (WACC).
   - For instance, Project "FreshLines" is expected to generate cash flows of €1.2M, €1.5M, and €1.8M over the next three years. The present value of these cash flows, discounted at 8%, yields an NPV of €2.1M, indicating a viable investment.

2. **Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**
   - The IRR should exceed our hurdle rate of 9% to be considered. Project "EcoPack" has an IRR of 12%, surpassing the threshold.

3. **Payback Period**
   - We aim for a maximum payback period of 2 years. The "QuickFreeze" initiative is projected to recover its initial investment within 18 months.

### **Evaluation Timeline**

- **Submission**: Project proposals must be submitted to the Finance Department by the 15th of each month for the following month’s review.
- **Review**: A committee, led by CFO Anna Müller, will evaluate proposals on the last Friday of each month.
- **Approval**: Approved projects will be announced by the 5th of the subsequent month, allowing time for resource allocation.

### **Reporting and Compliance**

All project managers are required to submit quarterly reports detailing actual versus projected performance. Any deviations exceeding 10% must be explained and rectified promptly. This ensures transparency and helps maintain our financial health.

By adhering to this framework, we can make informed, strategic decisions that align with our long-term goals.
