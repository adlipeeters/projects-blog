**Effective Date:** October 1, 2024

Internal reference code: CNRY-271d7165.

This document outlines the updated supplier payment terms for Brightmoor Foods, effective from October 1, 2024. These changes aim to improve cash flow management and strengthen our relationships with suppliers.

## Standard Payment Terms

For all new purchase orders placed on or after October 1, 2024, the standard payment term will be **Net 45 days**. This means that invoices must be paid within 45 days from the date of the invoice. For example, an invoice dated October 15, 2024, would be due for payment by November 29, 2024.

## Early Payment Discount

To incentivize timely payments and strengthen partnerships, Brightmoor Foods will offer an early payment discount of **2%** on all invoices. This discount is applicable if the invoice is paid within **15 days** from the invoice date. For instance, an invoice amounting to €50,000 would qualify for a discount of €1,000 if paid by November 14, 2024, instead of the due date of November 29, 2024.

## Extended Payment Terms for Key Suppliers

For our key suppliers, defined as those providing more than **€1,000,000** in annual revenue to Brightmoor Foods, we will offer extended payment terms of **Net 60 days**. This flexibility is intended to support our strategic partners and ensure continuity of supply. Suppliers must be formally notified in writing to qualify for these terms.

## Late Payment Penalties

To ensure timely payments, a late payment penalty of **1.5% per month** will be applied to any invoice not paid within the agreed terms. This penalty will be calculated on the outstanding amount and will accrue monthly until the invoice is settled. For example, an invoice of €20,000 not paid by the due date of November 29, 2024, will incur a penalty of €300 on December 29, 2024, and will continue to accrue until paid in full.

These updated terms are designed to balance the financial needs of both Brightmoor Foods and our valued suppliers, ensuring mutually beneficial business relationships.
