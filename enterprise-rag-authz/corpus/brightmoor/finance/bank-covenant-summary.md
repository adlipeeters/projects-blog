**Bank Covenant Summary**

Internal reference code: CNRY-19a18935.

As of Q2 2024, this document outlines the key banking covenants for our primary financing facilities with European Bank Consortium (EBC). These covenants are critical to maintaining our borrowing capacity and ensuring financial stability.

## Liquidity Covenant

Our primary liquidity covenant requires us to maintain a **minimum liquidity ratio of 1.5x** our short-term debt. As of June 30, 2024, our liquidity ratio stands at **1.7x**, calculated as follows:

- **Cash and cash equivalents:** €45 million
- **Short-term investments:** €20 million
- **Current liabilities:** €42 million

Liquidity Ratio = (Cash + Short-term investments) / Current liabilities = (€45 million + €20 million) / €42 million = **1.7x**

This ratio exceeds our covenant requirement, providing a comfortable buffer.

## Debt-to-EBITDA Covenant

The debt-to-EBITDA covenant limits our total debt to **3.0x our EBITDA**. For the 12 months ending June 30, 2024, our covenant compliance is as follows:

- **Total debt:** €150 million
- **EBITDA:** €50 million

Debt-to-EBITDA Ratio = Total debt / EBITDA = €150 million / €50 million = **3.0x**

Our ratio is exactly at the covenant limit. Going forward, we must carefully manage debt levels to avoid breaching this covenant.

## Interest Coverage Ratio Covenant

The interest coverage ratio covenant requires us to maintain a ratio of **3.0x**, calculated as EBITDA divided by interest expense. For the period ending June 30, 2024:

- **EBITDA:** €50 million
- **Interest expense:** €15 million

Interest Coverage Ratio = EBITDA / Interest expense = €50 million / €15 million = **3.33x**

We are comfortably above the required ratio, indicating strong ability to service our debt interest payments.

## Next Review Date

The next covenant review date is **September 30, 2024**. Please ensure all financial projections and actuals are up-to-date in the financial management system to facilitate covenant compliance reporting.

Going forward, we will continue to monitor these covenants closely and adjust our financial strategy as needed to maintain compliance and optimal borrowing capacity.
