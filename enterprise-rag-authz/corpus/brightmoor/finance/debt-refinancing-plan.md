**Debt Refinancing Plan**

Internal reference code: CNRY-67247868.

**Prepared by:** Elena Martinez, CFO
**Date:** October 5, 2024

## Current Debt Overview
As of Q3 2024, Brightmoor Foods carries a total debt of €120 million, composed of a mix of short-term and long-term loans. Our current debt structure includes:
- **€60 million** in a 5-year fixed-rate loan at 5% interest, maturing in June 2025.
- **€40 million** in a variable-rate loan linked to the EURIBOR + 2%, currently at 3.5%.
- **€20 million** in a short-term line of credit with an interest rate of 4%.

Our debt servicing costs have risen to €7 million annually, impacting our cash flow and profitability.

## Refinancing Strategy
To mitigate these challenges, we propose a three-phase refinancing plan to be executed over the next 18 months:

### Phase 1: Short-Term Loan Consolidation (Q4 2024)
We will consolidate our short-term line of credit (€20 million) and a portion of our variable-rate loan (€10 million) into a new 5-year fixed-rate loan. We have secured preliminary approval from NordBank for a €30 million loan at a fixed rate of 4.2%, reducing our variable-rate exposure and lowering our annual interest expense by approximately €650,000.

### Phase 2: Long-Term Loan Refinancing (Q1 2025)
In January 2025, we plan to refinance the remaining €30 million of our variable-rate loan and the €60 million fixed-rate loan maturing in June 2025. We are in discussions with EuroFinance to secure a 7-year loan at a fixed rate of 4.5%, which would reduce our annual interest expense by an additional €1.1 million.

### Phase 3: Cash Flow Optimization (2025-2026)
Post-refinancing, we will implement a cash flow optimization strategy to improve our debt servicing capacity. This includes:
- **Reducing operating costs** by €2 million annually through supply chain optimizations and energy efficiency measures.
- **Increasing cash reserves** by accelerating collections and extending payment terms with key suppliers.

## Expected Outcomes
Upon full implementation of this plan by mid-2026, we expect to achieve:
- **Annual interest savings** of €1.75 million.
- **Improved credit rating**, potentially upgrading from BBB to A-
- **Enhanced cash flow**, freeing up €2 million annually for reinvestment in growth initiatives.

We will provide a detailed timeline and risk assessment in the next financial team meeting scheduled for October 15, 2024.
