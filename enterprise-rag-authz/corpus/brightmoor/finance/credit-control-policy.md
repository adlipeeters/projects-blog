## **Policy Overview**

This document outlines the Credit Control Policy for Brightmoor Foods, ensuring timely collection of receivables and minimising credit risk. The policy applies to all customers and sales made on credit terms.

## **Credit Limit Policy**

Credit limits are assigned based on customer creditworthiness, determined through a rigorous evaluation process. New customers will have an initial credit limit of **€10,000**, subject to review after three months of trading. Established customers with a proven payment history may be eligible for an increased limit, up to a maximum of **€50,000**. Credit limits will be reviewed quarterly by the Credit Control Team.

## **Payment Terms and Reminders**

All invoices must be paid within **30 days** of the invoice date. Payment terms are clearly stated on all invoices and purchase orders. Brightmoor Foods will issue friendly payment reminders at **7, 15, and 21 days** past due. A late fee of **5% per month** will be applied to any outstanding balance after **30 days**.

## **Debt Recovery Procedure**

If a customer fails to make a payment within the stipulated time frame, the following steps will be taken:

1. **First Reminder**: Issued at 7 days past due, encouraging payment.
2. **Second Reminder**: Issued at 15 days past due, highlighting the impending late fees.
3. **Final Demand**: Issued at 21 days past due, with a statement of late fees and a request for immediate payment.
4. **Handover to Collections**: After 30 days, the account will be handed over to our accredited debt collection agency, **Nordic Credit Solutions**.

Brightmoor Foods is committed to maintaining strong customer relationships while ensuring the timely collection of receivables. Adherence to this Credit Control Policy is mandatory for all finance staff.
