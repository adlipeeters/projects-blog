**Promotion Criteria**

Internal reference code: CNRY-533bb70d.

Effective from **January 1, 2025**, Brightmoor Foods has updated its promotion criteria to ensure transparency, fairness, and alignment with our strategic goals. These guidelines apply to all full-time employees across all departments.

## Performance Metrics

Promotions will be primarily based on performance, measured over the last **12 months**. Employees must achieve a minimum annual performance score of **85 out of 100**. Performance scores are calculated based on the following weighted factors:

- **60%** - Delivery of key job responsibilities
- **20%** - Innovation and contribution to process improvements
- **10%** - Team collaboration and leadership
- **10%** - Adherence to company values and ethics

## Tenure and Development

In addition to performance metrics, candidates for promotion must meet the following tenure and development requirements:

- **Minimum Tenure**: Employees must have a minimum of **18 months** in their current role before being eligible for promotion.
- **Professional Development**: Employees must complete at least **3 professional development hours** per year, documented through our Learning Management System (LMS). Relevant certifications, internal training courses, and attending company-sponsored workshops all count towards this requirement.

## Promotion Pathways

Brightmoor Foods offers two primary promotion pathways:

1. **Vertical Promotions**: Moving up to a higher job level within the same department or function. For example, a **Production Supervisor** may be promoted to **Production Manager**.
2. **Lateral Moves with Responsibility Increase**: Moving to a different department or function that carries more responsibility, even if the job level remains the same. For instance, a **Financial Analyst** in the Corporate Finance department might move to a **Budget Analyst** role in the Operations department, which includes additional responsibilities for budget oversight.

## Review Process

Promotion requests are reviewed quarterly, with cut-off dates set for **March 31, June 30, September 30, and December 31**. The review process involves:

- **Self-Assessment**: Employees submit a detailed self-assessment form outlining their achievements, development activities, and reasons for seeking promotion.
- **Manager Assessment**: Direct managers provide a detailed evaluation of the employee's performance, tenure, and development.
- **HR Review**: The HR department conducts a final review to ensure all criteria are met and to assess fit within the broader organizational structure.
- **Promotion Board**: A Promotion Board, consisting of the HR Director, CFO, and three department heads, makes the final decision on promotions.

Employees will be notified of the outcome within **4 weeks** of the review cycle's conclusion.
