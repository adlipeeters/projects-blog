**Remote Work Agreement Terms**

Internal reference code: CNRY-22e0857a.

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

**Introduction**

This Remote Work Agreement outlines the terms and conditions for employees of Brightmoor Foods who wish to work remotely on a full-time or part-time basis. These guidelines ensure consistency and clarity across our organization.

**Eligibility Criteria**

Employees eligible for remote work must:

1. Have been with Brightmoor Foods for a minimum of six months.
2. Maintain a performance rating of “Meets Expectations” or higher for two consecutive quarters.
3. Work in roles that can be performed remotely without significant impairment to team productivity.

**Remote Work Schedule**

Remote work schedules can be either:

- **Full-Time Remote:** Employees work entirely from home or another approved location for a minimum of four days per week.
- **Part-Time Remote:** Employees split their time between the office and a remote location, with a minimum of two days per week working remotely.

All remote employees must adhere to a core collaboration window from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM Central European Time (CET) to ensure team alignment.

**Compensation and Benefits**

- **Salary:** Remote employees receive the same base salary as their in-office counterparts.
- **Home Office Allowance:** A one-time allowance of €500 is provided for setting up a suitable home office.
- **Internet Reimbursement:** Employees can claim up to €50 per month for approved internet expenses, subject to receipt submission.
- **Health and Wellness:** Remote employees remain eligible for the company’s health, dental, and vision plans, as well as the annual wellness stipend of €300.

**Performance and Accountability**

Remote employees are expected to meet all performance metrics and deadlines as if they were working on-site. Progress reviews will occur quarterly, and failure to meet expectations may result in the revocation of remote work privileges.

**Security and Data Protection**

All remote employees must comply with Brightmoor Foods’ Information Security Policy, including using company-issued devices, VPNs, and following data protection protocols.

**Termination of Agreement**

The company reserves the right to terminate remote work agreements if an employee’s performance deteriorates, if the role’s requirements change, or if business needs shift. Employees will be given a 30-day notice period to transition back to in-office work.

**Review and Updates**

This agreement will be reviewed annually and may be updated to reflect changes in company policy, legal requirements, or operational needs. The latest version will always be available on the company intranet.

**Contact Information**

For any questions regarding this agreement, please contact the HR department at hr@brightmoorfoods.com or schedule an appointment with your HR representative.
