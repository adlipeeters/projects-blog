**Overtime Pay Rules**

Internal reference code: CNRY-8a5012fa.

Effective from **January 1, 2025**, Brightmoor Foods has updated its overtime pay policies to ensure clarity and fairness for all employees. These guidelines apply to all full-time and part-time staff across all manufacturing and administrative sites in the EU.

## Eligibility for Overtime Pay

Employees are eligible for overtime pay when they work **more than 40 hours in a workweek**. This includes regular duties, special assignments, and any approved additional tasks. Overtime is calculated based on an employee’s **regular hourly rate**.

## Overtime Pay Rates

Overtime hours will be compensated at a rate of **1.5 times the regular hourly rate** for the first **4 hours** of overtime in a week. Any additional overtime hours beyond 4 in the same week will be paid at **2 times the regular hourly rate**. For example, if an employee’s regular hourly rate is **€18.50** and they work 5 overtime hours in a week, their overtime pay would be calculated as follows:

- First 4 hours: 4 hours * (€18.50 * 1.5) = €111.00
- Next 1 hour: 1 hour * (€18.50 * 2) = €37.00
- **Total overtime pay for the week: €148.00**

## Recording and Approval of Overtime

All overtime work must be recorded in the **Brightmoor Timekeeping System (BTS)** within **48 hours** of completion. Managers are required to **approve** all overtime entries **no later than 5 working days** after submission. Unapproved overtime will not be compensated unless there is documented justification and senior management approval.

## Payment Schedule

Overtime pay will be included in the **bi-weekly payroll cycle**. Employees can view their overtime earnings in the **Brightmoor Employee Portal** under the **Payroll History** section. Any discrepancies must be reported to the **HR department** within **10 working days** of the payroll date.

These updated policies aim to promote work-life balance while ensuring that extra effort is fairly compensated. For any questions or clarifications, please contact **HR Manager, Clara Jensen**, at **cjensen@brightmoorfoods.eu**.
