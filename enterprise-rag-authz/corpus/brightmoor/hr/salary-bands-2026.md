**Salary Bands for 2026**

Internal reference code: CNRY-4a63e156.

Effective from **1st January 2026**, Brightmoor Foods will implement updated salary bands across all departments to ensure fair compensation and reflect market trends. These adjustments have been made following a thorough market analysis and internal review process.

## Salary Band Structure

The salary bands for 2026 are structured to provide clear progression paths and competitive rates. Each band represents a range of salaries for a specific job grade, with the lower bound indicating the minimum salary and the upper bound indicating the maximum salary for that grade.

### Band 1: Entry-Level Positions
- **Range**: €30,000 - €38,000 annually
- **Examples**: Administrative Assistants, Junior Technicians
- **Annual Increment**: 5%

### Band 2: Associate Professional Positions
- **Range**: €40,000 - €55,000 annually
- **Examples**: Software Developers, Marketing Coordinators
- **Annual Increment**: 5%

### Band 3: Professional Positions
- **Range**: €55,000 - €75,000 annually
- **Examples**: Project Managers, Senior Accountants
- **Annual Increment**: 4%

### Band 4: Senior Management Positions
- **Range**: €75,000 - €110,000 annually
- **Examples**: Department Heads, Directors
- **Annual Increment**: 3%

## Implementation Timeline

The new salary bands will be implemented in the following phases:

1. **1st January 2026**: All new hires will be placed according to these new bands.
2. **1st April 2026**: Current employees will be reviewed and adjusted to fit within the new bands, with increments applied as per the annual increment rates.

## Additional Benefits

In addition to the updated salary bands, all employees will receive an increased company pension contribution of **2%** of their salary, effective from **1st January 2026**. Furthermore, the annual bonus structure will be revised to align with these new bands, ensuring that bonuses remain competitive and fair.

These changes aim to enhance employee satisfaction, attract top talent, and ensure that Brightmoor Foods remains a competitive employer in the European market.
