### **Purpose**

Internal reference code: CNRY-fc1cc03b.

The purpose of this policy is to outline the steps Brightmoor Foods will take to address misconduct or performance issues among employees. Effective from **1st June 2024**, this procedure ensures fairness, consistency, and a safe working environment for all staff.

### **Initial Discussion and Documentation**

When an issue is identified, the direct supervisor must schedule a private meeting with the employee within **five working days**. During this meeting, the supervisor will:

1. Clearly describe the observed behavior or performance shortfall, using specific examples and dates.
2. Allow the employee to present their side of the story.
3. Document the conversation in detail, including any witnesses present.

Following the meeting, the supervisor submits a **Formal Discussion Report** to the HR department. This report must be received by HR no later than **two working days** after the meeting.

### **Investigation and Formal Warning**

HR will conduct a thorough investigation, which may include interviews with additional staff, review of relevant records, and examination of physical evidence. The investigation report, due within **10 working days** of receiving the Formal Discussion Report, will be reviewed by the **Head of HR, Anna Müller**.

If the investigation substantiates the claim, Anna will issue a **Formal Warning Letter**. This letter, referencing specific incidents dated **15th June 2024**, will detail expectations for improvement and a timeline—usually **30 calendar days**—for the employee to demonstrate change. A copy of this letter will be placed in the employee’s personal file.

### **Further Action**

Should the employee fail to meet the expectations outlined in the Formal Warning Letter, further disciplinary action may be initiated. This could range from additional warnings to suspension or, in extreme cases, termination of employment. All further actions will follow a similar documented process, ensuring that employees are aware of the reasons and have the opportunity to respond.

Brightmoor Foods is committed to maintaining a workplace where all employees can thrive, free from misconduct and unfair treatment.
