## Current Pension Contribution Structure

At Brightmoor Foods, we are committed to supporting the long-term financial well-being of our employees through our company pension plan. As of January 1, 2025, the pension contribution rates are structured as follows:

- **Employee Contribution:** 5% of monthly gross salary
- **Company Contribution:** 10% of monthly gross salary

These contributions are subject to annual review and may be adjusted based on economic conditions, company performance, and industry standards.

## Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for the pension plan, employees must:

1. Have completed at least 6 months of continuous service with Brightmoor Foods.
2. Be at least 21 years old.
3. Work a minimum of 20 hours per week.

## Contribution Calculation Example

For illustrative purposes, consider an employee, Jane Doe, with a monthly gross salary of €3,000:

- **Jane's Monthly Employee Contribution:** €3,000 x 5% = €150
- **Brightmoor's Monthly Company Contribution:** €3,000 x 10% = €300

Thus, Jane’s total monthly pension contribution would be €450, with €150 coming from her own salary and €300 from Brightmoor Foods.

## Annual Review Process

The pension contribution rates will undergo an annual review process each December. HR will assess the following factors:

- Economic climate and inflation rates
- Company financial health and profitability
- Industry benchmarks and competitor practices

Any changes to the contribution rates will be communicated to all eligible employees by mid-February of the following year, with the new rates taking effect on April 1st.

Our goal is to maintain a competitive and sustainable pension plan that supports our employees’ retirement planning while ensuring the long-term viability of Brightmoor Foods.
