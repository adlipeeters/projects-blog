**Executive Compensation Summary for Fiscal Year 2023**

Internal reference code: CNRY-5feaa36f.

## Overview

This document provides a detailed summary of the compensation packages for Brightmoor Foods' executive team for the fiscal year 2023, covering January 1, 2023, to December 31, 2023. The compensation structure is designed to align executive interests with the company's long-term strategic goals and performance metrics.

## Base Salaries

The base salaries for our executive team reflect their roles, responsibilities, and industry benchmarks. The following outlines the base salaries for each executive:

- **Claudia Hartmann, CEO:** €480,000
- **Lars Björkman, CFO:** €420,000
- **Isabella Rossi, COO:** €410,000
- **Elin Nilsen, CMO:** €390,000

## Incentive Compensation

In addition to base salaries, executives are eligible for performance-based bonuses. These incentives are tied to both company-wide and individual performance metrics. The potential bonus payouts for FY2023 are structured as follows:

- **CEO:** Up to 120% of base salary, contingent on achieving EBITDA targets of €18 million and a 10% increase in revenue.
- **CFO:** Up to 110% of base salary, based on maintaining an investment grade credit rating and reducing operational costs by 8%.
- **COO:** Up to 105% of base salary, dependent on improving supply chain efficiency by 15% and reducing waste by 12%.
- **CMO:** Up to 100% of base salary, linked to a 20% growth in brand recognition and a 15% increase in market share.

## Long-Term Incentives

To further align executive interests with long-term company performance, we offer stock options and restricted stock units (RSUs). The details are as follows:

- **Stock Options:** Executives can purchase company stock at a discounted rate, with vesting periods of 3 to 4 years.
- **RSUs:** Grants of company stock that vest over a period of 3 years, contingent on meeting specific performance milestones.

The total value of long-term incentives for each executive is estimated at:

- **CEO:** €1.2 million
- **CFO:** €960,000
- **COO:** €930,000
- **CMO:** €880,000

## Conclusion

The compensation packages for Brightmoor Foods' executives are designed to ensure that their interests are closely aligned with the company's strategic objectives and long-term success. These packages combine competitive base salaries, performance-based incentives, and long-term stock-based awards to motivate and retain top-tier executive talent.
