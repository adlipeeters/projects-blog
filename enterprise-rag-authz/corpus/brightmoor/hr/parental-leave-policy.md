### Eligibility and Entitlement

Internal reference code: CNRY-4ed53a7f.

All full-time employees at Brightmoor Foods who have completed one year of continuous service are eligible for parental leave. This policy applies to both mothers and fathers, as well as adoptive and foster parents. Eligible employees are entitled to a total of **16 weeks** of unpaid parental leave per child. This leave can be taken consecutively or in intervals, provided the total duration does not exceed the allotted weeks.

### Leave Application Process

Employees must submit a **Parental Leave Application Form** to the Human Resources department at least **eight weeks** before the intended start date of leave. The form must include medical certification for maternity or adoption leave, or a relevant document for other types of leave. HR will review the application and confirm approval in writing within **two weeks** of receipt. Approved leave will then be recorded in the employee’s personal file and the company’s leave management system.

### Return to Work and Job Protection

Upon returning from parental leave, employees are guaranteed reinstatement to the same job or a comparable position with equivalent pay, benefits, and working conditions. Brightmoor Foods is committed to maintaining a supportive work environment and will engage in a **Return-to-Work Discussion** with the employee **two weeks** prior to the scheduled resumption date. This discussion will address any adjustments needed to facilitate a smooth transition back into the workplace.

### Financial Support and Resources

While parental leave at Brightmoor Foods is unpaid, the company offers several financial support options. Eligible employees can apply for a **Parental Leave Grant** of **€1,200** per child, disbursed in monthly installments. Additionally, Brightmoor Foods provides access to **Employee Assistance Programs** that offer counseling and resources for new parents. Details of these programs are available on the company intranet and from the HR team.
