**Policy Owner:** Human Resources Department

## Introduction

This document outlines Brightmoor Foods' policy on sick leave records, ensuring clarity, consistency, and compliance with European employment regulations. Our aim is to support employee wellbeing while maintaining operational efficiency.

## Eligibility and Entitlement

All full-time and part-time employees at Brightmoor Foods are entitled to a maximum of **10 paid sick days per calendar year**. This entitlement begins on the employee's start date and resets annually on January 1st. Sick leave can be taken incrementally, but employees must notify their direct supervisor **at least 24 hours** in advance, unless under exceptional circumstances such as sudden severe illness.

## Documentation Requirements

To maintain accurate sick leave records, employees must submit a **Medical Certificate** from a licensed healthcare professional for any sick leave lasting **three consecutive days or more**. The Medical Certificate should include the following details:

1. Employee’s full name and employee ID
2. Dates of absence
3. Diagnosis (generalized, not specific)
4. Healthcare provider’s name, contact information, and signature
5. Date issued

All documentation must be submitted to the HR department within **five working days** of the employee’s return. Failure to provide required documentation may result in the sick leave being recorded as **unpaid leave**.

## Record Keeping and Confidentiality

The HR department will maintain a confidential digital record of all sick leave instances in the **Brightmoor Foods Employee Health System (BF-EHS)**. Access to these records is restricted to HR personnel and managers with a legitimate need to know. Employees can access their own sick leave records by logging into the BF-EHS portal using their employee credentials.

Brightmoor Foods is committed to handling all health information with the utmost confidentiality, in compliance with GDPR and other relevant data protection laws. Any breaches of confidentiality will be subject to disciplinary action.

## Review and Updates

This policy will be reviewed annually by the HR department to ensure it remains aligned with legal requirements and best practices. Any updates will be communicated to all employees via email and posted on the company intranet.
