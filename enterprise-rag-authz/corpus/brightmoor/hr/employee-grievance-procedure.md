**Employee Grievance Procedure**

Internal reference code: CNRY-b5ec9706.

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

This document outlines the formal process for raising and addressing grievances within Brightmoor Foods. It is designed to ensure that all employees have a clear, fair, and confidential pathway to voice concerns without fear of retaliation.

## Filing a Grievance

Employees may file a grievance for any concern related to workplace conditions, harassment, discrimination, pay discrepancies, unsafe practices, or any other issue that affects their employment experience. To initiate the process, employees must submit a written grievance to their immediate supervisor or the Human Resources department.

The grievance form must be completed within **10 working days** of the incident or the date the employee became aware of the issue. The form requires detailed descriptions, dates, witnesses, and any supporting documentation. Forms are available on the intranet under HR Forms > Grievance Submission.

## Review and Investigation

Upon receipt, the HR department will acknowledge the grievance within **3 business days**. A designated HR representative, currently **Ms. Elena Torres, Senior HR Business Partner**, will conduct an impartial investigation. This may include interviews with the employee, the supervisor, witnesses, and a review of relevant records.

The investigation will be completed within **20 working days** of receipt, though complex cases may require extension up to an additional **10 days**. All parties will receive a written response outlining the findings and any actions to be taken.

## Resolution and Appeals

If the grievance is upheld, appropriate corrective actions will be implemented, which may include training, policy changes, or disciplinary measures. If the grievance is not upheld, the employee has the right to appeal the decision to the **Head of Human Resources, Mr. David Klein**, within **7 days** of receiving the response.

The appeal must include a written statement explaining why the employee disagrees with the decision. The Head of HR will review the appeal and provide a final decision within **10 working days**. All decisions are final and binding.

## Confidentiality and Anti-Retaliation

All grievances and related communications are strictly confidential. Brightmoor Foods maintains a zero-tolerance policy on retaliation. Any form of retaliation, including but not limited to intimidation, harassment, or adverse employment actions, will result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

Employees can report suspected retaliation to the HR department or directly to the **Ethics Hotline: +31 20 5555 123**.
