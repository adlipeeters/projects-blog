### Eligibility Criteria

Internal reference code: CNRY-bb5cb371.

All full-time employees who have completed a probation period of six months or more are eligible for the annual bonus scheme. Part-time and contract employees are not eligible unless they have worked a minimum of 20 hours per week on average over the qualifying period from January 1st to December 31st. New hires joining after June 30th of any year will be considered for the next year's bonus based on their pro-rated time with Brightmoor Foods.

### Bonus Calculation

The bonus is calculated as a percentage of an employee's annual base salary, varying between 5% and 15% depending on individual and company performance metrics. Individual performance is assessed using the Annual Performance Review scores, where employees must achieve at least a 'Meets Expectations' rating. The company performance component is determined by the annual EBITDA margin, with the following tiers:

- EBITDA margin of 8% or less: 5% bonus
- EBITDA margin between 8.1% and 10%: 10% bonus
- EBITDA margin above 10%: 15% bonus

For example, if an employee earns €50,000 annually and both individual and company targets are met with an EBITDA margin of 11%, the bonus would be €7,500 (15% of €50,000).

### Payout Schedule

Bonuses will be paid out in the first payroll cycle of February each year, following the finalization of the company's annual financial statements. Employees must be actively employed on the bonus payment date to receive their bonus. Any employee who resigns before the payout date will not be eligible to receive the bonus for that year.

### Additional Provisions

Brightmoor Foods reserves the right to adjust the bonus scheme rules annually, with any changes taking effect from January 1st and communicated to employees by November 30th of the preceding year. Employees receiving disciplinary action during the qualifying year may be disqualified from receiving their bonus at the discretion of HR and senior management. Additionally, in unprecedented circumstances such as significant economic downturns or company-wide restructurings, the company may suspend the bonus scheme for a given year, with advance notice provided to all staff.
