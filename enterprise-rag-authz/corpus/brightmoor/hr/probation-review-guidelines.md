### Purpose

Internal reference code: CNRY-748a756f.

The purpose of this document is to outline the probation review process for new employees at Brightmoor Foods. This guideline ensures consistency and transparency during the evaluation of probationary staff, helping to determine their suitability for permanent employment.

### Probation Period

All new hires at Brightmoor Foods undergo a probationary period of **three months**, commencing on their start date. For example, an employee who starts on **April 1st, 2024**, will have their probation review scheduled for **July 1st, 2024**. Extensions to the probation period may be granted by the department head and HR, but such cases must be documented and justified.

### Review Process

Probation reviews are conducted by the immediate supervisor in collaboration with the HR department. The review will take place in a structured one-on-one meeting and should be completed within **five working days** of the end of the probation period. Key performance indicators (KPIs) will be assessed, including but not limited to:

- Adherence to company policies and procedures
- Quality and timeliness of work
- Team collaboration and communication
- Achievement of set performance targets

During the review meeting, the supervisor will provide a detailed feedback report to the employee, highlighting strengths and areas for improvement. The supervisor will also confirm whether the employee has met the expectations set out in their job description and probation goals.

### Outcomes

At the conclusion of the probation review, three possible outcomes will be communicated to the employee in writing:

1. **Confirmation of Employment**: The employee successfully meets all criteria and is confirmed for permanent employment. This decision must be communicated within **two working days** of the review meeting.
2. **Extension of Probation**: In cases where the employee shows potential but needs more time to meet expectations, probation may be extended for an additional **one month**. This extension requires mutual agreement between the employee, supervisor, and HR.
3. **Termination of Employment**: If the employee does not meet the required standards, termination may be initiated. In such cases, HR must be notified immediately, and the employee will be informed according to the company’s termination procedures.

All probation review documents must be filed in the employee’s HR record by the **10th working day** following the review date.
