- **Career Transition Services**: Including resume workshops and interview preparation sessions.
- **Financial Assistance**: A redundancy payment equivalent to **one month’s salary per year of service**, plus a statutory redundancy payment.
- **Outplacement Counseling**: One-on-one sessions with career coaches to help with job search strategies.

## Communication and Feedback
We will hold regular update meetings every **seven days** to provide progress reports and address any concerns. Feedback from employees and their representatives will be actively sought and considered throughout the consultation period. All communications will be documented and shared via our internal portal and email.

We aim to conduct this process with the utmost care and consideration for all involved. Your cooperation and understanding are crucial as we navigate these changes together.
