Brightmoor Foods Workplace Investigation Procedure

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2025

This document outlines the formal process Brightmoor Foods follows when conducting workplace investigations. It ensures fairness, confidentiality, and compliance with employment laws across all European operations.

## Initiation and Scope

Any concern—be it harassment, safety violations, or ethical breaches—must be reported via the online portal or to an immediate supervisor. Reports are logged with a unique case number (e.g., BF-2025-001) and assigned to a qualified investigator within 3 business days. All cases are reviewed for merit using a standardized checklist to determine if a formal investigation is warranted.

## Investigation Process

Investigations are led by either HR Director Emily Sørensen or an external legal consultant, depending on the case’s complexity. The investigator interviews all involved parties and relevant witnesses, typically completing this stage within 10 working days. All interviews are recorded and transcribed. Evidence collection includes digital communications, CCTV footage where applicable, and any physical documents. A draft report is prepared and reviewed by the Legal Department before finalization.

## Confidentiality and Communication

All participants must maintain strict confidentiality throughout the process. Updates are provided only to directly involved individuals unless a broader policy update is required. If the investigation reveals a policy violation, corrective actions are implemented within 5 business days. These may include mandatory training sessions for entire departments or disciplinary measures for individuals, ranging from written warnings to termination.

## Timeline and Follow-Up

The full investigation, from intake to resolution, must be completed within 30 working days unless exceptional circumstances apply. Post-resolution, Brightmoor Foods conducts a follow-up review 60 days later to ensure that corrective actions are sustained and no further issues arise. All completed cases are archived for a minimum of 7 years per data retention policies.
