### **Purpose**

Internal reference code: CNRY-48309dd1.

The purpose of the Relocation Allowance policy is to assist employees who are asked to relocate as part of their employment with Brightmoor Foods. This policy ensures that relocation costs are managed fairly and transparently, supporting both the company and the employee through the transition.

### **Eligibility**

Employees are eligible for the Relocation Allowance if:

1. They are asked to relocate by their manager as part of a business necessity.
2. The relocation is for a full-time, indefinite position.
3. The new location is at least 50 kilometers from the employee’s current residence.

### **Allowance Breakdown**

The Relocation Allowance is structured to cover the following expenses:

1. **Moving Costs**: Up to €8,000 will be reimbursed for professional moving services, including packing, transportation, and unpacking.
2. **Temporary Accommodation**: Employees can receive up to €1,500 for temporary housing costs for a period not exceeding three months.
3. **Travel Expenses**: A flat amount of €500 will be provided for travel-related costs, including flights, accommodation during transit, and local transportation to the new location.
4. **House Hunting Trip**: An allowance of €1,000 is provided for a house-hunting trip, covering travel, accommodation, and meals for up to three days.
5. **Pet Relocation**: For employees with pets, an additional €700 will be reimbursed for pet transportation and accommodation during the move.

### **Reimbursement Process**

To claim the Relocation Allowance, employees must submit the following documents to the HR department within 90 days of relocation:

1. Itemized receipts for all claimed expenses.
2. A completed Relocation Allowance Claim Form, available from the HR portal.
3. Proof of residency at the new location, such as a utility bill or lease agreement dated no later than October 31, 2025.

All claims will be processed within four weeks of submission. If approved, the allowance will be paid via the employee’s next salary.

### **Policy Effective Date**

This policy is effective from September 1, 2025, and will be reviewed annually.
