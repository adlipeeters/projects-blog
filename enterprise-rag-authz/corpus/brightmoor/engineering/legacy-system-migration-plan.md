## Project Overview

The Legacy System Migration (LSM) initiative aims to transition our core inventory and production management systems from the current COBOL-based platform to a modern, cloud-native solution by **Q3 2024**. This migration is essential to enhance operational efficiency, improve data accuracy, and support scalability as we expand into new European markets. The project is led by **Eva Meyer**, Senior Engineering Manager, and is expected to deliver a **15% reduction in operational costs** and a **20% increase in production throughput** within the first year post-migration.

## Timeline and Milestones

The migration will proceed in three distinct phases:

1. **Phase 1: Assessment and Planning (Jan 1 - Mar 31, 2024)**
    - Conduct a comprehensive audit of existing systems.
    - Define requirements and select the target cloud platform (AWS).
    - Develop a detailed project roadmap and risk mitigation strategies.

2. **Phase 2: Development and Testing (Apr 1 - Jun 30, 2024)**
    - Build the new system modules using **Java Spring Boot** and **PostgreSQL**.
    - Perform rigorous unit, integration, and user acceptance testing (UAT).
    - Develop comprehensive documentation and training materials.

3. **Phase 3: Deployment and Optimization (Jul 1 - Sep 30, 2024)**
    - Execute a phased rollout to minimize disruption.
    - Monitor performance and address any issues promptly.
    - Optimize system configurations for peak efficiency.

## Resource Allocation

The project will require a dedicated team of **12 engineers**, **3 data analysts**, and **2 project managers**. A budget of **€1.2 million** has been allocated, broken down as follows:

- **Software and licensing**: €400,000
- **Cloud infrastructure**: €600,000
- **Consulting and external services**: €200,000

Regular progress reports will be submitted monthly to the **Engineering Steering Committee**.

## Risk Management

Key risks include data loss, system downtime, and resistance to change. Mitigation strategies include:

- Implementing robust backup and recovery procedures.
- Scheduling migrations during low-activity periods.
- Providing extensive training and support for end-users.

By adhering to this plan, Brightmoor Foods will modernize its IT infrastructure, ensuring we remain competitive and agile in the rapidly evolving market landscape.
