**Brightmoor Foods Disaster Recovery Plan**

Internal reference code: CNRY-7f1f8845.

## **Introduction**
This document outlines the disaster recovery procedures for Brightmoor Foods' primary manufacturing facility located in Antwerp, Belgium. The plan is designed to ensure business continuity in the event of natural disasters, cyber-attacks, or other unforeseen incidents. The most recent test of this plan was conducted on **March 15, 2023**, and the next scheduled review is set for **September 15, 2024**.

## **Immediate Response Protocol**
In the event of a disaster, the first 24 hours are critical. The **Disaster Recovery Team (DRT)**, led by **Eva Meyer, Head of Engineering**, will activate the plan. The team includes:
- **Janek Kowalski, IT Recovery Lead**
- **Lina Chen, Facilities and Logistics Coordinator**
- **Marcus Bergman, Communications Officer**

The DRT will assess the situation, secure all personnel, and begin initial recovery steps. Power backup systems, including **two 500 kVA diesel generators**, will be activated to maintain critical operations. All employees must report to the designated emergency assembly point at **Hoogstraat 12, Antwerp**.

## **Data Recovery and System Restoration**
To prevent data loss, Brightmoor Foods employs a robust backup strategy. All critical data is backed up daily to **off-site secure servers in Amsterdam** and **encrypted cloud storage via SecureCloud Systems**. In the event of data loss, Janek Kowalski will lead the restoration effort, aiming to restore all systems within **48 hours**. The primary production control system, **ProControl v3.2**, is designed to be fully operational within this timeframe.

## **Facility and Equipment Recovery**
Should the primary facility become inoperable, Brightmoor Foods has secured a temporary production site at **Industriepark De Hoek, Leuven**. This site can support up to **60% of current production capacity**. Repairs to the primary facility will be coordinated by Lina Chen, with an estimated recovery time of **14 business days** for minor damage and **30 business days** for major structural issues. Insurance claims will be managed by the Finance Department, with a dedicated budget of **€2 million** for disaster recovery expenditures.

## **Communication and Reporting**
Marcus Bergman will be responsible for internal and external communications. All employees will receive updates via the company’s secure internal messaging system, **BrightNet**, and emergency broadcasts. External stakeholders, including customers and suppliers, will be informed through designated press releases and direct communications. Daily situation reports will be submitted to the Executive Board, with the first report due **within 6 hours** of disaster declaration.

This plan will be reviewed annually and updated as necessary to reflect changes in technology, personnel, and risk assessment.
