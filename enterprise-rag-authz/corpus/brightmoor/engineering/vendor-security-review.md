**Vendor Security Review**

Internal reference code: CNRY-d2e36a6d.

**Department: Engineering**
**Date: 2025-03-15**

## Overview

This document outlines the results of the Q1 2025 security assessment conducted on our key vendors. The assessment was performed by the Security and Compliance Team under the supervision of Elena Petrov, Senior Security Analyst. The primary objective was to ensure that all vendors adhere to Brightmoor Foods' security standards and to identify any potential risks that could impact our operational integrity.

## Assessment Results

The assessment covered a total of 12 vendors, including both primary suppliers and sub-contractors. The vendors were evaluated based on several criteria: data security practices, physical security measures, incident response protocols, and compliance with industry standards (e.g., ISO 27001).

### Key Findings

1. **Data Security Practices**: 8 out of 12 vendors demonstrated strong data encryption and access control measures. However, 4 vendors showed weaknesses in their encryption protocols, particularly in the handling of sensitive customer data.

2. **Physical Security Measures**: 9 vendors had robust physical security in place, including biometric access controls and 24/7 surveillance. Three vendors, however, lacked adequate surveillance, increasing the risk of unauthorized access.

3. **Incident Response Protocols**: Only 6 vendors had a clear and tested incident response plan. The remaining 6 vendors either lacked a formal plan or had not conducted recent drills to ensure effectiveness.

4. **Compliance with Industry Standards**: 10 vendors were certified under ISO 27001, while 2 vendors had no relevant certifications, raising concerns about their overall security posture.

## Recommendations

To mitigate the identified risks, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Immediate Remediation**: The 4 vendors with weak encryption protocols must upgrade their systems by **2025-04-30**. A follow-up audit will be conducted to verify compliance.

2. **Enhanced Physical Security**: The 3 vendors lacking adequate surveillance should implement basic security cameras and access controls by **2025-05-15**.

3. **Incident Response Drills**: All vendors without a tested incident response plan are required to develop and conduct at least one drill by **2025-06-30**.

4. **Certification Pursuit**: The 2 uncertified vendors are encouraged to pursue ISO 27001 certification within the next year. Brightmoor Foods will offer financial support for certification costs.

## Conclusion

The Q1 2025 vendor security review has provided valuable insights into the strengths and weaknesses of our vendor ecosystem. By addressing the identified issues promptly, we can ensure the continued security and integrity of our supply chain.
