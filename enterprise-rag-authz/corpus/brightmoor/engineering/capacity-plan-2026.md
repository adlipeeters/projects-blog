## Production Line Upgrades

In order to meet the projected demand growth of 12% for 2026, we will be undertaking significant upgrades to our production lines at the Leipzig and Vienna facilities. The Leipzig facility will receive a new automated packaging line, increasing its daily capacity from 45,000 to 60,000 units. This upgrade, scheduled for completion by **1 March 2026**, will utilize the latest from AutoPack Technologies, reducing manual labor by 20% and increasing overall efficiency by 15%. The Vienna facility will focus on enhancing its baking line, boosting output from 30,000 to 40,000 units per day by **1 May 2026**. This will involve the installation of new conveyor belts and advanced temperature control systems from ThermoFlow GmbH.

## Workforce Expansion

To operate the upgraded facilities, we plan to expand our engineering and operations workforce by **20%**. This includes the hiring of **12 new engineers**, **8 additional maintenance technicians**, and **15 operations supervisors**. Recruitment will begin in **November 2025**, with onboarding scheduled for **December 2025**. Training programs will be conducted in partnership with the Technical University of Dresden, ensuring all new hires are fully prepared by **1 February 2026**.

## Investment and Budget Allocation

The total investment for these upgrades is projected at **€18.5 million**. The budget breakdown is as follows:
- Leipzig Facility Upgrade: **€9.2 million**
- Vienna Facility Upgrade: **€6.8 million**
- Workforce Expansion and Training: **€2.5 million**

Funding will be allocated from the 2025 capital expenditure budget, with additional funds secured through a **€5 million** loan from European Bank. We expect a return on investment within **18 months**, driven by increased production capacity and reduced operational costs.

## Timeline and Key Milestones

The following timeline outlines key milestones for the Capacity Plan 2026:
- **15 October 2025**: Final approval of budget and plans
- **1 November 2025**: Commencement of recruitment
- **1 December 2025**: Start of training programs
- **1 March 2026**: Completion of Leipzig facility upgrade
- **1 May 2026**: Completion of Vienna facility upgrade
- **1 June 2026**: Full operational capacity achieved

We anticipate these upgrades will not only meet our growth targets but also enhance our competitive position in the European packaged food market.
