## Monitoring Overview

Internal reference code: CNRY-f8139f4f.

At Brightmoor Foods, continuous monitoring of our manufacturing systems is essential to ensure optimal performance, minimize downtime, and maintain product quality. Our monitoring framework includes real-time tracking of critical equipment parameters, such as temperature, pressure, and vibration levels. All monitoring activities must comply with the following standards, effective from **January 1, 2024**.

## Key Metrics and Thresholds

1. **Temperature Monitoring**: All pasteurization units must maintain a core temperature between **72°C and 74°C**. Any deviation beyond **71°C or 75°C** should trigger an immediate alert.
   
2. **Pressure Monitoring**: Filling machines should operate within a pressure range of **2.0 to 2.5 bar**. Alerts must be generated if pressure falls below **1.8 bar** or exceeds **2.8 bar**.
   
3. **Vibration Monitoring**: Critical conveyor belts must not exceed a vibration level of **2.5 mm/s**. Alerts are triggered at **3.0 mm/s**.

## Alerting Protocol

All alerts must follow a structured protocol to ensure rapid and effective response:

1. **Level 1 Alerts**: Immediate notification to the **Engineering On-Call Team** via dedicated phone and SMS. These alerts include temperature deviations in pasteurization units and pressure anomalies in filling machines.
   
2. **Level 2 Alerts**: Notification to the **Production Supervisor** and **Quality Assurance Team** via email and internal messaging system. These alerts include vibration thresholds being met on conveyor belts.

3. **Resolution Timeline**: Level 1 alerts must be acknowledged within **15 minutes** and resolved within **2 hours**. Level 2 alerts must be acknowledged within **30 minutes** and resolved within **4 hours**.

## Reporting and Review

Monthly reports must be submitted by the **15th of each month** to the **Engineering Manager, Anna Kovács**. These reports should include a summary of all alerts, resolution times, and any trends observed. Quarterly reviews will be conducted with the **Operations Leadership Team** to assess the effectiveness of the monitoring system and make necessary adjustments.
