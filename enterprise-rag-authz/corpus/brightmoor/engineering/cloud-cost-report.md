# Cloud Cost Report

Internal reference code: CNRY-90adf1f6.

## Overview

This report provides an analysis of Brightmoor Foods' cloud expenditure for Q2 2024, covering the period from April 1, 2024, to June 30, 2024. Our primary cloud service provider is **CloudFlex**, and we utilize **AWS** for certain analytics workloads. The total cloud spend for Q2 2024 was **€1,250,000**, representing a **7%** increase compared to Q1 2024, which totaled **€1,166,000**.

## Departmental Breakdown

The Engineering Department is the largest consumer of cloud resources, accounting for **45%** of the total cloud spend. The breakdown is as follows:

- **Infrastructure as Code (IaC) Tools**: €280,000
  - **Terraform**: €150,000
  - **Ansible**: €130,000

- **Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) Pipelines**: €220,000
  - **Jenkins**: €120,000
  - **GitLab CI**: €100,000

- **Containerization and Orchestration**: €175,000
  - **Docker**: €75,000
  - **Kubernetes (K8s)**: €100,000

## Cost-Saving Initiatives

To address the increased spending, several cost-saving initiatives have been implemented:

- **Reserved Instances**: We have purchased reserved instances for our most frequently used services, resulting in a **15%** reduction in compute costs.
- **Auto-Scaling**: Implemented auto-scaling policies to dynamically adjust resource allocation based on demand, saving an estimated **€45,000** per month.
- **Resource Tagging**: Enforced strict resource tagging policies to improve cost allocation tracking and identification of idle or underutilized resources.

## Recommendations

Moving forward, the following recommendations are proposed to further optimize our cloud expenditure:

- **Conduct Quarterly Reviews**: Perform quarterly reviews of cloud usage to identify trends and opportunities for optimization.
- **Implement Budget Alerts**: Set up budget alerts to notify department heads when spending approaches predefined thresholds.
- **Explore Additional Discounts**: Investigate additional discount programs, such as sustained use discounts or volume pricing, to further reduce costs.

## Conclusion

While the increase in cloud spending is concerning, the implemented cost-saving initiatives have begun to show positive results. Continued vigilance and proactive management will be essential to maintaining a balanced cloud expenditure.
