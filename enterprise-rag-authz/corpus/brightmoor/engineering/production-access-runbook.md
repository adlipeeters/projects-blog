### Access Request Submission

Internal reference code: CNRY-29bd5554.

All production access requests must be submitted via the Access Management System (AMS) at least **five business days** prior to the desired access date. Requests should be initiated by the requesting employee and approved by their direct supervisor. All requests must include the following details:
- Employee ID and name
- Date and time of requested access
- Specific production area (e.g., Line 3 Packaging, Dairy Processing Unit)
- Purpose of access (e.g., maintenance, audit, process improvement)
- Expected duration of access

Approval times vary by department but typically take **48 hours**. Requests submitted less than five days in advance will be automatically rejected.

### Security Clearance and Escort Policy

All employees and external personnel must possess at least a **Level 2 Security Clearance** to access production floors. Level 2 clearance can be obtained by completing the Security Awareness Training (SAT) course, which is available on the Brightmoor Learning Platform. Training completion must be verified before access is granted.

All visitors, including contractors and vendors, must be escorted at all times by an authorized Brightmoor employee wearing a **blue escort badge**. Escort logs must be updated every **15 minutes** during the visit and signed by both the escort and the visitor upon completion.

### Emergency Protocols

In case of an emergency during production access:
1. Activate the nearest emergency alarm.
2. Proceed to the nearest emergency assembly point (e.g., Assembly Point Alpha for Line 1-2, Point Beta for Line 3-4).
3. Account for all personnel using the Emergency Headcount Sheet located at each assembly point.
4. Report any injuries or incidents to Security via extension **3333** or use the emergency phone located at assembly points.

Emergency drills will be conducted quarterly. All employees must participate. Failure to comply will result in disciplinary action.

### Access During Scheduled Downtime

Access during scheduled downtime (e.g., Line 4 maintenance from **2024-06-10 to 2024-06-14**) requires prior coordination with the Production Scheduling team. Access permissions will be granted through AMS with a special "Downtime Access" flag. During downtime, standard operating procedures still apply, but certain safety protocols may be adjusted. Review the specific downtime procedures document posted on the department bulletin board or available on the intranet under **Production/Downtime Procedures**.
