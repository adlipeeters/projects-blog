## Production Uptime Target

Internal reference code: CNRY-c1f17f86.

We aim to maintain a production uptime of **99.5%** per month, measured from January 1, 2024. This translates to no more than **12 hours** of unplanned downtime monthly. Key performance indicators (KPIs) will be monitored daily by the Operations Team, with a bi-weekly review conducted by the Engineering Leadership Committee.

## Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF)

Our target MTBF for all packaging lines is **500 hours**. This metric will be calculated every quarter. Any line falling below this threshold must undergo a root cause analysis within **5 business days**. The Maintenance Team will implement corrective actions, with a follow-up assessment scheduled **10 working days** after implementation.

## Mean Time To Repair (MTTR)

The goal is to achieve an MTTR of **less than 60 minutes** for any critical equipment failure. This includes conveyors, filling machines, and quality control systems. All maintenance staff will undergo quarterly training to ensure adherence to this standard. Reports of any incident exceeding this time frame must be submitted to the Engineering Manager within **24 hours**.

## Energy Consumption Reduction

We are committed to reducing energy consumption by **5%** year-over-year, starting from the baseline of 2023, which was **12,000 MWh**. This initiative aligns with our corporate sustainability goals. The Energy Management Team will conduct monthly audits and implement energy-saving measures. Progress will be reviewed at the end of each quarter, with a comprehensive report due by **April 15, 2024**.
